# Hledger Notes

Hledger Notes creates and manages hledger transactions inside the vault, with inline creation, fuzzy account suggestions from an accounts file, automatic balance calculation, multi-currency support, and k and m shortcuts for amounts. Transactions are imported from and exported to hledger journal files. Entries are organized in daily notes by detecting the date automatically and writing into a dedicated section.
