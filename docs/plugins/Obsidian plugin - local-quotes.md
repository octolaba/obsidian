# Local Quotes

Collects quotes from across the vault and embeds them as rotating quote blocks in other locations. Quotes renew automatically at intervals ranging from seconds to months, custom classes and formats can be applied, and an instant refresh shows updates in real time.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "local-quotes"
    name:   "Local Quotes"
    author: "sundevista"
    repo:   "sundevista/local-quotes"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/local-quotes"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/sundevista/local-quotes"
    description: "Collect your quotes from all over the vault and embed them in different locations and watch them refreshing in real-time."
    about:       "Collect quotes from across your vault and embed rotating quote blocks anywhere in your notes. Renew quotes automatically at intervals from seconds to months, apply custom classes and formats, and refresh instantly to see updates in real time."
```
