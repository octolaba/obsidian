# Eve Apple Tree Thinking System

Renders the vault as a 3D tree of light that can be flown through, with each top-level folder growing its own tree in a forest. Notes are placed as leaves by field and time, linked notes cluster together, and breakthroughs and outputs are marked as flowers and apples. Zooming in reveals titles and descriptions, and zooming out leaves translucent silhouettes.
