# Tracker

Occurrences and numbers are collected from note content, frontmatter, inline Dataview fields and checkboxes into datasets for analysis. The datasets are rendered as calendars, bar and stacked bar charts, pie charts and tables, with streak coloring and thresholds. Queries select by date range or by the existence of a field.
