# Popup Review for Spaced Repetition

Flashcards from the Spaced Repetition plugin are reviewed in a small always-on-top popup that appears at regular intervals and does not steal focus. Answers are revealed and rated again, hard, good or easy in the popup itself, and the ratings are saved through Spaced Repetition's own review pipeline so that scheduling stays consistent.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "sr-popup-review"
    name:   "Popup Review for Spaced Repetition"
    author: "uzusio"
    repo:   "uzusio/obsidian-sr-popup-review"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/sr-popup-review"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/uzusio/obsidian-sr-popup-review"
    description: "Review your Spaced Repetition flashcards in a small always-on-top popup that appears at regular intervals. Companion plugin for the Spaced Repetition plugin. - This plugin has not been manually reviewed by Obsidian staff."
    about:       "Review your Spaced Repetition flashcards without opening Obsidian via an always-on-top, non-stealing popup that appears at regular intervals. Reveal answers and rate cards (Again / Hard / Good / Easy) directly in the popup; ratings are saved through Spaced Repetition’s review pipeline to keep scheduling consistent."

    stats: {

}
```
