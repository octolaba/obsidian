# LaTeX Math

LaTeX Math evaluates the contents of LaTeX blocks with SymPy and writes the computed or simplified expression back into the math block. It solves equations and systems, expands, factors, performs partial-fraction decomposition, and handles units, constants and assumptions on symbols. Expressions can also be converted into SymPy code.
