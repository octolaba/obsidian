# AICHE Voice

Records speech on desktop and mobile from the ribbon icon, a hotkey or the command palette, and inserts the transcription at the cursor with punctuation, grammar cleanup and filler-word removal already applied. It is aimed at quick capture into daily notes, meeting summaries, drafts and fleeting thoughts. AICHE is a paid voice platform with a 7-day free trial; audio is processed for transcription and then deleted, and the notes stay in your vault.
