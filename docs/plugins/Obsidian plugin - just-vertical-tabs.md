# Just Vertical Tabs

Moves the tab bar from the top of the editor pane to a vertical orientation on the left or right side of the app. The right-sidebar toggle can sit in its default position, in the note header or at the bottom of the tab bar, and tab icons are optional. A single command collapses the tab bar to icons or to title initials.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "just-vertical-tabs"
    name:   "Just Vertical Tabs"
    author: "Roman Soriano"
    repo:   "narwhai/just-vertical-tabs"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/just-vertical-tabs"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/narwhai/just-vertical-tabs"
    description: "Moves the tab bar from the top to a vertical orientation on the left or right side of the editor pane. - This plugin has not been manually reviewed by Obsidian staff."
    about:       "Align note tabs vertically on the left or right side of the app. Place the right-sidebar toggle in default, note header, or bottom of the tab bar, show optional tab icons, and collapse the tab bar to icons or title initials with a single command."

    stats: {
        downloads:  206

    }
}
```
