# InkPilot

Manages novel projects with structured modules for characters, worldbuilding, foreshadowing and chapter outlines. Chapters are generated, streamed, expanded or rewritten with AI assistance across several providers, with consistency and AI-flavor checks, a brainstorming chat and token cost tracking. The recorded description frames the split as the AI writing most of the draft while the author supplies the rest.
