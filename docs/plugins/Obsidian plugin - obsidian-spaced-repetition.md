# Spaced Repetition

Flashcards and whole notes are reviewed on a spaced-repetition schedule against the forgetting curve. Cards may be single-line, multi-line, reversed or cloze and may carry images, audio, video and LaTeX, with decks organized through tags or folders. Due items and review statistics are tracked.
