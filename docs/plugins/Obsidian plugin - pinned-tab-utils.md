# Pinned Tab Utils

Pinned Tab Utils moves pinned tabs to the left end of the tab bar, mimicking browser behaviour. New tabs open to the right of the pinned section, additional pinned tabs take the next slot on the right, and a tab that is unpinned is moved into the area to the right of that section.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "pinned-tab-utils"
    name:   "Pinned Tab Utils"
    author: "Roman Soriano"
    repo:   "narwhai/pinned-tab-utils"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/pinned-tab-utils"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/narwhai/pinned-tab-utils"
    description: "Automatically moves pinned tabs to the left side of the tab bar, mimicking browser behaviour. - This plugin has not been manually reviewed by Obsidian staff."
    about:       "Place pinned tabs at the left end of the tab bar and keep new tabs opening to the right of them. Add additional pinned tabs to the next slot on the right and move unpinned tabs to the area to the right of the pinned section."

    stats: {
        downloads:  177
        updated_at: 1777315918000
    }
}
```
