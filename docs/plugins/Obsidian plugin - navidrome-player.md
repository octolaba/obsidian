# Navidrome Player

Docks a player in the sidebar that streams a Navidrome or Subsonic library, showing cover art spinning like a vinyl record or in a square mode. Albums, artists and playlists are browsed and searched, and saved internet radio streams play with now-playing information. Shuffle and a vibes mode build random queues.
