# LexVoice

LexVoice records audio in the background and transcribes speech into segmented Markdown notes for meetings, interviews, lessons and personal voice memos, using segmented or streaming transcription. Transcripts are organised and summarised through optional AI templates, imported audio can be merged, and the recordings are kept alongside the generated notes. Its recorded description and About are bilingual, in Chinese and English.
