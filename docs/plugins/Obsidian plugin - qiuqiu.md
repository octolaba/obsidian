# Qiuqiu

Adds a Qiuqiu widget to the workspace, based on the Sakana Widget. Four characters are available — qiuqiu, taotao, qiuqiu2 and qiuqiu3 — the widget is dragged to swing, and a controller switches characters or toggles auto mode. Custom widgets are enabled through the settings.

    id:     "qiuqiu"
    name:   "Qiuqiu"
