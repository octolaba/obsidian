# Alchemist

Bundles several exporters in one plugin. Notes and their linked assets export as .textbundle or .textpack with links preserved, multi-note recursion and hashing to avoid duplicates; audio is converted and extracted to MP3 or FLAC in bulk through FFmpeg with ID3 tags taken from note metadata; and Dataview queries export to CSV. It also strips UTM and tracking parameters from pasted URLs.
