# Eleven Days

Shows a date in eleven calendar systems at once, the Gregorian one plus ten alternatives such as Hebrew, Islamic, Chinese, Mayan, Discordian and Thelemic. Each calendar card opens the story of its calendar, personal annual holidays can be added, and daily notes are walked day by day even after they have been archived.
