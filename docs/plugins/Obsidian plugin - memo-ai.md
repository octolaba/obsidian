# Memo AI

Extracts knowledge chunks from notes with an AI model and custom templates, preserving Markdown and updating, creating or deleting chunks as the notes change. Reviews are scheduled with the SM2 spaced-repetition algorithm, and items are prioritized by importance and familiarity scores. Expired entries are removed automatically and reviews are AI-guided.
