# Desk Photos

Places sticky floating photos above the editor, fixed either to the screen or to a part of a note. Filters, frames, tapes, pins, text overlays and optional sounds customize each photo, and positions are stored relative to the editor or the note so that they persist across sessions.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "desk-photos"
    name:   "Desk Photos"
    author: "tapeau"
    repo:   "tapeau/Desk-Photos"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/desk-photos"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/tapeau/Desk-Photos"
    description: "Add sticky and floating photos in your editor, fixed to your screen or to some part of your notes. Customize them with frames, tapes, pins, texts, and they can even play a sound! - This plugin has not been manually reviewed by Obsidian staff."
    about:       "Add sticky, floating photos atop the editor and pin them to the screen or to specific parts of a note. Customize visuals with filters, frames, tapes, pins, text overlays and optional sounds; positions save relative to the editor or note and persist across sessions."

    stats: {
        downloads:  46

    }
}
```
