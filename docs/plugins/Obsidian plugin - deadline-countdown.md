# Deadline Countdown

Sets and manages deadlines from the sidebar with live countdown timers, repeat scheduling and visual warnings raised in advance. Deadlines are organized by category and their lists can be collapsed; repeating items roll forward while expired non-repeating ones are shown as passed.

    id:     "deadline-countdown"
