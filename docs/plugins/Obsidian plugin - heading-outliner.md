# Heading Outliner

Heading Outliner moves an entire heading section, its body text and its sub-headings together, up or down while the fold state is preserved. Tab and Shift+Tab indent or unindent a heading line, multi-selection is supported, and folded parents are unfolded automatically when needed. Per-level visual heading indentation is optional, and the recorded About states that it works alongside Outliner.
