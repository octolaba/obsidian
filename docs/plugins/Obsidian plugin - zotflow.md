# ZotFlow

ZotFlow brings a Zotero library into Obsidian and reads and annotates PDFs and EPUBs inline in a theme-matching reader. Items, metadata and annotations are synced bidirectionally with Zotero, and template-driven Markdown source notes are generated. Literature is cited by drag-and-drop, autocomplete or hotkeys.
