# Full Screen Toggle

Toggles a fullscreen view on every platform to enlarge the reading space, which the recorded text presents as most useful on mobile. Fullscreen is entered by double-click or double-tap or through the Full screen command, and a command-only mode avoids accidental triggers.

    id:     "full-screen-cross-platform"
    name:   "Full Screen Toggle"
    author: "donkeypacific"
    repo:   "donkeypacific/obsidian-full-screen-cross-platform-plugin"
