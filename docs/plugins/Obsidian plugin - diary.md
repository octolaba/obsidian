# Diary

Diary turns date-based Markdown notes into a planner with yearly, monthly grid, monthly list, daily timeline and 3-day timeline layouts, plus a compact planner in the sidebar. Single-date and date-range notes are created by filename convention and carry colors, tasks, start and end times, reminders and recurring schedules. Holidays, alternate and custom calendars and read-only overlays from external ICS calendars are supported, and everything stays in portable Markdown files in the vault.
