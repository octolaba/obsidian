# AI Organiser

Summarizes URLs, PDFs, YouTube pages, audio and notes one source or many at a time, researches the web with source scoring and numbered citations, and connects the whole vault through semantic search and chat. Builds slide decks from a plain-language brief and exports editable PowerPoint, Word and PDF, records meetings into structured minutes with action items, turns notes into spoken audio, and syncs Kindle highlights. It runs against 14 cloud providers, Azure AI Foundry or fully local models with your own API key, and shows every change as an inline diff preview first.
