# Apply Patterns

Apply Patterns applies collections of regular-expression find-and-replace rules in succession to a selection or to whole lines, chosen from a searchable menu. Natural-language date and date-range patterns can be defined, and saved rule sets update due dates, completion markers and other text across notes.
