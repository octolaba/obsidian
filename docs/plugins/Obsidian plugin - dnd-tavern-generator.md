# Tavernus

Generates detailed Dungeons & Dragons taverns into the vault, each with a name, size, prices and prestige. Menus of drinks and meals are built from the tavern parameters, and the full description is inserted into the current note.

    id:     "dnd-tavern-generator"
    name:   "Tavernus"
    author: "trikorka"
    repo:   "trikorka/tavernus-plugin"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/dnd-tavern-generator"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/trikorka/tavernus-plugin"
    description: "Generates detailed DnD taverns right into your Obsidian vault. - This plugin has not been manually reviewed by Obsidian staff."
    about:       "Generate detailed, atmospheric Dungeons & Dragons taverns in your vault, including name, size, prices, and prestige. Create customized menus of drinks and meals based on tavern parameters and insert the full description directly into your note."
