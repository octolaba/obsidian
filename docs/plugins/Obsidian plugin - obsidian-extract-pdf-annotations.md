# Extract PDF Annotations

Extracts annotations from PDF files inside or outside the vault, covering highlights, underlines, squiggles, notes and free text. Extracted items are inserted at the cursor or written into per-file annotation notes named after the source, from an open PDF, a clipboard path, or a folder processed in batch. Templates and the annotation types to collect are selectable, and results are sorted by topic.
