# DnD HP Tracker

Tracks hit points for D&D characters with a color-coded bar showing healthy, wounded and critical states. Damage and healing can be applied with resistance or vulnerability factored into the math, temporary HP added, and full HP restored with a long rest, on desktop and mobile.
