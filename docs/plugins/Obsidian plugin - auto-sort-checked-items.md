# Auto Sort Checked Items

Auto Sort Checked Items moves a checklist item to the bottom of its checkbox group when it is checked, so active tasks stay at the top. Nesting is preserved, since a parent moves together with its sub-items and indented items are reordered within their own level, and undo reverses the check and the move in one step.
