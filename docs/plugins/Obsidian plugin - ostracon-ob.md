# Ostracon OB

Ostracon OB connects Obsidian to MarginNote over a local WebSocket, with devices approved on first connection. MarginNote cards, Markdown, HTML and Canvas content are imported into the vault and browsed or filtered in a right-side panel. Notes are sent back to MarginNote, and rendered vault documents are exported as PDFs into a MarginNote study set.
