# Time Tracker and Invoicing

Time Tracker and Invoicing tracks billable time per note or project with a live status-bar timer and keeps the entries locally in the vault. Hours are aggregated by client and project, and markdown invoices are generated into an invoices folder. PDF export and full invoicing are unlocked by a one-time premium license, and no external account is involved.
