# logos-refs

Pastes passages taken from Logos into notes as formatted quote blocks, extracting BibTeX automatically and writing YAML frontmatter metadata compatible with Obsidian properties, Dataview and Bases. Bibliographies are generated in MLA, APA, Chicago or LaTeX form and exported as .bib files. Citations are tracked with block IDs, page ranges are handled and duplicates removed.
