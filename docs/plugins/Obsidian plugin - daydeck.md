# DayDeck

Organizes the day on a section-based dashboard that separates planning sections such as Backlog and Learning from execution sections such as Today and Next. Tasks are captured through a quick capture bar and narrowed with inline tags and filters, and sections can be dragged and resized. Reminders and waiting timers are available per item.
