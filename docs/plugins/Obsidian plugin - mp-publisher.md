# MP Publisher

Previews WeChat articles with custom CSS themes, offering eight built-in styles, a live WYSIWYG preview and loading of local CSS files. Articles publish directly to a WeChat Official Account, uploading images, choosing or uploading a cover, updating drafts and reporting publish progress. Formatted content can also be copied for pasting into the WeChat editor, and LaTeX math is rendered as PNG for compatibility.
