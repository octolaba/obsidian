# Lumen - AI Brain

Lumen turns Markdown notes into a lightweight async research workflow, with a research sidebar grouped by research status. Research idea notes are created from a selection, status is tracked in frontmatter, and deep research runs, either an internal stub or a Python sidecar, record run progress and a final report. Clarifying questions come from Ollama or OpenAI when a model is configured and fall back to deterministic placeholders otherwise.
