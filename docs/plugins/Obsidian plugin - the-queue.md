# The Queue

The Queue shows random notes from the vault one at a time behind a floating button bar that records a reaction and fetches the next note. Notes are turned into habits, flashcards, iterative-reading prompts or to-dos, which is what supports its spaced-repetition, habit-tracking and serendipitous-discovery workflows.
