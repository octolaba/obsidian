# Pinbox

Pinbox captures text and URLs from the mobile share menu and appends them to designated notes in the vault. Notes are pinned so they are quick to share into, and file bookmarks can be included. Global or per-note append templates add timestamps, and pinned paths are kept current when files are moved or renamed.
