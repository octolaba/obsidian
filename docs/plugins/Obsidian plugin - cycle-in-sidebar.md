# Cycle In Sidebar

Cycles through the tabs of the left or the right sidebar with a hotkey. Cycling runs forward or in reverse, and the preferred shortcuts are assigned in Hotkeys.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "cycle-in-sidebar"
    name:   "Cycle In Sidebar"
    author: "houcheng"
    repo:   "houcheng/obsidian-cycle-in-sidebar-plugin"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/cycle-in-sidebar"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/houcheng/obsidian-cycle-in-sidebar-plugin"
    description: "Hotkeys to cycle through tabs in the left or right sidebars."
```
