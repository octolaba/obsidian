# Strapi Exporter AI

Exports notes to Strapi CMS through configurable routes with per-route field mappings, including mappings for several languages. Frontmatter and SEO-oriented content are generated with AI, and single images and galleries are uploaded or updated with their paths managed automatically. Routes and fields are configured through a JSON schema.
