# Notion Video

This plugin embeds Notion-hosted videos into notes through the Notion API. It takes a Notion integration secret and a block ID, requires the source page to be shared with that integration, and then fetches and inserts the block's video content into the note.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "obsidian-notion-video"
    name:   "Notion Video"
    author: "lastknightcoder"
    repo:   "lastknightcoder/obsidian-notion-video"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/obsidian-notion-video"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/lastknightcoder/obsidian-notion-video"
    description: "Embed Notion videos."
    about:       "Embed Notion-hosted videos into Obsidian using the Notion API. Provide your Notion integration secret and the block ID, share the source page with the integration, then fetch and insert the block's video content into your note."

    stats: {

}
```
