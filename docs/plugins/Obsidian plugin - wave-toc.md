# Wave TOC

Places a floating rail beside the active note listing its H1 to H3 headings, with wave-hover ticks and heading bubbles. Hover cards can preview a heading's title, its first paragraph or a local section summary, and the current section is colour-marked. Navigation follows the pointer or waits for a click, and the headings stay in sync in both Editing and Reading views.
