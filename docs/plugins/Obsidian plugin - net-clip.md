# NetClip

NetClip saves web pages into the vault as Markdown together with title, content and metadata such as author, thumbnail, date and reading time. Clips are organised into folders or categories, browsed inside Obsidian, and searched with autocomplete and filters. Clipped content can also be processed with Google's Gemini using custom prompts and variables, with live progress shown.
