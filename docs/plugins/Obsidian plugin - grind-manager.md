# Gamified Tasks

Collects tasks from across the vault and pays coins for completing them through a built-in rewards system. Tasks can be filtered and searched, made recurring or turned into counters, and given a difficulty that scales the coin reward. Rewards can be purchased, and earning and spending history is reviewable.
