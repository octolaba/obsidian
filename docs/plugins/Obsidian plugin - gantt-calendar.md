# Gantt Calendar

Displays tasks in six interchangeable views: a task list, a calendar by year, month, week or day, a Gantt chart and a sidebar timeline. Solar and lunar holidays and the 24 solar terms are shown along with heatmaps and daily task stats, tasks are edited inline, timeline events dragged and dropped, and both the Tasks-plugin and Dataview formats are supported with recurring rules. The recorded text also names syncing with Feishu Task.
