# Verso

Verso schedules reading by taking a book, a target finish date and the days of the week you plan to read. Remaining pages can be redistributed after falling behind, and the day's chunks appear in a compact sidebar. Books are managed from an urgency-sorted dashboard, with a visual bookshelf for completed reads.
