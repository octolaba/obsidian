# Scholar Quest

Scholar Quest applies XP, levels, milestones and tiered avatars that evolve as you level up to academic work. XP is awarded automatically for reading papers, with a keyword emoji progression, for creating atomic notes and for manuscript writing, while offline activity is logged manually in seconds. Achievements, active projects and daily gains are tracked in the sidebar and the status bar.
