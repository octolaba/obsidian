# Clio

Turns a daily note into a compact ritual assembled from small generated sections. It can show a quote drawn from the vault, a word of the day with definitions, examples and recaps, an on-this-day entry over the vault's own notes, the featured Wikipedia article, a reflection prompt, a one-line journal, intentions with streak tracking, and a sky card with the moon phase and sunrise and sunset times.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "clio"
    name:   "Clio"
    author: "Matthew Newton"
    repo:   "ekrizdis367/obsidian-clio"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/clio"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/ekrizdis367/obsidian-clio"
    description: "Manage quotes from your vault, generates a word of the day, this-day-in-history, word-of-the-day recaps, daily featured wikipedia article, on-this-day for your vault, reflection prompts, and more. - This plugin has not been manually reviewed by Obsidian staff."
    about:       "Transform your daily note into a compact ritual. Display a vault-sourced quote, word‑of‑the‑day with definitions and examples, on‑this‑day/Wikipedia featured article, reflection prompt, one‑line journal, intentions with streak tracking, and a sky card showing moon phase and sunrise/sunset."
```
