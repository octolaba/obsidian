# Quick Capture

Sends text and links from an iPhone or Android device to Obsidian on a Mac or Windows machine without Obsidian being installed on the phone. Submissions queue in your own Cloudflare Worker with AES-256-GCM encryption, are appended to daily Markdown files, and sync into Obsidian when the desktop app is running.
