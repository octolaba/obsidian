# Cyclearn

Creates flashcards and organizes study material into decks, tags and templates inside the vault. Cards are reviewed in Obsidian in revision sessions that the plugin states adapt to the reader's pace and learning style, with no external application required.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "cyclearn"
    name:   "Cyclearn"
    author: "noahboos"
    repo:   "noahboos/obsidian-cyclearn"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/cyclearn"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/noahboos/obsidian-cyclearn"
    description: "The all-in-one solution tailored for flashcards user. With no external apps required, everything's made for and used by your vault. - This plugin has not been manually reviewed by Obsidian staff."
    about:       "Create flashcards and organize study material into decks, tags, and templates directly in your vault. Review cards inside Obsidian with personalized revision sessions that adapt to your pace and learning style."
```
