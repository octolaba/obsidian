# Discourse Graph

Applies the Discourse Graph protocol to notes by breaking research into atomic nodes — questions, claims, evidence and hypotheses — and modelling the relations between them. These semantic objects can be created, related and resurfaced to assemble structured arguments and to reuse evidence across notes.
