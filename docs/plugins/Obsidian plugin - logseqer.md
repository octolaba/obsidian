# Logseqer

Brings Logseq-native workflows into Obsidian to improve compatibility between the two applications. Bullet syntax is validated in real time in the status bar, date formats and namespaces are checked across the vault, Logseq favorites are synced to Obsidian bookmarks, and new journal files are auto-formatted.
