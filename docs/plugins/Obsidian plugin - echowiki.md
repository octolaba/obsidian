# EchoWiki

Compiles raw notes and voice transcripts into a structured, interlinked wiki inside the vault using a local LLM agent. It creates the raw and wiki folders itself and converts notes or recordings dropped into them into interconnected wiki pages through a bundled Mastra compiler.

    id:     "echowiki"
    name:   "EchoWiki"
    author: "mohammadmaso"
    repo:   "mohammadmaso/echowiki"
