# Word Sprint

Timed writing sprints run on a pomodoro-style timer, twenty-five minutes by default, with configurable goals. Nudges arrive when typing stops, and the statistics cover sprint length, total and net words, average words per minute and the longest idle stretches. Statistics can be reset daily or in full.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "obsidian-word-sprint"
    name:   "Word Sprint"
    author: "kinabalu"
    repo:   "kinabalu/obsidian-word-sprint"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/obsidian-word-sprint"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/kinabalu/obsidian-word-sprint"
    description: "Word Sprint for your writing projects like NaNoWriMo."
    about:       "Run timed writing sprints with a pomodoro-style timer (default 25 minutes) and configurable goals to keep you focused. Receive nudges when you stop typing and track detailed stats — sprint length, total and net words, average words per minute, longest idle stretches — with options to reset daily or all stats."

    stats: {
        downloads:  22817

    }
}
```
