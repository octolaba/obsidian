# Tabletop Music Player

Plays ambient tabletop RPG music in the background, driven by note front matter, with crossfades and scene-retention loops as notes and panes change. A dual-channel mixer combines music with looping weather layers. Tracks are also triggered from inline click-to-play links, and playback is controlled with global hotkeys and a searchable folder selector.
