# DDL Radar

Tracks submission deadlines for around seventy computer-science conferences across sixteen areas, with live countdowns, alias-aware search and calendar export. Conferences can be starred, abstract-deadline warnings and browser notifications raised, and five-year patterns viewed alongside a timeline or kanban board with shareable filtered URLs. The countdowns also surface in a sidebar panel, in-note deadline boards, frontmatter-linked banners and a status-bar badge.
