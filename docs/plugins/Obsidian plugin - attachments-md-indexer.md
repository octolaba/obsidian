# Attachments MD Indexer

Creates searchable Markdown metadata notes for attachments such as Canvas files, PDFs and images, so their content and outgoing links appear in search and in the graph view. Text is extracted from PDFs and images, updates and deletions are synced automatically, and the indexed content becomes reachable to Markdown-focused plugins and AI chat tools.
