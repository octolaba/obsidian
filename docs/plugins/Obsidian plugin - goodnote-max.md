# GoodNotes Max

Adds handwriting and an infinite canvas to Obsidian in the manner of GoodNotes. Strokes are pressure-aware with anti-jitter smoothing and stroke-precise erasing, and the canvas pans and zooms with multi-page notebook navigation and GPU-friendly incremental rendering.
