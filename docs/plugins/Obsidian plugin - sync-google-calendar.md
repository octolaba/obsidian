# Sync Google Calendar

Synchronizes Google Calendar events into Obsidian and manages them as tasks, with calendar edits synced back into notes. Obsidian tasks are pushed to Google Calendar by adding a start-date marker, and tasks can be listed by date with Dataview. The recorded About text states that recurring tasks are not supported yet.
