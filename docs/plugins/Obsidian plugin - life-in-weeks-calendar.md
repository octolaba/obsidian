# Life in Weeks Calendar

This plugin shows a whole life as a grid of weekly boxes colour-coded for past, present and future, mapped from a birth date and a projected lifespan. Weeks that already have a weekly note are marked, and the plugin integrates with Periodic Notes or Journals as well as running standalone.

    id:     "life-in-weeks-calendar"
    name:   "Life in Weeks Calendar"
    author: "szuc"
