# DnDJay Soundboard

Controls tabletop audio and maps from an Obsidian pane, mixing four Web Audio channels with live meters. A twelve-pad palette holds looping beds and one-shots with a quick-fire channel, and scenes can be prepared and switched with smooth or sudden transitions. Images or videos from the vault can be framed with pan, zoom and rotation, and maps streamed live to a Raspberry Pi HDMI display.
