# Image Converter

Image Converter converts pasted or dropped images to WEBP, JPG or PNG and compresses them by quality, with HEIC and TIF among the handled inputs. Images are resized destructively or non-destructively, dimensions are applied to the image link automatically, and images are annotated, marked up, cropped, rotated, flipped or aligned inline. Renaming uses template variables, batches run across notes or the whole vault, and processing is pure JavaScript and works offline.
