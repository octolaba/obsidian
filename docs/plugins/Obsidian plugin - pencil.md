# Pencil

Pencil is an infinite pan-and-zoom whiteboard for handwriting and drawing with a pen, mouse or finger, with pressure-aware strokes and palm rejection. Strokes can be erased, selected and moved, with undo and redo, and whiteboards are saved as .pencil files inside the vault. The recorded inputs list desktop, iPad and mobile support, including Apple Pencil.
