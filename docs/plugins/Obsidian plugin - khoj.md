# Khoj

An AI assistant that chats with local or online LLMs and queries the vault, the web and attached documents from inside Obsidian. Custom agents can be created with personas and tools, semantic search runs across PDFs, Markdown, Word, Notion and other formats, and repetitive research can be automated. It runs locally or connects to the cloud.
