# Lookout

Lets wide content be surveyed instead of scrolled sideways. Mermaid diagrams are panned and zoomed with the wheel, with Ctrl and the wheel or with buttons, fitted to the frame, or opened full-screen from an unobtrusive toolbar. Wide tables can also be opened full-screen to see their full width, and a table that is being edited is left untouched.

    id:     "lookout"
