# Lightbox

Lightbox opens images and Mermaid diagrams from Reading view in a zoomable overlay with open and close animations. The mouse wheel zooms and dragging pans, zoom-in, zoom-out and reset controls adjust the view, and Escape or a click on the background closes it.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "lightbox"
    name:   "Lightbox"
    author: "jackcipher"
    repo:   "jackcipher/obsidian-lightbox"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/lightbox"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/jackcipher/obsidian-lightbox"
    description: "Click to zoom images and mermaid diagrams with lightbox effect. - This plugin has not been manually reviewed by Obsidian staff."
    about:       "Click images and Mermaid diagrams in Reading view to open a zoomable Lightbox with smooth open/close animations. Use mouse wheel to zoom, drag to pan, and press ESC or click the background to close. Use zoom-in/out and reset controls to adjust the view."
```
