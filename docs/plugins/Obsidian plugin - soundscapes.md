# Soundscapes

A compact status-bar player plays lofi beats, nature sounds and ambient music to support concentration, remembering its volume and optionally starting on launch. Custom soundscapes are built from YouTube playlists or by indexing a local MP3 folder, which is then shown as a library with play, pause, next, previous, seek, shuffle and search.
