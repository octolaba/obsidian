# Wechat Messenger

Captures content from WeChat Official Accounts and from the other sources recorded for it, namely Xiaohongshu, Dedao, Yuanbao and Zhihu, and stores chat records, images and files in the vault. Imports can be automated and the sync rules adjusted so that the notes stay up to date.
