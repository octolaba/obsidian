# Pairwise Glicko Ranking

Ranks and compares cohorts of notes through pairwise comparisons scored with the Glicko rating system, so ratings adjust as further comparisons are made. Cohorts can be drawn from the vault, a folder, tags or Bases, two notes are compared at a time, and rank, rating or wins can be written to frontmatter for sorting and filtering.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "pairwise-glicko-ranking"
    name:   "Pairwise Glicko Ranking"
    author: "Jack Williams"
    repo:   "williamsjack/pairwise-glicko-ranking"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/pairwise-glicko-ranking"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/williamsjack/pairwise-glicko-ranking"
    description: "Easily sort notes by any subjective criteria - Rank and compare cohorts of notes by pairwise comparisons using the Glicko rating system. - This plugin has not been manually reviewed by Obsidian staff."
    about:       "Rank notes through pairwise Glicko comparisons to build dynamic, self-adjusting ratings that evolve as you add comparisons. Select cohorts (vault, folder, tags, or Bases), compare two notes at a time, and optionally write rank, rating, or wins to frontmatter for sorting and filtering."
```
