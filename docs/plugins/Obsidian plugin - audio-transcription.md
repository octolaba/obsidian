# Audio Transcription

Transcribes m4a and mp3 recordings into Markdown notes with speaker identification and automatic language detection for Greek and English. Summaries, key points, action items and follow-ups are extracted by AI, processing runs locally or in the cloud, and recordings longer than two hours are handled.
