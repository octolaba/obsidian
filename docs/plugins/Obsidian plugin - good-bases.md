# GoodBases

Renders a Bases table view styled after Notion databases, with hairline borders, horizontal scrolling and hover-revealed OPEN buttons that open the underlying note. Values appear as colored pills derived from deterministic hashing with pinned overrides, text and number cells can be edited inline, checkboxes toggle in place, and the grouping and view controls of Bases are respected.
