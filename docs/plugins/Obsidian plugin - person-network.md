# Person Network

Person Network draws the people recorded in a vault as an interactive, canvas-rendered relationship map with photos, silhouettes and ghost nodes for people who have no note. People are read from note frontmatter, positioned by role and connected by the contacts they list. The map supports search, filters, dragging and zooming, PNG export, and opening as a Bases view.
