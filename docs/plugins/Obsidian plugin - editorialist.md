# Editorialist

Imports structured editorial feedback - from human editors, beta readers or an LLM of your choice - into a manuscript and reviews the suggestions in context across its scenes. The edit types cover expand, condense, cut, revise, move and memo, guided by a side panel and a contextual toolbar. Contributor profiles and the rest of the configuration are curated in settings.
