# Docli Connect

Synchronizes the vault two ways with a docli.ru workspace on desktop and mobile, so edits made in Obsidian or in docli on the web flow in both directions. Notes and whole books can be published and shared with a team, renames, moves and folders are tracked so links follow the notes, image attachments sync alongside them, and sync can cover the whole vault or selected folders only. Overlapping edits keep both copies rather than overwriting silently, and the project states that all data is hosted in Russia under the personal-data law 152-FZ.
