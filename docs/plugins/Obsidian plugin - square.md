# Square

Habits are tracked through a grid of tappable squares, each writing its entry straight into the corresponding Markdown note in the vault. Counts, text, dates and attachments are recorded, and progress is reviewed in week, month and ranking views. The recorded inputs state that records stay local and that there are no reminders, points or social features.
