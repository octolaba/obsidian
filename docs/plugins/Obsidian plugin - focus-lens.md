# Focus Lens

Highlights the active paragraph or block and dims or blurs the surrounding content for distraction-free reading and writing. The focus either locks to the centre of the viewport or follows the clicked or active block, in both Live Preview and Reading mode. A Zen Mode toggle hides the sidebars.
