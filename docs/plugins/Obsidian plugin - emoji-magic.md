# Emoji Magic

Inserts emoji through a keyword search over a dictionary of 1,812 entries with 199,658 searchable keywords and thesaurus terms. Results are laid out in a keyboard-navigable two-dimensional grid, and what is inserted is the platform-native emoji character rather than an image. The search runs locally and needs no internet connection.
