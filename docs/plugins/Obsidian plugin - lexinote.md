# LexiNote

LexiNote highlights English words judged difficult for the reader's level as they are written, skipping inline and fenced code, URLs and wikilinks. Hovering a highlighted word shows its meaning, and entries can be added to a personal vocabulary library or browsed as a document word list. CET4 and CET6 lists are included and custom dictionaries are supported.
