# Redsmite Sync

Synchronizes Redsmite reports into the vault automatically at regular intervals. Client folder suggestions adapt to the user's filing preferences after two confirmations, and reports are filed without creating duplicates. It runs on Windows, macOS, and Linux, desktop only.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "redsmite-sync"
    name:   "Redsmite Sync"
    author: "redsmite-com"
    repo:   "redsmite-com/redsmite-sync"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/redsmite-sync"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/redsmite-com/redsmite-sync"
    description: "Automatically synchronizes your Redsmite reports to your vault. - This plugin has not been manually reviewed by Obsidian staff."
    about:       "Automatically synchronize Redsmite reports into your Obsidian vault at regular intervals.
```
