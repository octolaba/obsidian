# Athena

Ingests URLs, papers, repositories, screenshots and videos into the vault and has a language model synthesize each into a structured wiki page. Digests follow a fixed shape of key findings, methods, notable quotes and relevance, and bidirectional links are maintained between them. The resulting graph is queried through the bundled Gryphon chat or the verb-driven kb command-line tool.
