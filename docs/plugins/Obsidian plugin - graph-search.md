# Graph Search

Ranks notes by graph link proximity combined with title and body relevance, to surface results related to particular notes or people. Results can be filtered by tags and paths and biased toward one or more near notes. The scoring and falloff are adjustable.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "graph-search"
    name:   "Graph Search"
    author: "scottschroeder"
    repo:   "scottschroeder/obsidian-graph-search"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/graph-search"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/scottschroeder/obsidian-graph-search"
    description: "Search and rank notes by graph link proximity combined with title and body relevance. - This plugin has not been manually reviewed by Obsidian staff."
    about:       "Rank notes by graph proximity while combining title and body relevance to surface results related to specific notes or people. Filter by tags and paths and bias results toward one or more \"near\" notes, with adjustable scoring and falloff to tune relevance."
```
