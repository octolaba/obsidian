# Bookshelf Base

Bookshelf Base renders notes as a visual bookshelf through a custom Bases view, turning each Base group into its own shelf with a header and a count. Cover images come from external URLs, data URIs or vault-relative paths, notes without a cover get a deterministic colored spine, and wikilinks in properties are rendered. Shelf texture, card size, gap and aspect ratio are configurable, notes open by click or modifier-click, and it works on desktop and mobile.

    name:   "Bookshelf Base"

    about:       "Display notes as a visual bookshelf in Bases with real covers on realistic shelves, turning each Base group into its own shelf with headers and counts. Pick cover images from external URLs, data URIs, or vault-relative paths; auto-generate deterministic colored spines for coverless notes and render wikilinks in properties. Choose from multiple shelf textures, adjust card size, gap and aspect ratio, and open notes via click, Ctrl/Cmd+click or right-click; works on desktop and mobile."
