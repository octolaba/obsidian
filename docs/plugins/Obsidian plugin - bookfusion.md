# BookFusion

BookFusion imports highlights, notes, reviews and annotations from PDFs, EPUBs, MOBI, CBZ and CBR files and other ebook formats into the vault. Text and image highlights are synced as atomic items, content can be routed to more than one vault, and exports and frontmatter are customized with Liquid templates.
