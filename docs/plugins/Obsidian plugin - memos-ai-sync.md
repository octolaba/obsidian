# Memos AI Sync

Syncs Memos content into Obsidian manually or automatically, filing notes into year and month folders, saving attachments with correct relative paths and naming files from a content preview and a timestamp. AI summaries, smart tags and weekly rollups are generated through OpenAI, Gemini or a local Ollama model. Duplicates are skipped by memo ID and the original content is preserved.
