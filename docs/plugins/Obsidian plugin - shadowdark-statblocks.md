# Shadowdark Statblocks

Shadowdark Statblocks creates, imports and renders Shadowdark RPG monster stat blocks in notes. Statblocks are parsed from the clipboard or from a selection, which handles messy text pasted out of PDFs, and several monsters can be bulk-imported with smart defaults and duplicate handling. The results are browsed and shown as live inline or hover-rendered statblocks.
