# Audio Sidebar

Adds a persistent audio panel to the right sidebar that keeps playing while notes are navigated and scrolled. Whole folders of audio load at once, covering mp3, wav, ogg, flac, m4a, webm and aac, and tracks are searched and played either singly or overlapping, with master, music and effect volumes, fades, crossfades, looping and a searchable sound-effect picker. Buttons embedded in notes load a folder or trigger a one-shot effect.

    id:     "audio-sidebar"
    name:   "Audio Sidebar"

    about:       "Add a persistent audio player to the right sidebar that keeps playing across note navigation and scrolling. Load entire folders of audio (mp3, wav, ogg, flac, m4a, webm, aac), search and play tracks, choose single-track or overlapping playback, set master/music/SFX volumes, enable fades/crossfades and looping, and open a searchable SFX picker. Embed clickable buttons in notes to load folders or trigger one-shot SFX."
