# Writing Studio

Turns the vault into a writing environment with a project binder, sprint timer, focus and typography modes, session word counts and a daily writing log. Manuscripts export from the binder, and finished pieces publish to WordPress. A left-sidebar launcher tracks active projects, goal progress, the statistics of the day and quick actions.
