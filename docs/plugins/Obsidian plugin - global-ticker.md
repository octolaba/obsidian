# Global Ticker

Adds top and bottom ticker bars showing headline news and stock-market updates drawn from the Currents and Finnhub APIs. Speed, direction, footers and refresh behavior are configurable, and headlines can be filtered by category, domain, country or language. Free registration for both APIs is required to fetch live data.
