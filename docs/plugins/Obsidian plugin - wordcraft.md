# WordCraft

Finds rhymes, synonyms and describing words inside Obsidian, aimed at songwriting and poetry. The modes cover thesaurus and reverse-dictionary lookups, rhymes including near rhymes, and noun to adjective and adjective to noun conversion. Results can be clicked to chain further searches and filtered by syllable stress, and an internet connection is required.
