# Calendar Plus

Calendar Plus is a sidebar calendar with daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly and yearly periodic notes built in, presented by its inputs as an update of Liam Cain's original Calendar plugin. Periodic notes are integrated rather than separate, month, quarter and year labels are clickable, a year navigator is added, and featured images can be shown on the calendar. The inputs also record bug fixes bringing the calendar view and periodic note functionality up to current Obsidian plugin standards.
