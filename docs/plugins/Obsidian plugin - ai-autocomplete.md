# AI Autocomplete

AI Autocomplete shows inline ghost-text suggestions as you type, reading the text before and after the cursor to offer continuations and insight-driven prompts. Completions come from Groq or other OpenAI-compatible endpoints in English and Chinese, and are accepted with Tab or dismissed with Esc.
