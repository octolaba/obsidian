# Immich

Inserts images from a self-hosted Immich album into notes with one click. A shared album is browsed inside Obsidian and one or several images are selected for immediate insertion, and the recorded inputs advise protecting the album share key.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "immich"
    name:   "Immich"
    author: "talal-a"
    repo:   "talal-a/obsidian-immich"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/immich"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/talal-a/obsidian-immich"
    description: "Link your Immich images within your vault."
    about:       "Insert images from a self-hosted Immich album directly into Obsidian notes with one click. Browse a shared album, select single or multiple images for immediate insertion, and protect your album share key."
```
