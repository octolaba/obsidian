# JMdict Popup Dictionary (Japanese-English)

JMdict Popup Dictionary looks up a selected Japanese or English word offline and shows its readings together with English and Japanese definitions in a popup, using JMdict as its data. Lookup works in the editor, in preview and in embedded PDFs, with automatic normalization of hyphenation, ligatures and inflected forms. It works on desktop and mobile.

    id:     "jmdict-popup-dictionary"
    name:   "JMdict Popup Dictionary (Japanese-English)"
    author: "Katsuhiko Hata"
    repo:   "hata-suriiken/obsidian-popup-dictionary"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/jmdict-popup-dictionary"
