# Journalit

Journalit keeps a local-first trading journal inside the vault, storing notes and trades offline. It shows draggable dashboards and a trading heatmap, tracks performance and account targets, offers review templates from daily to yearly, imports CSV files and syncs MetaTrader 4 and 5 over FTP. Network-backed features are optional and are stated to require a Journalit account and a Pro subscription.
