# TikToker

TikToker saves TikTok videos as Markdown notes with embedded playback and extracted metadata. URLs are processed from the clipboard or in batches, notes open automatically, and transcription runs locally on desktop. A sidebar queue reviews saved TikToks with filters, sorting, tags, progress tracking and Dataview.
