# ZoyClip - Note to Short Video

ZoyClip turns a note into a faceless 9:16 vertical short video for TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and Xiaohongshu. A cloud LLM, OpenAI or DeepSeek, rewrites the note into a script and the note text is sent to that service, while the voiceover is generated locally and ffmpeg renders the result with auto images under Ken Burns zoom and pan, animated single-line CJK-aware captions, background music with ducking, and an auto-generated cover. The finished 1080x1920 MP4 and its cover image are embedded back into the note, with no watermark, in English or Chinese.
