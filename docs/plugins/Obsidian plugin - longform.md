# Longform

Helps with writing and editing novels, screenplays and other long projects by organizing multi-scene or single-note projects into ordered manuscripts, managed from a dedicated sidebar. Scene, draft and project word counts are tracked and daily writing goals can be set, scenes are reordered and nested, and finished manuscripts are compiled with workflow-based tools.
