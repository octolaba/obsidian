# Synapse

Generates AI-backed expansions, summaries and recursive topic trees for stub or incomplete notes, and transcribes audio and video into searchable text. Notes can be enriched with tags, internal links and references. The recorded About text states that each proposed change is reviewed or accepted before anything is written.
