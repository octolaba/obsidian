# Star InfoLearn

Notes are turned into learning cards generated with AI assistance and reviewed through four quiz types: flashcard, multiple-choice, cloze and short-answer. Scheduling uses FSRS spaced repetition, reviews can be filtered by note, folder or date, and progress is shown on a study dashboard. The recorded inputs state that study data is kept locally.
