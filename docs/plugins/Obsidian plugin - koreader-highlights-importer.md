# KOReader Highlight Importer

Imports highlights and notes from KOReader by scanning .sdr directories and parsing their Lua metadata into structured Markdown files. Author, title, pages and keywords are captured, and highlights are organized by chapter and page. Duplicates are handled, and entries are rendered through customizable templates with optional reading statistics.
