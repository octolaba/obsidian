# SideCards

SideCards creates cards in the sidebar by typing, then edits them inline and gives them colors, tags, statuses and expiry dates. Cards drag into the editor to insert their content, convert into Markdown notes, and sync changes in both directions. They can also be imported automatically from a chosen folder into Today, Tomorrow, Archived or custom categories.
