# LINE Notes Sync

Saves messages from LINE into the vault as notes, automatically or on demand. According to the recorded inputs, messages are encrypted before transmission and server-stored copies are temporary and unreadable. Notes are organized by date with customizable filename templates, duplicates are prevented, several vaults can be connected, and only text messages are handled.
