# Lovely-Mindmap

Builds mindmaps and knowledge graphs on the Obsidian canvas with node-based creation, connection and zoom-to-fit navigation. Navigation and editing are keyboard-first across touch, navigation and creation views, and nodes are moved with smart-intent placement for faster layout.
