# Heraldry Weaver

Heraldry Weaver generates procedural or hand-built coats of arms for worldbuilders and tabletop campaigns. Shield shapes, fields, tinctures and layered charges are customized with live preview and rule-of-tincture hints. Designs are saved to a searchable library, embedded in codeblocks or inline in tables, and exported as SVG or PNG.
