# Musical Text

Colours sentences by their word count as they are written, so short, medium and long sentences show up as a pattern across the page. Three marking styles are available, highlight, text colour or underline, with contrasting colours chosen automatically for readability.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "musical-text-highlighter"
    name:   "Musical Text"
    author: "tynanpurdy"
    repo:   "tynanpurdy/musical-text"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/musical-text-highlighter"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/tynanpurdy/musical-text"
    description: "Highlight sentences with color based on their length as you write."
    about:       "Visualize the rhythm of your prose by color-coding sentences by word count to reveal short/medium/long patterns across pages. Highlight sentences in real time with three marking styles — highlight, text color, or underline — and automatic contrasting colors for readability."
```
