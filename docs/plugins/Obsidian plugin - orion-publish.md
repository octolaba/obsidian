# Orion Publish

Orion Publish publishes notes to the web through Orion Publish and manages them from inside Obsidian. Published notes are updated or unpublished, their URLs are copied to the clipboard, publications can point at a custom domain, and notes are grouped into feeds that collect related posts.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "orion-publish"
    name:   "Orion Publish"
    author: "seanrcollings"
    repo:   "seanrcollings/orion-publish-plugin"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/orion-publish"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/seanrcollings/orion-publish-plugin"
    description: "Quickly and easily publish your notes to the web with Orion Publish."
    about:       "Publish Obsidian notes to the web and manage them from Obsidian. Update or unpublish notes, copy published URLs to the clipboard, point publications to a custom domain, and group notes into feeds to collect related posts."

}
```
