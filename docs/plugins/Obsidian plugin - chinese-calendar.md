# Chinese Calendar

Chinese Calendar shows a Chinese-style calendar with lunar dates, traditional festivals, holiday adjustments and solar terms, in a month or a year view. Clicking a day, week, month, quarter or year creates the corresponding Periodic Note, and QuickAdd templates and date fields are supported for automated note creation.
