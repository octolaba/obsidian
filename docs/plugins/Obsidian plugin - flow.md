# Flow

Implements key processes of the Getting Things Done method of David Allen inside Obsidian. Tasks, ideas and commitments are captured into an inbox and then processed into projects, actions, reference material or someday and maybe items. Work is organized into spheres with hierarchical projects, a Focus view collects next actions, and Waiting For items are tracked so follow-ups surface.
