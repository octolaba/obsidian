# Timestamp Notes

A video player opens in the sidebar and plays local or web videos beside the notes being written. A hotkey inserts a timestamped link, and selecting a timestamp seeks the video to that exact moment. Playback and seeking are controlled from the sidebar for review.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "obsidian-timestamp-notes"
    name:   "Timestamp Notes"
    author: "juliang22"
    repo:   "juliang22/ObsidianTimestampNotes"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/obsidian-timestamp-notes"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/juliang22/ObsidianTimestampNotes"
    description: "Side-by-side notetaking with videos. Annotate your notes with timestamps to directly control the video and remember where each note comes from."
    about:       "Open a video player in Obsidian's sidebar to play local or web videos alongside your notes. Insert timestamped links with a hotkey and select timestamps to jump to exact moments in the video. Control playback and seek from the sidebar for quick review."

    stats: {
        downloads:  25713
        updated_at: 1658715201000
    }
}
```
