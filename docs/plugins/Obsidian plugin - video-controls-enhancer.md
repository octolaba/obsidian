# VideoControlsEnhancer

VideoControlsEnhancer adds touch-first gesture controls to Obsidian's player for local videos, covering horizontal scrubbing, vertical volume adjustment, configurable double-tap jumps and long-press fast-forwarding. Lightweight on-screen overlays show the current action, and the gestures apply to every local video element in notes, Canvas, previews and fullscreen, on desktop and mobile. Embedded players such as the YouTube one are not affected.
