# Automatic List Management

Automatic List Management reorders lists and checklists as they are edited. Numbered lists are renumbered automatically, hierarchical checkbox blocks are kept together, and checked items are sorted to the top or the bottom. Pastes, drags and deep nesting are handled as well.
