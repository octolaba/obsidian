# VaultLlama

Chats with locally installed Ollama models from a side panel in the vault. Installed models are selected in the panel, responses stream with a stop button, sessions can be cleared, and the latest answer copied or inserted at the cursor of the active note. The recorded About states that the panel is desktop-only.
