# TOC Reader

TOC Reader renders a Typora-style TOC line as a clickable, nested table of contents in both reading view and Live Preview, reverting to the editable source when the cursor sits on that line. Entries jump to their heading by line position, duplicate headings and non-contiguous levels are handled with relative indentation, and the list updates as headings change.
