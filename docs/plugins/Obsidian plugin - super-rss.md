# Super RSS

RSS feeds are fetched and articles saved as Markdown notes with configurable filenames, frontmatter properties and content templates, turning feeds, blogs, newsletters and YouTube channels into a reading archive inside the vault. Feeds are grouped into folders with their own update intervals, imports are deduplicated, old or read articles are cleaned up, images are downloaded or linked, and items are marked read through a URI handler. YouTube feeds are handled specifically, detecting channel feeds, saving video duration, tagging or skipping shorts and cleaning up live stream entries.
