# Accounting Journal and Ledger

Accounting Journal and Ledger records simple double-entry journal entries in the modern or classical Spanish libro diario formats together with T-account ledgers. Entries are saved as editable Markdown, and accounts are recognized manually or from a chart file. Journal and ledger blocks convert to HTML in the active note, with a minimal interface aimed at teaching and example creation.
