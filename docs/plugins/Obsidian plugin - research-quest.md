# Research Quest

Research Quest generates research questions from the open note, using OpenAI's GPT models for both question generation and analysis. It then tracks which of those questions the writing answers and maintains lists of active and completed questions.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "research-quest"
    name:   "Research Quest"
    author: "narthur"
    repo:   "narthur/research-quest"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/research-quest"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/narthur/research-quest"
    description: "Use AI to generate and track research questions based on your notes."
    about:       "Generate relevant research questions from the open note using OpenAI's GPT models for question generation and analysis. Track which questions your writing answers and manage active and completed question lists."
```
