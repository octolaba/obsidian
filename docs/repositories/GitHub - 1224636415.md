# PhilemonChiro/obsidian-notekeeper

Notekeeper presents the vault's notes as a masonry wall of cards sized to their content, with quick capture from a centred input, inline editing, and a preview modal that renders the note and inherits its colour. Cards can be pinned, coloured from a twelve-colour palette, archived, dragged to reorder, or acted on in bulk after a multi-select, and a context menu offers rename, copy link, copy embed, open in a new pane and delete. Search combines free text with tag, pinned, archived, colour and path filters, sorting follows modification, creation or title with pinned cards first, and three densities including a list view are recorded alongside lazy infinite scrolling for large vaults.
