# Moyf/yearly-glance

Yearly Glance draws a year-at-a-glance calendar of annual events in Obsidian, showing every month and day in one view. It distinguishes holidays, birthdays and fully customizable events, tracks birthdays with attributes such as age and zodiac sign, and supports the traditional Chinese lunar calendar alongside English, simplified Chinese and traditional Chinese interfaces. Layouts, display styles, colours and emojis are configurable, events can be filtered by type with empty dates hidden, and clicking an event opens its details for editing. Events may come from the plugin's own data or from notes carrying frontmatter properties, the latter integrating with Bases for filtering.
