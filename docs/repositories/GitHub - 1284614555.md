# flex-moto/easy-obookshelf

ISBN Bulk Import Bookshelf Builder builds a bookshelf of notes in an Obsidian vault from book metadata fetched by ISBN from the National Diet Library of Japan, Google Books and Open Library. Photographs of back covers in a chosen directory are scanned for ISBN barcodes, with OCR fallback for printed ISBN-10 and ISBN-13 text on macOS, and exported as a CSV that a second action turns into book notes in bulk. Cover images are downloaded, converted to WebP and cached locally, can be replaced by drag and drop, clipboard paste or a file picker, and can be refreshed at higher resolution with low-resolution results rejected and providers tried in turn. Bookshelf views for want to read, reading and finished are provided through Bases, book notes can also be entered and edited by hand, and a bulk metadata refresh preserves reading status, progress, ratings and notes.
