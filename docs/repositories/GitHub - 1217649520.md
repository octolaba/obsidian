# AlucPro/obsidian-lexinote

Obsidian plugin that highlights difficult English words in notes and builds vocabulary while writing. Words above a configurable difficulty level, from 1 to 30, are highlighted in the editor with a background or a straight or wavy underline, hover cards show local meanings and dictionary metadata, and a per-document list summarizes the difficult words in the active note. Words can be saved to a personal vocabulary library that supports search, sorting, known-word markers, deletion and export as its own JSON or as Anki-compatible TSV. Built-in CET4 and CET6 dictionaries derived from ECDICT ship with the plugin, and further dictionaries can be imported from JSON, CSV or TXT files and managed in a table.
