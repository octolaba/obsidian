# sagersmith8/obsidian-journal-utils

The README describes a mobile-only Obsidian plugin for inserting people, group and location links quickly while journaling, built for the author's mind-palace vault workflow. Its commands offer pickers covering people, groups, ghosts and locations, creating a new entry, and converting a person into a group, plus a one-time migration that moves flat notes and merges sub-notes into a per-person folder. Insertions maintain deduplicated, append-only people and locations frontmatter lists, expanding a group into its members rather than recording the group name, and unresolved links are surfaced as ghost mentions to ignore, reclassify or turn into profiles.
