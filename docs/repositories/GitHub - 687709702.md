# ironsigma/obsidian-text-conversions

Text Conversion applies a range of conversions to the text selected in an Obsidian note. The README lists upper, lower, word and sentence case, ROT13, Base64 encoding and decoding, URL encoding and decoding, and reversal of the text or of its word order. It also lists whitespace and character operations, among them removing all spaces, removing repeated spaces, swapping spaces for underscores or dashes and back, stripping non-alphanumeric characters, spacing out the letters of a title, turning words into hash tags, and converting between a bulleted list and a comma-separated line.
