# janisringli/bible-sidecar-obsidian-plugin

Bible Sidecar adds a panel to Obsidian's sidebar for reading the Bible and copying verses into notes. The panel opens from a Bible icon, lists the books, opens a chapter's verses on a click, copies an individual verse to the clipboard when it is clicked, and moves between chapters with navigation buttons. Its single setting selects the Bible version.
