# Andre482/O-tie

O-Tie builds and edits risk bowtie diagrams, the visual method that relates a hazard, the threats that can cause a top event, the consequences that may follow, and the barriers that prevent or reduce harm. Diagrams are stored as their own file type in the vault and saved automatically while editing, on a canvas with panning and zooming, undo and redo, an image export, and an inspector for labels, notes and deletion. Escalation factors, escalation barriers and per-barrier analysis stacks recording type, effectiveness, criticality and custom rows are recorded as supported, as are chained diagrams with barriers between two top events.
