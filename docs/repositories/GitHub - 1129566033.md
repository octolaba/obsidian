# AICHE-app/obsidian-aiche-voice

Voice-to-text plugin for Obsidian in which recording is started from the ribbon icon, a hotkey, a command or the status bar, and speech comes back as text with grammar and punctuation applied rather than as raw transcription. Settings control whether text is inserted at the cursor or at the end of the note, where voice notes are stored, whether the audio is attached, and whether notes are filed into category folders. The README states audio is sent to AICHE servers and deleted immediately after transcription with zero retention, offline recordings are encrypted locally with AES-GCM until upload, and the service is a paid subscription with a seven-day trial.
