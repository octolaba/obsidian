# yoriGo77/obsidian-yori-dashboard

Gathers daily events, data logs, tasks, check-ins, moments, monthly plans and quick links onto a single dashboard page that can be read and edited in place. A month mini-calendar sets the focused date beside an events column, whose More button opens a weekly view supporting archiving, and the data log records daily metrics such as weight, words written, sleep or expenses and archives its monthly history as a note. Tasks are grouped into category boxes with a full task view behind them, check-ins are coloured habit buttons with a monthly streak, moments capture timestamped notes in 12- or 24-hour form, and the monthly planner browses the whole year. Quick links are added by hovering the empty area at the bottom of either column and jump to a chosen note.
