# twrusstw/penny-wallet

PennyWallet tracks personal finances in Obsidian, storing expenses, income, transfers, credit card payments and refunds as plain Markdown tables in the vault. Its README describes a finance overview with monthly income and expense summaries, a six-month chart and category pie charts, a transactions view with type and wallet filters, category and account dropdowns, date ranges, keyword search and sticky subtotals, and an assets view with account balances, net asset trend, savings rate and allocation over three, six or twelve months. It records cash, bank and credit card account types with debt tracking, custom categories, a touch-optimized entry form, transaction entry through iOS Shortcuts by URI, and English and Traditional Chinese interfaces.
