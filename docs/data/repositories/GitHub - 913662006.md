# Lemon695/obsidian-theme-ouroboros

Ouroboros is a clean, warm and focused Obsidian theme, inspired by the calm structure of Things and the warmth of Flexoki and aimed at long-running knowledge work such as reading, writing, research, planning and plugin-heavy vault maintenance. It keeps the accent color as a guide for state, focus and hierarchy while leaving the content surface quiet, and it offers warm light and dark palettes plus Style Settings controls for accent presets, UI density, heading style, link decoration, CJK typography, reduced motion and reading modes. Further listed coverage includes focus and keyboard modes, semantic workflow callouts for decisions, risks, principles, insights and iteration cycles, styling for Bases, Properties, Canvas, Search and Outline, research and longform reading modes, print and PDF export support, and mobile-aware adjustments.
