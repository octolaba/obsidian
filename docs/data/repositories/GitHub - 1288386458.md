# mrrepac/russian-rhymes

Russian Rhymes is an offline rhyming dictionary for Russian inside Obsidian, aimed at songwriters, poets and rappers. It groups words by a phonetic rhyme key rather than by spelling — normalising the stress tail for vowel reduction, final devoicing and consonant clusters — and ranks them in exact, near, slant and assonance tiers plus alliteration, following the cursor so the panel shows rhymes for the last word of the line being typed. Every word carries a stress mark that can be moved by clicking a vowel, with manual choices remembered, and further tabs give definitions, usage examples, etymology and word forms from Russian Wiktionary along with synonyms, antonyms, hypernyms, related words, set phrases and anagrams. Rhymes can be filtered by meaning, mood or semantic category, and copied or inserted words collect into a stash that can be dropped into the note.
