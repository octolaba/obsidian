# Minomaster/ObsMat

Brings WordMat-style shortcuts to writing mathematics in Obsidian. Alt+M inserts a display math block and puts the cursor inside it, and pressing Space inside a math field expands the shortcut just typed: a slash becomes a fraction, a caret and an underscore become superscripts and subscripts, an asterisk becomes a centered dot, and shortcuts cover common Greek letters, square roots, integrals, sums, vectors and hats. It requires Obsidian 1.5.0 or newer, works with desktop and mobile keyboards, and is deliberately limited to input shortcuts rather than a full LaTeX or equation editor.
