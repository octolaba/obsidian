# jordanlong121/singlefilesectioncards

Renders the sections of one note as a wall of cards, one card per heading at a configurable level, with deeper headings nested inside their parent card. Cards are edited in place, checkboxes ticked, tasks and paragraphs dragged between cards, and sections pinned or appended to without opening the editor; every write touches only that section's lines and re-locates it by content first, so a stale card refuses rather than overwriting the wrong ones. Layouts include stacked columns, masonry and aligned grids, plus a freeform canvas where cards snap to a dot grid and their placements are stored in the plugin's data rather than in the note. Sort order and the chosen view are remembered per note.
