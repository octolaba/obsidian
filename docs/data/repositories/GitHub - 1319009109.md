# PleiadesM/TufteObsidian

Tufte for Obsidian adapts Tufte CSS to the app, carrying ET Book type, a marginalia gutter, restrained rules and a single accent red into notes, chrome and the graph, alongside a full Chinese typographic system for body text, italic contexts and data labels. Prose tables and Bases database views share one specimen-table register with a single hairline under the head and no vertical rules, and the Bases cards view is rendered as a bookshelf gallery. A banner callout opens a note with a full-bleed plate, tags and highlights are colored text rather than pills, and sidenotes and three figure display modes place material in the margin. Both light and dark modes are covered, faces and weights are selectable from the theme's typeface settings, and contrast is documented in the stylesheet.
