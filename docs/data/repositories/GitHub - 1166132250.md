# juanchiparra/obsidian-focus-lens

Focus Lens is an Obsidian plugin for distraction-free reading and writing that highlights the active paragraph or block and fades or blurs the rest of the document. It follows the scroll position or the cursor and works in Live Preview and Reading mode across text, tables, callouts and other widgets, with two modes: locking the focus to the viewport centre, or following the paragraph clicked or navigated to. A separate Zen mode hides sidebars and ribbons entirely, and ribbon icons and commands toggle the overlay and Zen mode and step focus to the next or previous block. Settings cover the master switch, the height of the focused band in viewport units, the intensity of the effect, and whether unfocused text is blurred or dimmed.
