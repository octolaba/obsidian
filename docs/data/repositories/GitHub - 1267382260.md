# groundfic/image-peek

Image Peek gives Obsidian a Quick Look-style image preview: an image opens by zooming out of its thumbnail with a FLIP animation and closes back into the same place. It opens on a double-click on desktop, a tap in notes or a double tap on Canvas on mobile, or the space bar while hovering an image, and closes with space, Escape, a click on the backdrop, or a downward swipe on mobile that drags the image with the finger. Inside the preview the arrow keys step through the other images on the page, the scroll wheel or a pinch zooms around the pointer, dragging pans once zoomed, a double-click toggles between fit and double size, and zoom never goes below the fitted size. On desktop the window can be moved by its title bar and resized from any edge, and action buttons share the image, copy it, open it in the default application or reveal it in the file manager.
