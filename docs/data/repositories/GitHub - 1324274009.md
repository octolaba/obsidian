# StashwiseAI/stashwise-obsidian

Stashwise syncs a Stashwise account's saved library and generated wiki into an Obsidian vault as real notes, placing one note per saved item under a Saves folder and one note per wiki topic, linked to related topics, under Topics. The account is connected through a browser or app approval flow started from the settings tab, and syncing runs every fifteen minutes or on demand from a Sync now command. A sidebar panel and command-palette entries search saves and wiki topics together and insert a result at the cursor as a link or as a quote callout, or open it at its source. It installs from Obsidian's community directory and works on desktop and mobile, where items shared to Stashwise from other apps appear in the vault after a sync.
