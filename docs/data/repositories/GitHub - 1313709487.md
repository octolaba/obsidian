# A-K-6/obsidian-finance

A local-first personal finance manager holding cash, bank and credit-card accounts, with expenses, income, refunds, transfers and card payments recorded in integer minor units and always tied to one explicit currency. Typed expense and income categories support one level of subcategories and feed monthly budgets that are separated by currency, where a root-category budget also counts its immediate children. Scheduled bills, subscriptions and recurring income use flexible intervals and manual confirmation, and credit-card accounts add statement and due days, statement balance, minimum payment and a utilization figure computed from the amount owed. A right-sidebar dashboard shows balances and recent transactions while a Planning tab groups budgets, schedules, cards and upcoming items; Gregorian and Persian calendars are both supported and nothing is posted or paid automatically.
