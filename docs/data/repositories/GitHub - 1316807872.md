# seekseep/focus-safari

Focus Safari adds a side panel that accumulates focus time while the Obsidian window is in the foreground, one second per second, with large jumps such as a wake from sleep clamped to two seconds per tick. As the cumulative total crosses a threshold, an animal arrives in the panel and a notice is shown; six animals are free and ten rarer ones require a paid activation code. Codes are Ed25519-signed and verified offline against a public key embedded in the plugin, so nothing is sent anywhere and no telemetry is collected. The panel opens from a ribbon icon or a command, time keeps accumulating whether or not it is open, and settings paste or remove a code and reset the safari.
