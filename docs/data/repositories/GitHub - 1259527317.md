# rapphcd/daily-tracker

Daily Tracker records habits and daily statistics in an Obsidian sidebar view. The React-based right sidebar pane logs up to five custom habits, work and sleep duration from 0 to 24 hours in 30-minute intervals, and a text summary of the day of up to 400 characters, with a monthly calendar marking days that have logs and loading or initializing an entry on click or double-click. A statistics modal shows monthly averages, 7-day bar charts of sleep versus work time, a radar chart of habit consistency for three or more habits, and pie charts breaking down the last four days' time allocation. The README states the view switches and initializes a new log entry automatically at midnight.
