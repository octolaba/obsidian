# Heptazero/obsidian-lexis

The repository records no description; its README, written in English with a Chinese summary, presents Lexis as a personal dictionary for Obsidian that turns a folder of notes into a lexicon of words, terms and names. Entries are highlighted wherever the reader reads — in Obsidian, its PDF viewer, and through a companion extension in the browser — with hover definitions, FSRS spaced-repetition review, automatically collected occurrences and a review heatmap. The README frames the design around noticing and re-encounter rather than study sessions, and cites the extended-mind thesis and second-language research as its stated rationale. It also notes that the plugin has no build step.
