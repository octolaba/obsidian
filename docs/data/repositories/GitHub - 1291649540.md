# DevilSurvivor2/character-companion

Character Companion animates configurable sprites in a side panel and along the bottom edge of the Obsidian window, with walking, resting, speech, click reactions, quotes, and roleplay prompts. Characters can use individual images, image lists, folders, or emoji, while locally generated feed sources provide streams, mail, blogs, news, shows, and oracle-style commentary without AI or Internet access; the feed is desktop-only.
