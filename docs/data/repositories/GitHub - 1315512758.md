# zcallan/callander-obsidian

Callander keeps notes about friends and shared plans and surfaces them on a dashboard when they become useful. The dashboard counts down birthdays for the next thirty days and flags whether a gift idea is recorded, holds recently missed ones for a fortnight with a tick-off button, and lists upcoming events, trips being planned and a wishlist of loose Somedays. A page per friend records a name, a birthday and the day they were met at whatever precision is known, files ideas by kind such as gifts, conversations, things to do and places to go, and merges them into one timeline. Plans carry a timeline for ideas, travel and accommodation, a what-to-bring checklist and a cost breakdown, and the dashboard is read from the notes themselves.
