# Change-Wyc/mermaid-flow-zoom

Mermaid Flow Zoom adds zooming, panning, resizing and fullscreen viewing to Mermaid diagrams rendered in Reading view and Live Preview. The wheel zooms around the pointer, dragging pans, and horizontal panning comes from trackpad scrolling, Shift with the wheel, or the wheel near the left or right edge; on mobile one finger pans and two fingers pinch to zoom. A toolbar offers zoom in, zoom out, reset and fullscreen, where reset and the first render fit the whole SVG proportionally and centre it in the container, and on desktop the container itself can be resized from its lower-right corner.
