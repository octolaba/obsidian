# kumoonet/obsidian-family-bank

A fixed-deposit ledger for children's savings, with one account per child and two products, a monthly deposit at 0.5% per month and a yearly deposit at 7% per year. Interest accrues once a full period has passed and is settled either as cash or by rolling it into a new deposit, with a settlement history that can be reverted, while deposits can be reduced proportionally or individually with a recorded reason. A dashboard opened from a ribbon icon shows total assets, a chart of assets over time, the split between monthly and yearly deposits, a comparison between children, and progress toward each saving goal. Accounts can be added or removed at any time, and all data is written to a single JSON file in the vault at a configurable path.
