# peerdraft/obsidian-plugin

Peerdraft shares Obsidian documents and folders for collaborative editing, either as end-to-end encrypted ad-hoc sessions or as long-term shares that support asynchronous and offline editing, and it also offers a web editor beside the Obsidian client. Sharing produces a secret, unguessable link, and collaborators need no Peerdraft account to join from their own vaults or a browser; entire folders stay in sync, with documents created by anyone in a shared folder appearing for everyone. The README states that fleeting sessions pass no content through Peerdraft servers and end when the creator leaves or closes the document, and that long-term sharing needs a paid account starting at 30 USD per year, with a 14-day trial of all features.
