# garetneda-gif/obsidian-realtime-transcription

Real-time speech-to-text inside Obsidian with a choice of two engines: SenseVoice-Small running locally with Silero VAD and sherpa-onnx so audio stays on the machine, or a hosted cloud service that needs no local Python or model. Recognition covers Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean and Cantonese, can be restricted to a single language or a mixed Chinese-English mode, and offers a steadier or a faster preview mode, hot words for proper nouns, and correction rules that rewrite mistaken words before display and saving. Non-Chinese content can be translated automatically through an OpenAI-compatible API, and transcripts can be polished into written style on demand or summarised automatically at a word-count threshold, with accumulated summaries condensed again into an overall summary.
