# baileylu121/obsidian-compline

This repository holds Compline, an Obsidian port of the Compline colour palette, designed for focused, distraction-free writing with muted colours chosen to stay easy on the eyes during extended use. It ships two variants sharing one palette: Compline, a deep warm dark theme, and Lauds, a soft warm light theme, with each colour's role, from backgrounds and primary and secondary text to links, strings, warnings, errors, info and tags, tabulated in the README. It installs from Obsidian's community themes, by copying the theme files into the vault, or through a Nix flake input, and the palette is credited to Joshua Blais.
