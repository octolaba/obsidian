# purplelawnchairs/Timeline-View

Timeline adds a custom Bases view that plots the notes matching a Base's filters along a horizontal, zoomable time axis, drawing single dates as dots and date ranges as bars. Dates come from frontmatter properties, by default date, date_start and date_end, and may be full ISO dates, a year and month, or a bare year; BC years are accepted with BC, BCE, AD or CE before or after the year, or as ISO negative astronomical years. View options set the date, range and category properties, optional labels from any property including the file name, the axis interval in years and whether the axis is shown. Colours are taken from Obsidian's accent palette in first-seen category order so a category keeps its colour as filters change, and notes with no category render in grey.
