# diegoeis/obsidianotion

Obsidianotion gives Obsidian a look and feel similar to Notion, aimed in part at people migrating from Notion who want a familiar context while learning the new interface. It supports a wide number of alternate checkbox types, covering incomplete, done, cancelled, forwarded and scheduling states plus extras such as question, star, quote, location, bookmark, idea and key, using the same styles as the Things and Minimal themes. With the Style Settings plugin pinned tabs can be made smaller, and the theme also offers colourful tags, a choice of three fonts and a wide colour variation for callouts.
