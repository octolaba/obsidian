# kaventro/notion-sync

Notion Vault Sync mirrors an entire vault to Notion, recreating the folder tree as nested pages and converting headings, emphasis, code, tables, quotes, lists and dividers into native Notion blocks. Frontmatter becomes page properties, wiki links resolve to the synced pages, and attachments can be uploaded through a configurable endpoint, while a pull brings pages back and downloads their images locally. Sync runs on command, incrementally, on save or on a schedule, and pages newly created in Notion can be pulled on their own. A sidebar panel, a pending-changes list with per-file push buttons, a history modal with rollback for pulled files, a log modal and a connection test are recorded.
