# DrSigmaLi/MediaLog

MediaLog shows personal records of movies, TV shows, anime, games and books on one card wall inside Obsidian, with tabs for media, games and books and filters for year, category or platform, status and sort order. Movie, TV and anime entries come from year files in a MovieLog-compatible folder, can be created through a TMDB search from the card wall, and update their status, watched date, rating, platform and review when a card is clicked. Game and book libraries are read from gamelog.json and booklog.json, the first including playtime fields and the second accepting Open Library or Google Books style metadata. Data stays in the vault as Markdown or JSON at configurable paths, cover images may be local or remote, and the plugin is a beta fork of MovieLog with no cloud service.
