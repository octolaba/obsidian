# solorpgstudio/obsidian-rpg-detect-dice-roll

Detects dice formulas in reading view and turns them into clickable roll controls, keeping recent results with timestamps in a roll history side panel. It handles forms such as `d20`, `1d20 + 4`, `2d6 - 1` and keep- or drop-highest and lowest variants like `4d6kh3` and `4d6dl1`, plus advantage and disadvantage rolls, and offers quick buttons for the common dice as well as custom formula buttons and custom narrative dice that pick from weighted text outcomes. Roll notices showing the total, the formula and the individual rolls are optional, display, color, toast placement, history and control visibility are configurable, and the plugin works offline with its settings stored in the vault.
