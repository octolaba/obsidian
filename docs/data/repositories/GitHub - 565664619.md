# 7368697661/Origami

Origami is a theme for Obsidian that its author began in October 2022 as a first attempt at theming and that won Obsidian October that year. It deliberately stays close to vanilla Obsidian rather than deviating from it, taking inspiration from printed works and the creases of folded paper, with dashed borders standing in for the folds. The default title font is Fraunces and the default body and interface font is iA Writer, and further title and body fonts can be selected per note by adding values such as aspekta, chillax or lexend to a note's cssclasses property.
