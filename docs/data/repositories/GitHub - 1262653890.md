# ritalee2333/obsidian-quick-date-picker

Quick Date Picker inserts dates in Obsidian by typing a trigger character, by default the at sign, which pops up a mini calendar that can be driven with the mouse or the keyboard. Relative shortcuts such as three days ahead, a week back or two months on insert a date without opening the calendar at all. Several output formats are built in — standard, wiki link, Chinese and compact among them — with configurable prefix and suffix and buttons at the bottom of the popup for switching format without going to settings. The picker also works in note titles, and its settings panel and popup follow Obsidian's interface language in Chinese or English.
