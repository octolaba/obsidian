# bighead2425/obsidian-chinese-emoji-picker

Chinese Emoji Picker searches emoji by Chinese keyword rather than English shortcode: typing a doubled full-width colon followed by a hanzi word opens a picker panel below the cursor. The panel has nine tabs, recently used plus eight categories, over a seven-column grid; arrow keys move the selection, Enter or a click inserts, and the whole trigger text is replaced. What it inserts is a plain Unicode character rendered in the system font, so unsupported glyphs show as boxes in the panel exactly as they will after insertion, and notes stay portable. It runs entirely locally with no network requests, and its keyword mapping and dataset of over 1900 emoji are adapted from SiYuan Notes, which in turn draws on the Unicode CLDR data.
