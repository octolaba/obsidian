# contrapasso3/Reshi

Reshi is an Obsidian theme named after the protagonist of Patrick Rothfuss's The Name of the Wind and addressed to storytellers, teachers, students and lifetime learners. Its palette combines warm greens and yellow-golds with a full spectrum of grays, and it offers both light and dark modes. Custom checkboxes drawn with Lucide icons cover states such as in progress, canceled, scheduled, question, idea and quote, and the theme adds two custom textboxes per mode, a sparkle callout block, colorized bold and italics, and links with their underlines removed. CSS modifications are included for the Dataview, Obsidian Columns and Multi-Column Markdown plugins.
