# fyaic/Mermaid-Export-Fit-Plugin

Mermaid Export Fit keeps a Mermaid diagram wider than the reading pane from being clipped when a note is exported to PDF or HTML. Rather than shrinking every diagram in normal reading, it adds horizontal scrolling for wide ones and fits diagrams to the page width during export, applying itself automatically in reading mode, live preview and export once the vault has been reloaded. Two commands refresh all diagrams in the workspace or force them to fit their container, and the settings page opens in English with a button for switching between English and Chinese.
