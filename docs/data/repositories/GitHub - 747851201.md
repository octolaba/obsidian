# lychileng/Obsidian-Theme-Pure

Pure is described by its author as not a conventional theme: it has no visual creative optimizations, no complex colour design and no custom plugin functions, keeping Obsidian's original flavour while making minor adjustments to operation and usability. Its features are a ribbon that shows and hides automatically as the mouse enters or leaves the left side of the window, a round task list, and a tiered bullet list marking the first four levels with solid and hollow circles and squares. The README reports normal behaviour on macOS and Windows, and on iOS and iPadOS apart from the ribbon, with Linux and Android untested, and its log records 0.1.6 as an adaptation to Obsidian 1.6.
