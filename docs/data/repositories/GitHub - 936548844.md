# MrParalloid/pomme-notes

Pomme Notes is a lightweight, subtle theme designed to harmonize with macOS and iOS, following a Native+ philosophy that keeps the familiar structure of the default Obsidian interface while adding organic textures, smoother animations and a Liquid Glass aesthetic. Translucent frosted overlays blur the content behind them, the Quick Switcher and settings modals float, and the status bar becomes a centered floating capsule, while contrast is softened away from pure white and pure black and blockquotes, highlights and checkboxes derive their colours from the primary accent hue. Mobile work gives larger touch targets, a glass effect tuned for mobile performance on navigation bars and floating menus, and simplified sidebars on smaller screens. Dedicated styling is included for plugins such as Dataview, Calendar and Ink.
