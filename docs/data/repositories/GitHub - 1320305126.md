# dawnofthedebayan/Confidant

Confidant reads journal entries in a vault and generates reflective summaries at weekly, biweekly, monthly and yearly tiers, each tier reasoning over the summaries below it rather than the raw entries. Thirteen reflection frameworks drawn from psychological and philosophical traditions can be selected or replaced with a custom one, and person, tone, directness and length are configurable. Mood, valence, energy, sleep, company and topics are extracted per entry and feed both the summaries and a dashboard of streaks, mood trends and word and topic counts that needs no model at all. It works with any OpenAI-compatible backend, OpenRouter or a local server, and generates only on demand from a ribbon button or the command palette.
