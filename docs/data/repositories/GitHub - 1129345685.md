# COKAIZOKU/obsidian-global-ticker

Adds a top ticker for headline news and a bottom ticker for stock updates, drawing on the Currents and Finnhub APIs named in the recorded description. Display, scrolling speed, direction, refresh behaviour and separate footers for each ticker are configurable, headlines are clickable and open their source, and news can be filtered by category, domain, country and language. The README states that registration for both APIs is required, that the free news plan allows up to 20 requests and 10 headlines, and it points readers who prefer not to register to two other plugins.
