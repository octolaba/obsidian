# misterGF/obsidian-surface

Surface scans every Markdown file for headings matching one of the enabled date formats and treats everything below such a heading, up to the next heading of equal or higher level, as that date's entry; a sidebar panel then browses those entries by day, week or month. A second and independent mode collects any heading containing a configured term, such as Important or Follow Up, into a Pinned tab grouped by term and regardless of date. The panel opens from a ribbon calendar icon or a command, and arrow controls step backwards and forwards in the date modes.
