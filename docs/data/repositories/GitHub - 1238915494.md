# dualyze-ai/obsidian-japanese-calendar

Japanese Calendar adds a calendar to Obsidian with month, two-month, six-month and year views that adapt to the width of the sidebar. Japanese public holidays taken from official Cabinet Office data are highlighted in red with their names, the header shows the wareki era, and cells can carry rokuyo and kichijitsu auspicious days with hover tooltips. Events can be added to any date with coloured markers and popovers, any note can be attached to a date or an event, and clicking a date creates or opens the daily note. Settings cover the first day of the week, weekend colouring, a light and dark toggle independent of Obsidian's own theme, and persistence of the last display mode.
