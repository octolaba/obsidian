# zakiruz/synapse

Bonds treats links in Obsidian as notes in their own right: notes are atoms, and the connection between two of them is a bond that is itself a note carrying its own content. Information belonging to a relationship — the README's examples are credentials for a site, the reason two ideas connect, and the history between two people — lives in the bond note alone and is rendered automatically at the bottom of both endpoint notes. That rendered section is titled Synapses by default and the title is configurable. The README frames the design as correcting an inversion in which notes get structure while links stay bare pointers.
