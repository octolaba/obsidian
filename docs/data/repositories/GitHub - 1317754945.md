# SirHobby/doms

A mobile-first workout tracker that records sets per body part rather than exercises, weights or reps, with the week's completion and streaks as the measure of progress. Each session is one Markdown note in a chosen folder, carrying flat frontmatter for the date, week, routine, per-body-part sets and total, so the history stays queryable by Dataview or Bases. The Week tab counts sets as they happen and keeps an unfinished session resumable until it is committed, custom and backdated sessions reach up to 30 days back, and Stats adds streaks, a monthly activity calendar and weekly volume against the routine's target. Four routines are offered, from three days a week to push, pull and legs across seven, plus a custom option, and the plugin bundles an exercise library and physio-style rehab material.
