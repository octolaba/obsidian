# andrewmcgivery/obsidian-soundscapes

Soundscapes adds a music and ambiance player to the Obsidian status bar for lofi beats, nature sounds and other background audio, with the chosen soundscape and whether it starts with Obsidian set in settings and the volume remembered between launches. A custom soundscape is a playlist of YouTube links built in the settings screen, with next and previous buttons for moving between its tracks. The My Music soundscape indexes a local folder of MP3 files, re-indexing occasionally in the background, and adds a dedicated view with play, pause, next, previous, seek, shuffle and search that keeps playing after the view is closed.
