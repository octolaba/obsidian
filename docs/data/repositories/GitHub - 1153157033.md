# tulgardinc/obsidian-interactive-timeline

Timeline View places notes on an infinite scrollable timeline canvas with panning and zooming. A note joins a timeline by carrying timeline, date-start and date-end frontmatter with an optional colour, and dates may be written in European or US style or in an extended form covering ranges from twenty billion years BCE to twenty billion years CE. Cards can be multi-selected, dragged across layers and time, resized by their edges and colour-coded, with undo and redo for move, resize and layer changes. Several named timelines can be configured in settings, each scoped to a folder that is scanned recursively, and reference cards link one timeline to another.
