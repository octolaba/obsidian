# kdnk/obsidian-bullet

Bullet turns Markdown lists in Obsidian into a keyboard-driven outliner closer to Workflowy or Roam Research, while the note keeps its plain-text structure. Enter, Shift-Enter, Tab, Shift-Tab and repeated select-all operate on list structure rather than on raw Markdown prefixes, an item moves or drags together with every nested child, and the caret is kept in editable content away from hidden bullet or checkbox markup. Indentation guides connect nested items and branches fold from the guides themselves, while native list and heading fold controls move to the right edge in Live Preview. The README states that Bullet requires Obsidian 1.12.7 or later and supports desktop and mobile, with editing, appearance, folding and drag-and-drop behaviour adjustable in its settings.
