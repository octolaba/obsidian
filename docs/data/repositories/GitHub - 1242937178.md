# jdsimcoe/obsidian-books

Books adds a long-form manuscript authoring environment on top of ordinary Markdown files stored under a Books folder in the vault. A left sidebar navigator lists books with chapter and word counts, and a spine view organises a book into sections, chapters, interludes, appendices, back matter and canvases with drag reordering. Chapters get a manuscript treatment — serif typography, drop caps, paragraph indentation and numbering, small caps and TK highlighting — while storage filenames stay stable behind readable book and chapter titles in the editor. A per-book scratchpad captures selections for reuse, a research sidebar inserts blocks from notes outside the Books folder as blockquote citations, and the ordered spine compiles into one Markdown manuscript for export.
