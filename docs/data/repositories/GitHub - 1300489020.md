# extzzy/Meeting-Tools

Meeting Tools creates Minutes of Meeting notes in Obsidian from Markdown templates, with a compact form replacing the manual work of creating a file, choosing a folder, copying headings and formatting tasks. A Quick meeting command appends a date-and-outcome entry to a meeting journal, while New meeting produces a structured note with participants, decisions, tasks and custom sections; tasks are entered through a dynamic form with title, assignee and due date and can be reviewed or added in a searchable dashboard. Notes can be organised by year and month, by year, or without subfolders, and the template, meeting, journal and task folders are configured independently. The README states the plugin works locally through the Obsidian Vault API without a server, container, account or network connection, and offers an English or Russian interface.
