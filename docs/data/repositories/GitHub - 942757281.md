# mts7/obsidian-word-frequency

Word Frequency counts the words in the current note and lists them with their counts in a sidebar, ordered from the most to the least common, with special characters excluded from a word. The sidebar is opened from a ribbon icon or a command, and a text field at its top filters the list to words containing what is typed. The README describes the counts as a way to identify keywords for purposes such as SEO or a resume, and records a blacklist: clicking a trash icon beside a word hides it from the counts, and the list of ignored words is editable in the settings.
