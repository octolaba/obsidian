# rvzenteno/O-Monthly-Expense-Tracker

Monthly Expense Tracker records recurring monthly bills and subscriptions, each with a name, amount, due day, payment method, optional category and a start and optional end month, and marks them paid with an optional confirmation number. A sidebar dashboard opened from a wallet ribbon icon navigates between months, color-codes expenses as paid, unpaid or overdue, and shows summary totals, while the status bar carries the current unpaid count. Unpaid expenses carry over into the next month, monthly notes can be generated automatically or on command, and discontinued expenses can be archived rather than deleted. Payment history per expense, monthly summaries and reports over a chosen date range are available on desktop and mobile.
