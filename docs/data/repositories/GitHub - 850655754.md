# jtprogru/obsidian-vault-full-statistics-plugin

Vault Full Statistics quantifies the shape of an Obsidian vault, offering a click-to-cycle status bar item for the headline numbers and a side view for the detail. The status bar reports notes, links, tags and a quality-of-vault ratio of links divided by notes, own and source counts, concepts and orphans without incoming links, and the share of source notes referenced by at least one own note. The side view adds a hero panel, an own-versus-source ratio bar with concepts as a grey zone, a metrics grid, dangling sources, tangles, inbox health, taxonomy drift and a 30-day sparkline. The README states that the plugin is a modified fork of the Obsidian Vault Statistics Plugin, requires Obsidian 1.13.1 or later on desktop, and offers English by default with Russian and follow-Obsidian as options.
