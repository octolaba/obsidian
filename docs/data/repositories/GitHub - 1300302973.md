# NikShukrem/obsidian-constellations

Constellations is a 3D star map offered as an alternative to Obsidian's graph view: a note becomes a star sized and colored by how connected it is, a tag shared by two or more notes becomes a constellation whose stars are joined by lines, and a top-level folder becomes a universe that grows spiral arms once it is big enough. The vault's most-connected note is drawn as a pulsating quasar with particle jets, brand-new or fast-growing notes appear as birth or supernova flashes without a rebuild, and untagged notes drift as a dense bulge at the core of their universe. Clicking a star flies the camera to it and opens an info card with tags, a text preview and constellation neighbors, and a toolbar dropdown jumps between universes.
