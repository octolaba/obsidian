# mmattia09/obsidian-project-manager

Bases Timeline adds a Notion-style Timeline view alongside the native Table, Cards and List views of Obsidian Bases, intended for managing projects that are notes with start-date, end-date and priority frontmatter. Projects appear as horizontal bars with a colored priority pill and a collapsible side panel, and dragging a bar or its edges writes the new dates back to the frontmatter; an unscheduled project can be scheduled by clicking a previewed bar in its row. The README also records a red today indicator with a re-centering button, zoom levels from day to month, arrow chips for bars scrolled out of view, and click and modifier-click behaviours for opening the note in the same tab, a new tab or a split.
