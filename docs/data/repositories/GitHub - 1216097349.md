# ksawl/obsidian-alchemist

Obsidian plugin that bundles several unrelated vault utilities into one toolkit. It exports notes together with their linked images, audio and PDFs to the `.textbundle` and `.textpack` formats and imports them back, following linked notes up to five levels deep and hashing files to avoid duplicates. A desktop-only converter drives FFmpeg to turn recordings and the audio track of MP4, MOV, MKV and AVI files into MP3 or FLAC in bulk, taking ID3 tags from YAML frontmatter with fallbacks to the note H1 and creation date. It also adds a CSV export button to Dataview tables, recording the source note and an export timestamp, and strips tracking parameters such as UTM from pasted links.
