# ReadineApp/readine-obsidian

Pulls articles from the Readine service into the vault in one direction only, so local edits to synced notes are never overwritten. Notes are written as Markdown or HTML, with a path template built from tokens such as feed name, date parts and title, and an article template controlling frontmatter and layout. Auto-sync runs on a chosen interval from five minutes to twenty-four hours or not at all, network gating restricts it to Wi-Fi, Wi-Fi and cellular, or any network, images and files can be fetched alongside articles, and a progress bar reports the run. A delete policy decides whether server-deleted articles are removed, cached articles older than a chosen age are cleaned up, and favorited articles or articles carrying notes can be exempted from deletion.
