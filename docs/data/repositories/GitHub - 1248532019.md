# wesswart77/obsidian-job-search

Job Search Tracker records job applications in an Obsidian vault, covering company, role, location, salary range, job address, source and status. Commands add an application, update its stage with a note, and add a contact linked to a company or application with name, role, email and notes. An interview preparation note with sections for company research, questions to ask and answers to prepare is created automatically when a status moves to interview. A pipeline sidebar shows the applications as a kanban by status, from bookmarked through applied, phone screen, interview, offer, rejected and withdrawn, with cards showing role, company and the date applied.
