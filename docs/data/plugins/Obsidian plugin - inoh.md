# Inoh

Underlines words from an Inoh vocabulary deck as they are typed, and hovering or tapping a highlight opens the full card with its definition, phonetic transcription, example sentence, pronunciation audio, a link to the word in Inoh and a remove-from-deck action. New words are collected by selecting text and using Add to Inoh, with inflected forms matched back to their entries and a choice of meaning where a word has several definitions. The deck is cached locally so highlighting works offline, and signing in takes an email address and a six-digit code.
