# Email to Vault

Creates a free mailbox in one click and turns the emails sent to it into Markdown notes in the vault, fetched automatically with their full metadata and attachments. No backend or paid service of your own is needed, but the mail is routed through third-party mail.tm servers, which is why the plugin itself advises against sending sensitive data.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "email-to-vault"
    name:   "Email to Vault"
    author: "Michael Naumov"
    repo:   "mnaoumov/obsidian-email-to-vault"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/email-to-vault"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/mnaoumov/obsidian-email-to-vault"
    description: "Sends emails to your vault as notes. Create a free mailbox with one click — no backend or paid service required. - This plugin has not been manually reviewed by Obsidian staff."
    about:       "Create a free one-click email mailbox and forward emails into your vault as Markdown notes. Fetch messages automatically with full metadata and attachments; emails route through third-party mail.tm servers, so avoid sending sensitive data."

    stats: {
        downloads:  1448
        updated_at: 1784677151000
    }
}
```
