# Shiori Bookshelf

Shiori Bookshelf turns a vault into a browsable digital library of manga, light novels and ebooks, organising PDF, EPUB and CBZ files into series or single libraries. A grid bookshelf shows automatically extracted covers alongside companion markdown metadata, with search, reading-status filters and lazy loading. Context-menu actions scan libraries, open metadata, rename entries and hide cover and metadata files, and external readers are supported.
