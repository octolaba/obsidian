# Day Planner OG

Plans a day from a plain task list in a Markdown note, turning it into timed pomodoro-style blocks with drag-and-drop and a weekly view. Progress appears as a visual gauge, in the status bar and in notifications at the end of a block, tasks are shown on a vertical timeline, and current and past blocks are marked and highlighted in the linked note. It is described as bare bones and as preserving the features and behavior of the original plugin.
