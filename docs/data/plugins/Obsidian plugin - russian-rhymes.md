# Russian Rhymes

Russian Rhymes is an offline Russian rhyming dictionary whose phonetic engine groups words by sound into exact, near, slant and assonance tiers and highlights alliteration. Stress marks are shown and can be tweaked to re-rank the rhymes, while definitions, etymology and related words are available with filtering by meaning. Dictionaries can be imported for offline use on mobile and desktop, and the recorded description says a word is looked up by pressing Ctrl+C twice.

    name:   "Russian Rhymes"
    author: "mrrepac"

See stress marks and tweak stress to re-rank rhymes; view definitions, etymology and related words, filter by meaning, and import your dictionaries for offline mobile and desktop use."
