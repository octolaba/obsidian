# Scroll Anchor

Scroll Anchor remembers the scroll and cursor position of every note across all tabs and restores the open tabs to their saved spots after the app restarts. Positions are saved continuously in both editing and reading modes, with aggressive mobile-first saving so that a place is not lost.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "scroll-anchor"
    name:   "Scroll Anchor"
    author: "senjanson"
    repo:   "senjanson/obsidian-scroll-anchor"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/scroll-anchor"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/senjanson/obsidian-scroll-anchor"
    description: "Remembers scroll and cursor position for every note across all tabs. Restores all open tabs on app restart — great for mobile. - This plugin has not been manually reviewed by Obsidian staff."
    about:       "Anchor scroll and cursor positions per note and restore all open tabs to their saved spots after app restart. Save positions continuously in both edit and reading modes with mobile-first aggressive saves to avoid losing your place."
```
