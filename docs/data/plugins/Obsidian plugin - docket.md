# Docket

Manages projects, tasks and subtasks with statuses, due dates and automatic roll-up progress. The Docket panel shows a Gantt timeline, a tree-structured WBS or a Kanban board where dragging a card between columns updates its status. Items trace back to meetings and daily notes through frontmatter links.
