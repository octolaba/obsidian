# Ink Floating TOC

Shows a floating table of contents drawn as proportional bars for headings H1 to H6, giving a compact visual overview of a document's structure. Mouse and touch controls scroll, fold headings and collapse sub-sections, and colors, bar styles and sizing are customizable across devices.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "ink-floating-toc"
    name:   "Ink Floating TOC"
    author: "Jayanta Kumar Das"
    repo:   "dsvictor/ink-floating-toc"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/ink-floating-toc"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/dsvictor/ink-floating-toc"
    description: "A highly customizable, floating Table of Contents with multi-device support. - This plugin has not been manually reviewed by Obsidian staff."
    about:       "Display a minimalist floating Table of Contents as proportional bars for H1–H6 to give a compact, visual overview of document structure. Adapt controls for mouse and touch to scroll, fold headings, and collapse sub-sections. Customize colors, bar styles, and sizing to match your theme."
```
