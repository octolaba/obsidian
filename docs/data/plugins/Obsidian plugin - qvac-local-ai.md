# Local AI for your vault

Chats with the vault on your own machine through a local QVAC companion, answering from semantic search with citations back to the notes. Inline writing commands and OCR for images are included, and related notes are discovered or created with suggested links. A model can be fine-tuned on your notes so that answers mirror your knowledge and style, and the recorded inputs state that nothing leaves your machine.
