# RecallFlow AI

Generates flashcards with AI from a selection or a whole note, then previews, edits and saves them as question-and-answer, cloze or multiple-choice cards. Review uses the FSRS spaced-repetition scheduler with interactive answers, and a cram mode replays the day's cards without changing their schedule. Progress is shown as a 12-month heatmap, and all data is stored locally so reviewing works offline.
