# VaultGuard

VaultGuard puts a customizable lockscreen in front of the vault, encrypting with AES-GCM and PBKDF2 at 250k iterations and reporting password strength through zxcvbn in real time. Backgrounds and greetings are configurable, and optional full-vault encryption of markdown files runs in batches with fault-tolerant decryption. The vault can auto-lock after a period of inactivity.
