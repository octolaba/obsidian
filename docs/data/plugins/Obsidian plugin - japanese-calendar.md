# Japanese Calendar

Japanese Calendar shows Japanese public and substitute holidays from official government data, marking them in red with the holiday name inside the cell. It also displays the Japanese era, or wareki, and rokuyo, and colors weekends and dates from adjacent months. Clicking a date creates or opens the daily note, with holiday callouts added automatically.
