# Weave EPUB AI Reader

Reads EPUB books inside Obsidian with highlights and deep links, and embeds AI actions into the reader's own selection toolbar. Selecting a word, sentence or paragraph offers concept explanation, English translation, grammar analysis, contextual commentary or a custom assistant, answered by DeepSeek. The result appears in a window positioned to stay inside the viewport.
