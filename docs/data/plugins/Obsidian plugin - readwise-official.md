# Readwise Official

Syncs highlights from Readwise into the vault, covering sources such as Kindle, Apple Books, Google Play Books, Pocket, Instapaper, Medium, Twitter, and PDFs. The export format is customizable with Jinja2 templates and can carry metadata such as cover image, author, chapter headings, tags, and the original URL. An active Readwise subscription is required.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "readwise-official"
    name:   "Readwise Official"
    author: "readwiseio"
    repo:   "readwiseio/obsidian-readwise"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/readwise-official"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/readwiseio/obsidian-readwise"
    description: "Sync highlights from Readwise to your vault."
    about:       "Sync highlights from Readwise into Obsidian automatically from Kindle, Apple Books, Google Play Books, Pocket, Instapaper, Medium, Twitter, PDFs, and more. Customize export format with Jinja2 templates and include enriched metadata like cover image, author, chapter headings, tags, and original URL; requires an active Readwise subscription."

    stats: {

}
```
