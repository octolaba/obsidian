# stardew-valley-pals

Stardew Valley pets and characters are brought into the vault as animated pixel sprites covering 11 species and more than 35 characters, shown in a dockable panel or as a free-roaming overlay with draggable companions and seasonal backgrounds. An optional persona mode adds AI speech bubbles with contextual comments, auto-rants and multilingual prompts through OpenAI-compatible APIs.
