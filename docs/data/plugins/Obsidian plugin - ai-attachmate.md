# AI Attachmate

AI Attachmate transcribes PDFs, images and Canvas files into searchable Markdown notes, using Google Gemini for PDFs and images and a local conversion for Canvas. Transcripts are saved beside the attachment or in a chosen folder, a top user zone survives re-transcription, and custom prompts and templates shape the output. A sidebar reports background progress, files can be filtered, and orphaned transcripts are removed when their attachment is deleted.
