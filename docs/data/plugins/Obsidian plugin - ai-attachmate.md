# AI Attachmate

Transcribes PDFs, images and Canvas files into searchable Markdown notes, sending PDFs and images to Google Gemini with a user-supplied key while Canvas is converted locally. Transcripts are written beside the attachment or into a chosen folder, and a user zone at the top of each transcript survives re-transcription. Custom prompts and templates shape the output, a sidebar reports background progress, files can be filtered, and transcripts orphaned by a deleted attachment can be removed.

    about:       "Transcribe PDFs, images (.png/.jpg/.jpeg) and Canvas files into searchable Markdown notes using Google Gemini for PDFs/images and local conversion for Canvas. Save transcripts next to attachments or to a chosen folder, preserve a top user zone across re-transcriptions, and apply custom prompts and templates.
