# Swipe Words

Turns a folder of notes into swipeable flashcards that are swiped or tapped through and flipped to reveal answers. Ratings of Again, Hard, Good or Easy schedule the next review with SM-2 spaced repetition. The recorded About text states that due date, interval and ease are recorded in note frontmatter.

    id:     "swipe-words"
    name:   "Swipe Words"
    author: "LeafChage"
    repo:   "leafchage/swipe-words"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/swipe-words"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/leafchage/swipe-words"

    about:       "Turn a folder of notes into Tinder-style flashcards and swipe or tap through them. Flip cards to reveal answers and rate Again / Hard / Good / Easy to schedule reviews with SM-2 spaced repetition while the plugin records due, interval, and ease in frontmatter."
