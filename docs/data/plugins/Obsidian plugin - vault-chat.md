# Vault Chat

Indexes the vault so that a ChatGPT-backed assistant can answer questions about its content. The active note or the whole vault can be discussed, individual notes summarized, and semantic searches run across files with context-aware responses.

plugin: {
    id:     "vault-chat"
    name:   "Vault Chat"
    author: "exoascension"
    repo:   "exoascension/vault-chat"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/vault-chat"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/exoascension/vault-chat"
    description: "A ChatGPT bot trained on your vault notes. Ask your AI questions about your own thoughts and ideas!"
    about:       "Chat with ChatGPT about your vault or active note, summarize individual notes, and run semantic searches across your files. Index your vault to train the assistant and query your notes conversationally with context-aware responses."
