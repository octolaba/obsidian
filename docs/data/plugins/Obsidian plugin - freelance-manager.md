# Freelance Manager

Manages clients, projects, time logs and invoices as plain Markdown, without external services. Clients and projects are created, hours logged, and itemized invoice notes generated that sum un-invoiced time logs with totals and due dates. A dashboard sidebar shows active projects, total hours and estimated earnings.
