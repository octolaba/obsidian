# RPG Detect Dice Roll

RPG Detect Dice Roll finds dice formulas in reading view and turns them into clickable roll controls, covering common and advanced expressions such as d20, 1d20+4 and 4d6kh3. Recent results are kept in a roll history side panel with timestamps and quick-dice buttons. Custom numeric or narrative dice can be defined with optional weighting, and rolls can be made with advantage or disadvantage.
