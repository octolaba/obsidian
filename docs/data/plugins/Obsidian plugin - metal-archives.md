# Metal Archives (Unofficial)

Metal Archives (Unofficial) creates notes about metal bands and albums by fetching their metadata from Metal Archives. Searching for a band or an album generates a prefilled note, and the resulting bands, albums and tags map those relationships in the vault graph.
