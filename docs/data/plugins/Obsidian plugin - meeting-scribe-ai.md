# Meeting Scribe AI

Records meeting audio into the vault and keeps the original audio files. The recording is transcribed with Doubao Speech, then polished and summarized by any OpenAI-compatible model. Structured Markdown minutes are written into the current note or a new file, with recovery and retry support for long recordings.
