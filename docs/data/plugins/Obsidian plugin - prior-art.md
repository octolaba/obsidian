# Prior Art

Shows notes that already exist and resemble the one being titled and written, listing up to five matches in a sidebar together with the reason for each match. Duplicates are merged by opening the notes side by side, appending the new content into the existing note, or redirecting the duplicate by adding an alias, replacing its body with a redirect and offering to trash it. Confirmation, undo, and preservation of frontmatter accompany the merge.
