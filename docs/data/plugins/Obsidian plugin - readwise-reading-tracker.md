# Readwise Reading Tracker

Syncs reading documents from Readwise and Reader and tracks reading progress inside the vault. A dashboard shows a heatmap, current and completed books, highlight navigation, and reading timelines for books, EPUBs, and PDFs. Linked highlight notes and inbox notes are created with book, source, and date properties, and the recorded text states that the data stays local.
