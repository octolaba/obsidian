# Reminder Telegram

Sends Telegram notifications when tasks reach their deadline. Tasks are parsed from YAML frontmatter or from inline lists, scanning the vault or one folder, and due items are sent as grouped messages through the Telegram Bot API. Duplicate alerts are prevented.
