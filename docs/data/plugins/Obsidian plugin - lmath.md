# LMath

Graphs functions, systems of equations, symbolic derivatives and definite integrals in interactive blocks inside a note. Each block shows the LaTeX-rendered formula beside a Cartesian plane with pan, zoom, crosshair and a rail mode for walking along curves. Explicit, implicit, parametric and polar curves are rendered with arc-length tracing, dense implicit sets are rasterized, integral regions are shaded and intersections of systems are marked.
