# TaskWeaver

TaskWeaver gathers the TODOs scattered through a vault into one Kanban-style view, reachable from a sidebar list and switchable board layouts. Sub-tasks expand with progress tracking, and priorities and due-date badges are shown on the cards. Cards are dragged between customizable columns based on date, overdue state or tag, with work-in-progress limits, pinning and archiving, and a jump back to the source note.
