# Anki Forge

Creates Anki cards from within notes, keeping the explanations, images, Markdown, math, code, cloze deletions and tags alongside the surrounding text. Every create, update and deletion is previewed before syncing, and cards are organized by tags, folders or frontmatter. The sync runs both ways, so editing or deleting a card in Anki is reflected in Obsidian and the reverse, subject to confirmation.
