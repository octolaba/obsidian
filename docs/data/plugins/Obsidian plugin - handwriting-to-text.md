# Handwriting to Text

Transcribes photographed handwritten pages with Google Gemini and inserts the result at the cursor in the note. Images can come from the camera, a file, the clipboard or the vault, or be dragged and dropped, and the OCR output can be previewed and edited before insertion. HEIC images and multilingual handwriting, including Thai, are handled on desktop and mobile.
