# Mi Agrupacion

Mi Agrupacion records community activities, visits and the educational process of a Bahá'í grouping from a dashboard with quick access links. Visits are stored with sector, cycle, people visited, teachers, prayer, campaign and photos, while classes, circles and attendees are kept with autocompletable teacher names. It produces KPI reports by sector and by campaign and synchronizes data between members through Supabase; its recorded description and About text are written in Spanish.
