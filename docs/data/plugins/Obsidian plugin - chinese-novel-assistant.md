# Chinese Novel Assistant

Chinese Novel Assistant collects a local settings library whose keywords are highlighted in the text and previewed on hover, with entries added from the right-click menu and inserted inline through a fuzzy search. It also creates floating inspiration notes, counts Chinese characters and milestones, and applies novel-style typography. Punctuation, typos and sensitive words can be fixed in batches.
