# ART de ONE

Opens an AI panel that answers questions about selected text through any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, streaming the response, switching models and applying a full or partial reply back into the note. It also highlights text in six colors and inserts or edits tables through a visual grid picker and a floating toolbar.
