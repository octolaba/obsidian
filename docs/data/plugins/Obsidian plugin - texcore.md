# TeXcore

TeXcore numbers and references display math in the active note through identifier comments, adding tag numbers in arabic, alph, Alph, roman or Roman styles that are referenced by block reference or by eqref autocomplete. Rendered equations are previewed in suggestions and on hover, reusable LaTeX snippets and callout math are managed, and notes or whole folders export to PDF with headers, margins and a table of contents.
