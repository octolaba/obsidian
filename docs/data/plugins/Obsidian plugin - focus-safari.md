# Focus Safari

Opens a side panel that tracks focus time and populates a safari with animals as the cumulative total grows. Six animals are available for free and ten rarer ones are unlocked by a paid activation code, which is verified offline. The plugin makes no network calls and collects no telemetry.

    id:     "focus-safari"

    description: "Animals gather in a side panel while you stay focused on your notes. The longer you focus, the more show up. A paid activation code unlocks rarer animals. - This plugin has not been manually reviewed by Obsidian staff."
