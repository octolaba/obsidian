# Sidebar Expand on Hover

Hovering the mouse over a ribbon expands the corresponding sidebar, and moving away collapses it again. Double-clicking a ribbon pins or unpins the sidebar state, the width is adjusted by dragging, and sidebar states are preserved across a relaunch.
