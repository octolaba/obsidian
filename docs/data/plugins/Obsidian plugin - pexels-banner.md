# Pixel Banner

Pixel Banner adds customizable banner images to notes, sourced from AI generation, stock collections such as Pexels, Unsplash, Pixabay and Flickr, local files, direct URLs, or MP4 and MOV videos. Position, sizing, transparency, icons and inline title styling are controlled from the note properties.
