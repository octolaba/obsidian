# Ntfy Notifications

Sends ntfy push notifications from Obsidian by scanning unchecked tasks and standalone reminder lines in Markdown, understanding several reminder syntaxes and date and time formats. Queued, scheduled and delivered notifications are managed in an internal notification manager, and alerts can be delayed or repeated. Notifications can optionally be routed through AI webhooks.
