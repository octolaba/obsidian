# ReadItLater

This plugin saves online content into the vault as Markdown notes, archiving web pages, videos and social posts with content-specific templates for articles, YouTube, Twitter and more. Images can be downloaded, lists of URLs batch-processed, and saved content inserted into new or existing notes through a template engine with variables. The recorded text describes it as usable on Obsidian desktop and on iOS and Android.
