# Made Up Words

Stores invented words as a folder of Markdown notes with structured frontmatter and indexes them for fast lookup. A word is looked up by highlighting or hovering it, showing every matching sense and recognizing inflected forms, and a side-panel lexicon browses the vocabulary across several languages. Phonological placeholders are generated by a deterministic cipher and, per the recorded text, are clearly labeled as placeholders.
