# Caissa

Caissa embeds playable chess boards in notes for studying openings and endgames and browsing 68 curated World Championship games. Any position can be played against an offline Stockfish with adjustable strength, and PGNs can be imported, pasted or pulled from Lichess and annotated. Variations can be compared across multiple boards, Lichess opening statistics explored, and boards exported as PNG or SVG.
