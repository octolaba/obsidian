# Screvi Sync

Screvi Sync imports highlights and sources from Screvi, automatically or on demand. Highlights are organized into source-specific notes for books, articles, tweets and other source types, and kept up to date at regular intervals. A Screvi account with an active trial or subscription is required.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "screvi"
    name:   "Screvi Sync"
    author: "pedroaraujo74"
    repo:   "pedroaraujo74/screvi-obsidian-plugin"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/screvi"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/pedroaraujo74/screvi-obsidian-plugin"
    description: "Import highlights and sources from Screvi. - This plugin has not been manually reviewed by Obsidian staff."
    about:       "Export and sync Screvi highlights to Obsidian automatically or on demand. Organize highlights into source-specific notes (books, articles, tweets, etc.) and keep them updated at regular intervals. Ensure you have a Screvi account with an active trial or subscription. https://screvi.com"
```
