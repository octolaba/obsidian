# Wechat Public Platform

Publishes articles, images and videos from the vault to a WeChat Public Account and to Baidu Baijiahao. Media can be uploaded and downloaded, drafts added, mass messages sent and YouTube videos imported. A statistics dashboard collects the publishing and audience data.
