# Bookshelf

Bookshelf gathers book notes in one place and tracks reading progress, recording status and completion details. Reading insights and statistics are shown so past reading habits can be reviewed.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "bookshelf"
    name:   "Bookshelf"
    author: "weph"
    repo:   "weph/obsidian-bookshelf"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/bookshelf"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/weph/obsidian-bookshelf"
    description: "Manage your books and reading progress"
    about:       "Organize your personal bookshelf and gather all book notes in one place. Track reading progress and record statuses and completion details. View reading insights and statistics to understand and review your reading habits."
```
