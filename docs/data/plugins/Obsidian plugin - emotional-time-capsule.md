# Emotional Time Capsule

Creates sealed emotional time capsules — letters, reflections, milestones or messages to a future self — from prompts and emotion tags. A capsule stays hidden until its chosen revisit date, and a dashboard groups them as ready to revisit, sealed, unsealed, without a revisit date, or archived. Capsules can be opened early, postponed, archived or deleted.
