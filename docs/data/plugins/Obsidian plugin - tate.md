# Tate

Tate renders and edits Japanese notes in vertical writing, top to bottom and right to left, and states that editing stays smooth on files of over a million characters. Text is annotated with ruby, tate-chu-yoko, emphasis marks and headings in Aozora Bunko notation, and inline editing expands and collapses the raw Aozora text so writing happens in the rendered form without switching modes. An outline panel lists the headings extracted from that notation and jumps to any section, and a search panel finds and replaces across the whole file. Typography helpers write real full-width space characters for indentation rather than visual padding, and auto-indent matches the leading spaces of the preceding paragraph.
