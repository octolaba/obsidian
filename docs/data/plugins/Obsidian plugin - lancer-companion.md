# Lancer Companion

Tracks Lancer encounters and initiative in a dedicated sidebar with roster management, automatic detection of linked character notes and highlighting of the active turn. PCs and NPCs are imported from COMP/CON exports in .json or .lcp form into vault templates, extracting mechs, weapons, systems, talents and lore. Dice can be rolled inline in notes, and hovering a highlighted Lancer keyword shows the full rule as a tooltip.
