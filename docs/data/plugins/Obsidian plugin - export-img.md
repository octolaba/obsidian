# Export Img

Exports the reading view as images rendered through Obsidian's MarkdownRenderer, waiting for images, fonts and Mermaid diagrams to finish before the capture. A studio previews the result at single scale, fits wide blocks and splits long notes into pages. Images are copied or saved at selectable scale multipliers, and whole folders are batch-exported to a ZIP archive.
