# Research Paper

Research Paper searches academic databases from inside Obsidian and gathers relevant studies. An AI step then produces either an APA 7-formatted paper or a quick answer, with source papers reviewable and filterable and natural-language instructions accepted, and the resulting draft is inserted into the note. The recorded description names support for seven LLM providers and ten research domains.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "research-paper"
    name:   "Research Paper"
    author: "Jesús García"
    repo:   "jaliriogbarrios19/Research_Paper"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/research-paper"

    about:       "Search academic databases and gather relevant studies directly inside Obsidian. Generate APA 7–formatted papers or quick answers with AI, review and filter source papers, add natural-language instructions, and insert the resulting draft into your note."
```
