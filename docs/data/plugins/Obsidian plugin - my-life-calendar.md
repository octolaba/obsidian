# My Life Calendar

Draws a life-in-weeks grid of heart-shaped cells, 52 columns for the weeks of a year and one row per year, based on the Life in Weeks idea from WaitButWhy. Lived, future, birthday and event weeks are color-coded, and weeks holding a journal entry carry a yellow dot. A journal and an events list are built in, weeks show tooltips, and one click exports the data for the Life Calendar Android app.
