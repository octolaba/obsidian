# Multi Cloud Sync

Syncs individual vault folders to Notion, Dropbox, Google Drive and OneDrive at once, with per-folder and cross-drive mapping that covers subfolders. Sync runs in real time against a three-way snapshot with content merge, so files can also be managed outside Obsidian. Sync confirmations and configurable trash bins are provided to cut duplication.
