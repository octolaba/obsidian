# Clinical Workspace

Manages clinical follow-up, care episodes, tasks and a surgical logbook inside Obsidian as Markdown with YAML metadata, stable identifiers and Obsidian Bases, in a mobile-first and local-first arrangement. Patient sheets, task and operating-room queues, archives and integrity checks are the main surfaces. The stated scope excludes use as an electronic health record or for prescribing, diagnosis or autonomous decision support.
