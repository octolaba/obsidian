# Auto Classifier

Classifies notes automatically through an OpenAI-compatible model or Jina AI and suggests tags drawn from the vault. The title, the frontmatter, the full content or a selected area serves as the input, and the resulting tag is inserted where configured. The recorded inputs name DDC classification, keyword extraction and research papers among the intended cases.
