# Knox Sync

Syncs the vault with the Knox portal in order to back up files, share notes through Knox projects and keep several devices aligned. Syncs can be scheduled at regular intervals or during idle periods, and Knox documents are refreshed monthly so they are not deleted. Conflicts are resolved on a last-write-wins basis.
