# Yearly Glance

Shows a year at a glance and manages holidays, birthdays and custom events in one interactive overview, deriving age and zodiac for birthdays. Layouts, colors and emojis are customizable, events are filtered by type, empty dates are hidden on request, and clicking an event opens its details for viewing or editing. The Chinese lunar calendar and several interface languages are supported.
