# Bamboo Walking

Bamboo Walking is a reader for a personal writing column, browsing curated articles by category in a sidebar. Articles are saved to the vault with one click, searched full-text and navigated through a table of contents or the keyboard, while a bamboo progress bar tracks reading. Its recorded About states that it is usable immediately with zero configuration.
