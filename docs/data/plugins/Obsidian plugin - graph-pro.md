# GraphPro

Extends the graph view with layout and position controls, including saving and restoring positions, undo and redo, alignment, arranging in a circle, snap-to-grid and a grid display. Selection tools cover area selection, regular expressions and selecting related notes or backlinks, and selection statistics and neighbour labels are shown.
