# JW Meeting Timer

Fetches the weekly Life and Ministry meeting programme from wol.jw.org in the chosen language and builds per-assignment countdown timers in a sidebar. Each part shows elapsed time, a progress bar and its scheduled end time, with optional one-minute advice timers, overtime alerts and a global meeting progress bar. Weeks can be navigated, parts customized, and text summaries exported.
