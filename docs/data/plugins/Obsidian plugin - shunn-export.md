# Shunn Export

Exports a note as a DOCX manuscript in William Shunn's Proper Manuscript Format, as expected for short fiction submissions. Formatting is applied automatically: Courier, Courier New or Times New Roman at 12pt, double spacing, half-inch first-line indents and one-inch margins. A title page and headers are generated, and scene breaks, italics and em dashes follow the same standard.
