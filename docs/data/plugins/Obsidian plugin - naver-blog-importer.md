# Naver Blog Importer

Imports Naver Blog and Cafe posts into the vault one at a time or in bulk, saving images locally and embedding YouTube videos natively. Blogs can be subscribed to for automatic syncing, and private cafe articles are reached with cookie authentication. Tags and excerpts are generated automatically, optionally with AI, and duplicates are skipped.
