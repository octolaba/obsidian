# Yet Another Memos Sync

Syncs Memos into daily notes incrementally, bounded by time and aware of deletions, against both the modern and the legacy Memos API. Entries render as emoji timelines, list callouts or cards, attachments download automatically, and sync runs periodically or at startup behind a multilingual interface.
