# HiWords

HiWords keeps vocabulary books on Canvas, with draggable cards and coloured nodes recording difficulty and mastery. Words being learned are highlighted across notes and PDFs, and hovering shows a Markdown-formatted definition, marks a word as mastered, plays pronunciation or requests an AI-assisted explanation. Highlights are synchronized automatically.
