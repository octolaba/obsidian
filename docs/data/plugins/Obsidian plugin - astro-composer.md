# Astro Composer

Turns notes into posts and pages for an Astro blog, applying kebab-case filenames, MDX support and standardized frontmatter. Obsidian wikilinks are converted into links Astro can follow, drafts can be marked with an underscore prefix, and posts can be renamed while their property values are preserved.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "astro-composer"
    name:   "Astro Composer"
    author: "David V. Kimball"
    repo:   "davidvkimball/obsidian-astro-composer"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/astro-composer"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/davidvkimball/obsidian-astro-composer"
    description: "Turn your notes into posts and pages for your Astro blog with automated content management features. - This plugin has not been manually reviewed by Obsidian staff."
    about:       "Compose Obsidian notes into Astro blog posts and pages with automated kebab-case filenames, MDX support, and frontmatter standardization. Convert Obsidian wikilinks to Astro-friendly links, manage drafts with optional underscore prefixes, and rename posts while preserving property values."

    stats: {

    }
}
```
