# Mini Notes

Mini Notes captures quick thoughts and shows them in a Google Keep-style, colourful card view for fast review. Cards can be pinned, tagged, coloured and reordered, and filtered by tag, folder, colour or type. The view follows the active theme and runs on iOS and Android.
