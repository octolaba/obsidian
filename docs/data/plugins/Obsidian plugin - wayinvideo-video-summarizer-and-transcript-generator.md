# Video Summarizer and Transcript Generator

Takes a pasted video or audio URL and a chosen output language and produces either a summary or a speaker-labeled transcript in a new note. The note carries the video title, the source link, a YouTube embed where one is available, highlights, clickable timestamps and speaker labels with consistent colours. The recorded sources are YouTube, TikTok, Vimeo, Twitch, Instagram, Facebook, Zoom, Rumble and Google Drive.
