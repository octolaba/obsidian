# EPUB Reader Plus

Opens .epub files from the vault in a centered, paginated reader that reflows responsively and follows the current theme's background. Pages are turned with the mouse wheel and navigated through a compact table of contents. Publisher styles are preserved, reading progress is kept per book, and a font-size change anchors the visible text before reflowing.
