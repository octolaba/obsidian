# FormulaR

Validates and completes LaTeX mathematical derivations inside align blocks in real time. Incorrect steps are marked with a red wavy underline, unverifiable lines with an orange dashed underline and optional AI review, explanations appear on hover, and AI next-step suggestions are offered when a line break is typed.
