# Live Background

Adds a dynamic background to the workspace from an image or a video, covering common still and animated formats. Placement and visual effects such as opacity, blur, brightness, and contrast are customizable, wallpapers can be scheduled by time or day or rotated automatically, and history and full-resolution images are managed. Transparency and modal styling are controlled through CSS selectors.
