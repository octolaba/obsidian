# Stashwise

Receives videos, posts, threads and articles shared from a phone to the Stashwise service, which reads them, video included, and writes a summary, the key takeaways and the topics they connect to. Those notes arrive in the vault as Markdown, searchable and linkable from any note, with topics linked to each other so graph view reflects what has been read. It runs on iOS and Android, so a note saved on a phone is waiting in the vault on that same phone, and anything written underneath a synced note is preserved and synced back to the Stashwise account.

    about:       "Most of what you find worth keeping turns up on your phone, in a video, a reel, a thread or an article, and most of it is never opened again. Stashwise is where you send it. Share anything to Stashwise from any app and it reads the whole thing, video included, then writes the summary, the key takeaways and the topics it connects to. This brings that library into your vault as Markdown notes, so saved material sits beside your own writing instead of behind another app. Search it from any note and drop in a link or a quote without breaking your flow. Topics link to each other, so graph view shows the shape of what you have been reading. It runs on iOS and Android too. Save something on your phone, open your vault on the same phone, and the finished note is already waiting, with no computer in the middle. Anything you write underneath a synced note is yours.
