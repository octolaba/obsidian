# Zima Topic Sync

Mirrors an Obsidian vault into a selected Zima Topic, one way from Obsidian to Zima. New and changed files are uploaded continuously, renames and deletions are mirrored, and hidden and system folders are excluded. A vault is bound to a Zima account through a single-use pairing link that expires after ten minutes.

```cue

    id:     "zima-topic-sync"
    name:   "Zima Topic Sync"
    author: "atopos31"
    repo:   "atopos31/zima-topic-sync"

    github_url:  "https://github.com/atopos31/zima-topic-sync"
    description: "Sync the current Obsidian vault into a selected Zima Topic. - This plugin has not been manually reviewed by Obsidian staff."
    about:       "Mirror your Obsidian vault into a selected Zima Topic for one-way Obsidian → Zima access. Sync continuously by uploading new or changed files, mirroring renames and deletions while excluding hidden and system folders; connect with single-use pairing links that bind a vault to a Zima account and expire after ten minutes."
}
```
