# Monthly Expense Tracker

Tracks recurring monthly expenses such as bills and subscriptions, recording each payment with an optional confirmation number. Monthly tracking notes are generated automatically and unpaid items carry over into the next month. An interactive sidebar dashboard shows color-coded status, monthly summaries, payment history and custom reports.
