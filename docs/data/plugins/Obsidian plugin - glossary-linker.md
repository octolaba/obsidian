# Glossary Linker

Finds glossary terms in notes and links them in any word form, matching plurals, declensions and verb endings through morphological stemming for English, Russian, Ukrainian, Spanish, German and French. A glossary term is a note in a chosen folder, optionally with aliases, and matches are highlighted like links with hover preview, click and context actions. They can be converted into permanent wiki links in one note, a selection or the whole vault, and an overview panel tracks how often each term is used, which are unused and which frequent words are not yet defined.
