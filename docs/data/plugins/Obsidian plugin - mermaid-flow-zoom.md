# Mermaid Flow Zoom

Adds zooming, panning, resizing and fullscreen viewing to rendered Mermaid diagrams. The mouse wheel zooms around the pointer, dragging pans the diagram, and trackpad horizontal scrolling and touch gestures are handled, with toolbar controls for zoom, reset and fullscreen. Reading view and Live Preview are supported wherever Obsidian exposes the rendered SVG.
