# Carbon Voice Sync

Carbon Voice Sync imports Carbon Voice conversations and voice memos into the vault as Markdown notes on desktop and mobile. The notes include transcripts, AI summaries and embedded audio playback, and People and Workspace wiki links are generated to connect conversations in the graph. Tokens are stored locally and syncing runs in the background.
