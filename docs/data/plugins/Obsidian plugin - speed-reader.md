# Speed Reader

Notes are read by rapid serial visual presentation: one word is flashed at a time with its optimal recognition point highlighted. Words per minute is adjusted while reading, pauses are inserted at punctuation and at long words, and a clickable progress bar jumps through the text. Reading can be limited to a selection or run in focus mode.
