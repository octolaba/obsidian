# Power Desk

Brings Microsoft 365 with multiple accounts, Google Calendar, CalDAV services such as iCloud, Fastmail and Nextcloud, and ICS feeds into Obsidian as month, week, work-week, day and agenda views, with a triage inbox and a linked note for every event. Dated vault notes can be shown as events without connecting an account, and Google and Microsoft events are created and edited by dragging, moving or resizing them on the grid, while CalDAV and ICS feeds are read-only. Event cards are draggable and resizable and carry details and one-click join buttons.
