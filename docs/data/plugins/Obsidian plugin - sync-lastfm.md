# Sync Last.fm

Fetches scrobbles, artists, albums and cover art from Last.fm and creates Markdown notes whose properties are meant to be used directly in Base. The recorded About text describes incremental syncs, artist and album notes carrying playcounts and covers, automatic linking of tracks to those notes, timezone adjustment and re-stamping, and backfilling history through the Last.fm API.
