# Wiktionary Sidebar

Looks a word up in Wiktionary from a sidebar when a selection is right-clicked in editor or reading view. The entry is shown structured into part of speech, definitions, etymology and synonyms, with direct links back to the source. Seven language editions are covered, German, English, French, Italian, Swedish, Dutch and Spanish, each read from its own native Wiktionary, and the styling follows the theme.
