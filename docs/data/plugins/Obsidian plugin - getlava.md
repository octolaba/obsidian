# Getlava

Provides a chat with AI models inside the vault and stores the conversations alongside the notes. Notes are mentioned with an at-sign to include their content in the assistant's responses, and the assistant stays read-only with respect to the files.

```cue

    id:     "getlava"
    name:   "Getlava"
    author: "Timur"
    repo:   "itipo/lava-plugin"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/getlava"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/itipo/lava-plugin"
    description: "Getlava brings the ideas you have already captured into the conversation—so your vault becomes active material for better thinking. - This plugin has not been manually reviewed by Obsidian staff."
    about:       "Chat with AI models directly inside your vault and store conversations alongside your notes. Mention notes with @ to include their content in assistant responses while keeping the assistant read-only to your files."
```
