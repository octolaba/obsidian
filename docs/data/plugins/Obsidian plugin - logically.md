# Logically AI Research Assistant

Runs an AI research assistant over the files in the vault, producing citation-backed research with inline citations and source tables. Semantic Scholar, Google and document retrieval modes search the vault and the web, models from OpenAI, Anthropic and Gemini can be used, and chat output is inserted straight into notes.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "logically"
    name:   "Logically AI Research Assistant"
    author: "afforai"
    repo:   "afforai/obsidian-logically-plugin"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/logically"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/afforai/obsidian-logically-plugin"
    description: "Use Logically's AI Research Assistant to conduct citation-backed research on the files in your vault. - This plugin has not been manually reviewed by Obsidian staff."
    about:       "Conduct citation-backed research inside Obsidian with inline citations and source tables. Use Semantic Scholar, Google, or Document Retrieval modes to search your vault and the web, run OpenAI, Anthropic, or Gemini models, and insert chat output directly into notes."

    stats: {
        downloads:  3282
        updated_at: 1780403870000
    }
}
```
