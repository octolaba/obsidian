# Realtime Transcription

Transcribes speech locally in real time using SenseVoice-Small, Silero VAD, and sherpa-onnx, with a live preview and switchable stable and fast modes. Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, and Cantonese are detected, non-Chinese speech can be translated into Chinese through OpenAI-compatible APIs, and AI polishing and auto-summaries can be applied. Output is exported as timestamped Obsidian Markdown with a persistent history.
