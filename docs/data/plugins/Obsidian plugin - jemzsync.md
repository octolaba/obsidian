# jemzsync

Detects which ecosystem a device belongs to and checks that the vault sits in a folder its cloud service can reach - iCloud on Apple devices, Google Drive on Windows and Android. Vaults are compared across devices to confirm that files match between the device and the cloud, and the vault is watched in real time so changes, including files arriving from other devices, are re-checked within seconds. Duplicate sync files are cleaned up, and exact fixes are shown when the cloud cannot reach the vault.
