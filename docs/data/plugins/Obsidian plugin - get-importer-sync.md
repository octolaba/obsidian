# Dedao Brain Importer

Imports notes from the Get service into the vault with incremental, silent background and scheduled syncing, alongside manual one-click or ZIP imports. Imported notes are viewed in a Moments timeline or a Canvas graph, attachments are preserved, wiki-links are handled experimentally, edits are detected automatically, and HTML mark highlights are converted to Obsidian highlight syntax.
