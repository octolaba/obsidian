# Chatting with AI

Chatting with AI is an agentic assistant that reads, edits and creates notes through conversation on phone, tablet and desktop. Fourteen vault-native tools let it read, search, create, rename, update frontmatter and backlinks and apply selection-scoped edits from the chat. Anthropic, OpenAI or a ChatGPT account sign-in can be used, and secrets are kept in the operating system keychain.
