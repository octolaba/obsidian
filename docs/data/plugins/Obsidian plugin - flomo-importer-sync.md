# Flomo Importer Enhanced

Imports Flomo memos into the vault as notes together with their image, audio and video attachments, optionally creating a Canvas or Moments. Syncing runs silently in the background on an automatic, hourly or manual schedule, and edited memos are detected and re-imported without producing duplicates. Attachments are kept in a simplified flat structure and the sync history can be reset.
