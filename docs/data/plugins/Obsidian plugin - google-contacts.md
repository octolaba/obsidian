# Google Contacts

Synchronizes Google Contacts into the vault as separate contact notes, with metadata in YAML frontmatter and free-form body text left to the user. Stale local contact notes can be audited, contacts filtered by Google label, folder and filename conventions and naming strategies chosen, including vCard and array formats, and relations converted into Obsidian links.
