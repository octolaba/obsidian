# Natural Language Dates (Revived)

Natural Language Dates (Revived) parses date and time expressions in English, French, German, Japanese, Dutch, Portuguese, Spanish and Italian, converting combined durations, weekday-plus-time phrases and date or range expressions into concrete dates. Its suggestions are document-aware: the plugin learns the patterns you use and detects nearby dates, analysing only the note currently open.
