# AI Mentor

Provides writing guidance and formatting help from Alfred, an AI mentor that explains selected text, redacts bullet lists and corrects or improves notes. Connects to the OpenAI or Perplexity APIs, so GPT-4 Turbo, Llama2, Mixtral 8x7b or web-sourced pplx online models can answer questions.
