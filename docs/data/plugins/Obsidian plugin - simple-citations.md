# Simple Citations

Simple Citations imports literature notes from Zotero through Better BibTeX exports and generates linked Obsidian notes ready for queries and review. Notes are added or updated from the bibliography, and manuscripts with citations export to DOCX through Pandoc and Citeproc with CSL styles.

    id:     "simple-citations"
    name:   "Simple Citations"
    author: "masaki39"
    repo:   "masaki39/simple-citations"

    github_url:  "https://github.com/masaki39/simple-citations"
