# Bullet

Bullet works with nested lists in the manner of Workflowy or RoamResearch for building deeply structured outlines. List items and sublists are moved, indented, outdented and reordered from commands and toolbar actions, vertical indentation lines are drawn, and the caret is kept inside the item content while editing.
