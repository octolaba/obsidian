# Canvas Positioning Toolkit

Canvas Positioning Toolkit offers three ways to move cards on a Canvas board. Auto-layout arranges the whole board or the current selection into a grid; pick up and place attaches the selected card to the pointer or finger with a hotkey until a click or a lift places it, with Escape cancelling and snapping it back; and a fuzzy text search across every card selects and picks up the match in one move. The inputs frame the second and third tools as answers to shrinking drag targets and dense clusters at low zoom.

Zoom out on a busy board and a card's drag target shrinks until grabbing the one you want means hovering around for the hand cursor.
