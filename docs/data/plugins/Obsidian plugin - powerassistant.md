# Power Assistant

Captures meetings, voice memos and other audio into structured notes with automatic transcription, through a local or cloud provider such as Whisper, AssemblyAI, Deepgram or WhisperX. Each note carries an AI-written summary with decisions, risks and questions, and actionable tasks written as real task lines with owners and dates. Attendee metadata, clickable timestamps, the raw transcript and the embedded audio are kept alongside them.
