# Focus Tracker

Tracks activity, progress and attention across notes and draws them on a visual timeline that shows patterns, gaps and consistency over time. Past focus is reviewed for successes and gaps, and future attention is planned against goals. Engagement across projects and habits is monitored in the same view.
