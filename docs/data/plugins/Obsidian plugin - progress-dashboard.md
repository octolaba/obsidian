# progress-dashboard

Visualizes learning-skill progress as interactive progress bars and hierarchical skill trees. Notes are linked automatically by scanning the YAML skill and skill-progress fields, and progress can also be adjusted by dragging. Associated notes are listed and opened from the view, categories are managed and pinned, start and end dates are tracked, and multiple vaults are supported.
