# Stereo

Streams music from a Navidrome or other Subsonic-compatible server through a sidebar player with selectable art treatments, full transport controls and a compact mini player. The library can be browsed and searched, with tracks and stations added to a queue and synced lyrics highlighted as they play. Plays are scrobbled back to the server, and favorites and playlists are saved there.
