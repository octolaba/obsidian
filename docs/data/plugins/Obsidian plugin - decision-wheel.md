# Decision Wheel

Puts a list of choices on a spinning wheel in the sidebar and spins it to pick one, with friction and inertia physics. Color palettes are customizable with contrast handling, and wheels are browsed, saved, edited or duplicated in a persistent manager held in the vault.

```cue

    id:     "decision-wheel"
    name:   "Decision Wheel"
    author: "Gowanii"
    repo:   "moonsand79/obsidian-decision-wheel"
```
