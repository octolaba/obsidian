# Study Vault

Courses and exam schedules are recorded together with exam dates, topic counts and subjects. Topic notes are created with sections for summary, key concepts, flashcards, practice questions and related links, and interactive reviews pull question and answer pairs from those notes and track known against unsure. A sidebar shows per-course progress bars and the days remaining until each exam.
