# Solo RPG Toolkit

Dice are rolled from clickable die icons, and cards are drawn from built-in or custom decks defined by image files or URLs, including standard, joker and oracle decks with rotation and flip options. Random words and custom table rolls are generated from templates, aimed at solo tabletop play with a game-master emulator.
