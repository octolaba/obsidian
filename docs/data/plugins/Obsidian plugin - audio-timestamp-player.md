# Audio Timestamp Player

Turns transcript timestamps into playback controls for audio and video embedded in a note. Selecting a timestamp link in minutes-and-seconds or hours-minutes-seconds form, in Reading view, Live Preview or Source mode, jumps to that moment and starts playback, and timestamps can be bound to a specific recording when a note holds several media files. A floating player keeps playback reachable while scrolling or switching views, the common audio and video formats from MP3 to M4V are supported, and everything runs locally with no telemetry or network requests.
