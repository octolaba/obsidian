# Draggable List Items

Reorders list items by dragging them in reading view and live preview, on desktop and mobile. Nested children move with their parent, drags are restricted to matching list types such as tasks, bullets or ordered items, and ordered lists are renumbered after a drop. Only rendered views support dragging.
