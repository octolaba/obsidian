# TNR - Track n Review

Searches TMDB for movies, TV series and people and inserts embed cards at the cursor position, optionally including cast and person photos. Direct season and episode queries in the form Title s01e03 are supported. Track 'n' Review profile exports are read to display ratings, collections and memory statistics.

    id:     "tnrapp-tnr"
