# Constellations

Constellations presents the vault as a 3D star map and an alternative to Graph View: notes are stars, shared tags form constellations, and top-level folders form universes. Flying to a star opens an info card with its tags, a preview and its linked neighbors, filtering by name or tag spotlights the matches, and stars twinkle while comets drift through the scene.
