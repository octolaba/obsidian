# ZettelCasting

Assembles a note into a social post, inlining linked and embedded notes recursively so the post stands on its own, and uploading embedded images and videos. Posts are scheduled to platforms connected through the ZettelCasting service, with optional reflow for social formatting. An exact copy of what was sent is saved beside the source note as a .zcast.md file.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "zettelcasting"
    name:   "ZettelCasting"
    author: "Dayne Daniell"
    repo:   "daynedaniell/zettelcasting-plugin"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/zettelcasting"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/daynedaniell/zettelcasting-plugin"
    description: "Publish and schedule notes from your vault to your connected social platforms via the ZettelCasting service. Linked and embedded notes are pulled in, so the post stands on its own. - This plugin has not been manually reviewed by Obsidian staff."
    about:       "Turn notes into scheduled social posts by assembling content, inlining linked and embedded notes recursively, and uploading embedded media (images/videos). Schedule posts to connected ZettelCasting platforms with optional social-friendly reflow, and save an exact sent copy (.zcast.md) beside the source note."
}
```
