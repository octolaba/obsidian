# LexiBridge

LexiBridge generates structured English and Chinese vocabulary notes from a local ECDICT database and enriches an entry on demand with Youdao pronunciations, definitions and examples. Selected Eudic wordlists sync in both directions and notes export one way to Anki Desktop through AnkiConnect, while the dictionary data itself stays local. Inflected forms resolve to their lemma note, virtual links are non-destructive and show previews, and user-written sections survive a refresh.
