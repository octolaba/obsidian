# TaskNotes Views

Embeds progress charts in TaskNotes project notes, in reading view and Live Preview, plotting cumulative scope, scheduled, started, completed and ideal target progress. Estimates may be durations or unitless values such as timeEstimate or story points, the ideal target pauses on weekends by default, and hover popups show task names with their scope. Project capacity is estimated from recent velocity, and both virtual and materialised recurring TaskNotes occurrences are handled.
