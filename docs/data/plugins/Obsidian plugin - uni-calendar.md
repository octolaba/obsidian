# UniCalendar

Aggregates events from Google Calendar, CalDAV servers and ICS feeds into one colour-coded month view, deduplicating events that arrive from more than one source. Chinese lunar dates, traditional festivals, the 24 solar terms and public holiday overlays can be shown. Sync runs automatically and the view works on mobile.
