# Weave EPUB Reader

Opens EPUB, MOBI, AZW3, FB2, CBZ, TXT and other ebook files inside the vault with a bookshelf, chapter navigation, a remembered reading position, pagination or scroll modes and theme controls. Passages can be highlighted and extracted into Markdown, Canvas or Weave card data with backlinks and deep links pointing at an exact position in the book. Highlights held in notes are shown back in the text, and selections can be exported or snapshotted for review.
