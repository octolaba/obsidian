# Meeting Tools

Creates meeting minutes from template-driven forms, or records a simple date-and-outcome entry through a Quick meeting flow. Entries are appended to a meeting journal, and tasks with assignees and due dates are managed and searched in a dashboard. Notes are stored locally in the vault, the interface is English and Russian, and the recorded description states that no external service is involved.
