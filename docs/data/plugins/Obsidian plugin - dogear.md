# Dogear

A reading tracker that keeps the library in plain Markdown. Books can be searched and imported from Goodreads, progress logged by page, percent or listening time, and the collection browsed as a cover grid, with notes on every book kept in one place.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "dogear"
    name:   "Dogear"
    author: "Masoud Masoumi"
    repo:   "masoudmim/obsidian-dogear"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/dogear"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/masoudmim/obsidian-dogear"
    description: "Track your reading: search for books, log progress by page, percent, or listening time, and keep notes on every book in one place. - This plugin has not been manually reviewed by Obsidian staff."
    about:       "A reading tracker that keeps your library in plain Markdown. Import from Goodreads, log progress by page, percent, or listening time, and browse your books as a cover grid."
```
