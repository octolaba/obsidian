# AI Cloze Review

AI Cloze Review generates cloze deletions from Chinese or English notes in one click, with density adapting to length and difficulty. Review is interactive, keeping answers hidden until a placeholder is clicked and tracking progress, while cached clozes preserve the underlying notes. Several APIs are supported, including on mobile.
