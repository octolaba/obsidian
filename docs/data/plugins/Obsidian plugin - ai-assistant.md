# AI Assistant

AI Assistant chats with OpenAI and Anthropic models or runs prompt-based edits directly from notes, including image uploads for vision models. Images can be generated with DALL-E 2 and 3 and saved into the vault with their paths copied to the clipboard. Audio is transcribed with Whisper into the note at the cursor.
