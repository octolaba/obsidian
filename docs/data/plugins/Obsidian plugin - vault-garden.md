# Vault Garden

Renders the vault as a garden in which every note becomes a procedurally drawn plant, its colour showing how recently the note was edited and its size showing how well it is linked. The view can be panned and zoomed, hovering a plant shows its stats, and dragging one moves the note between folders. Plants can be shed to archive, composted to trash, or watered to refresh without opening the note, and everything runs locally with no backend or telemetry.
