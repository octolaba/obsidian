# Stashpad

Notes are captured in a chat-style composer, stacking and nesting into an outliner that can be drilled into and out of. Subtrees are searched and reorganized by move, merge and clone, notes are pinned and colored, and tasks carry due dates and reminders. The workspace can be popped into a small window, and notes export to Open Knowledge Format for use by language models and agents.
