# Card View Switcher

Card View Switcher is a quick switcher that presents notes as cards to browse and select. It searches in three modes: recent, which reads the contents and filenames of the ten most recently opened files; normal, which searches all filenames behind a quote or leading space; and random, which searches all filenames behind a semicolon and picks ten at random.

    id:     "obsidian-card-view-switcher-plugin"

Search in three modes: recent (search contents and filenames of the 10 most recently opened files), normal (search all filenames; prefix query with ' or a leading space), or random (search all filenames and randomly pick 10; prefix with ;)."
