# Day Echo

Gathers daily notes into a single scrolling vertical timeline of two-column cards, with sticky section labels and a running entry count. Month and year zoom levels and representative-year summaries make longer spans navigable, cards preview tags and lazily loaded thumbnails, and a click opens the day in a modal or in the editor.

    id:     "day-echo"
    name:   "Day Echo"

    github_url:  "https://github.com/tcyeee/obsidian-day-echo"

Scan months or years with month/year zoom and representative-year summaries, preview rich cards with lazy-loaded thumbnails and tags, and open any day in a modal or editor with a click."
