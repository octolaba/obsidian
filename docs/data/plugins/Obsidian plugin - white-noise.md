# White Noise

Plays ambient sounds such as rain, coffee shop noise and gentle static to mask distractions while working. Its volume is controlled separately from any music that is playing, and a built-in Pomodoro timer runs alongside the sounds.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "white-noise"
    name:   "White Noise"
    author: "zhouhua"
    repo:   "zhouhua/obsidian-white-noise"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/white-noise"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/zhouhua/obsidian-white-noise"
    description: "Play white noise to help you focus on your work."
    about:       "Play dozens of ambient sounds like rain, coffee shop noise, and gentle static to mask distractions and promote focus. Control white-noise volume independently alongside your music, and run a built-in Pomodoro timer that pairs with the sounds."
```
