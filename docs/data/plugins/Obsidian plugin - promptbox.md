# Promptbox

Keeps a local library of AI prompts as Markdown notes with YAML frontmatter, held in the vault with no network calls and no account. Prompts are searched, filtered, favorited, and linted, and their placeholders are filled through a form with defaults and context variables such as the selection, title, date, and clipboard. A palette inserts placeholders, a quick picker and a URL launcher open prompts, and the library imports and exports as JSON.
