# Meal Plan

Meal Plan manages recipes in the vault and imports or downloads recipes from the web in the RecipeMD or heading-based formats. Recipes can be searched and filtered by ingredient, assigned to days and collected into a weekly Meal Plan note. A consolidated shopping list is generated from the planned recipes.
