# Mouse Handwriting

Turns a full page into a penmanship sheet for practising handwriting with the mouse, tracing on-screen guides to build control and form. Letters, words and short or long passages are chosen in English, Korean or Japanese. Guide styles switch between outline, filled, ruled or none, and a guide-free image of the writing can be previewed; the recorded text says the practice is not scored.
