# One Step Wiki Link

One Step Wiki Link converts matched text in the open note into wiki links in a single step. All potential matches are shown with a convert button or command, conversion runs manually or automatically, and files or directories can be excluded to avoid unwanted changes.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "one-step-wiki-link"
    name:   "One Step Wiki Link"
    author: "busyogg"
    repo:   "busyogg/OneStepWikiLink"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/one-step-wiki-link"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/busyogg/OneStepWikiLink"
    description: "One step to add wiki links."
    about:       "Convert matched text in the open note into wiki links in one step. Show all potential matches and provide a Convert button or command; support manual or automatic conversion and allow excluding files or directories to avoid unwanted changes."
```
