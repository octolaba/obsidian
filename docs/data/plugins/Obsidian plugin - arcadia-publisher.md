# Arcadia Publisher

Exports notes to print-ready PDF or self-contained HTML from configurable templates with embedded styles. Templates can be customized and whole notes or folders exported in batch. Word, EPUB and LaTeX export and citation formatting in Chicago, APA and MLA styles belong to the premium tier.
