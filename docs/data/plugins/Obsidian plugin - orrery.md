# Orrery

Orrery renders the vault's link graph as an auto-rotating 3D scene where notes become glowing stars and links become connecting threads, lit by bloom against a nebula backdrop and a starfield. Hubs, clusters and isolated notes are explored by hovering to highlight neighbours and clicking a star to open its note. A live orrery can be embedded in any note.
