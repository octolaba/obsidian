# Share to NotionNext

Share to NotionNext pushes files from Obsidian into any Notion database through the Notion API, using a general shape, the NotionNext blog template, or a fully custom schema. Frontmatter keys map to Notion properties, the target database can be switched on the fly, and local images and PDFs referenced by Wikilinks or Markdown links are uploaded. It was originally created as EasyChris/obsidian-to-notion.
