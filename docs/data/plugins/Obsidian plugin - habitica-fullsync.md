# Habitica Full Sync

Imports personal and group Habitica tasks into the vault as Markdown, keeping their notes and tagging dailies, habits and rewards explicitly. Tasks marked complete in Obsidian are scored back to Habitica automatically, due dates are handled with time-zone awareness, and sync behaviour can be bound to a device. Folder selection and automatic sync are among the recorded options.
