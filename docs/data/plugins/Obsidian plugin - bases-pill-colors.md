# Bases Visuals

Adds compact Notion-style pill colors and conditional formatting to Base tables. Colors are assigned automatically and stably, scoped by property and value, or chosen from named presets or a custom hex value and edited by right-clicking. Ordered rules are managed in a searchable settings panel with ellipsis and full-value tooltips, and native and embedded Base tables are supported with soft cell or full-row highlighting and light and dark aware palettes.
