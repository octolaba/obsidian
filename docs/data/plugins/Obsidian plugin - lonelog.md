# Lonelog

Supports solo and group tabletop role-playing journaling by bringing Lonelog notation, including Partylog, into the vault. Lonelog symbols are inserted, campaign and session headers and scene markers generated, and records wrapped in lonelog blocks. Syntax highlighting is live, and side panels cover clocks, NPC threads, scene navigation, combat and dungeon tracking.
