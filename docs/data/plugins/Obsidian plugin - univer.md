# Univer

Creates, edits and views spreadsheets and documents inside the knowledge base. The sheet side offers cell styles, formulas, conditional formatting, data validation and search and replace; the document side covers lists, paragraph layouts, mixed text and images, and multi-column sections. Files in xls, xlsm and xlsx form are read and saved, and the interface switches between English and Chinese.
