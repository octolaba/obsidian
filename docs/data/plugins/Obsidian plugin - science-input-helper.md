# Science Input Helper

Science Input Helper is aimed at Chinese science-course notes and converts frequent expressions into LaTeX or MathJax that Obsidian can render: chemical formulas and ions, reaction arrows with conditions above and below, units, scientific notation, Greek letters, Roman numerals and light math structures such as roots, fractions, brackets, vectors, superscripts and subscripts. It is positioned as a companion to Latex Suite rather than a replacement, and Latex Suite remains recommended for full snippets, matrices, cases and complex LaTeX editing. It does not solve problems, check scientific correctness, balance chemical equations or perform unit conversions, so complex expressions still need manual checking.

The plugin is designed as a practical companion to Latex Suite. Latex Suite is still recommended for full mathematical snippets, matrices, cases, and complex LaTeX editing. Science Input Helper focuses on the small but frequent expressions that often slow down classroom note-taking in chemistry, physics, mathematics, and biochemistry.
