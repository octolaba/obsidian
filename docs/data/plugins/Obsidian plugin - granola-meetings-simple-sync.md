# Granola Meetings Simple Sync

Syncs Granola meeting notes, AI summaries and transcripts into the vault through Granola's MCP API, with a one-time OAuth step and scheduled automatic sync. Templates and frontmatter are customizable, existing notes are skipped so local edits survive, and meetings are deduplicated by meeting ID. Attendees can be auto-linked to person notes.
