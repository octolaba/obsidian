# Desktop Sidebar Gestures

Adds horizontal swipe gestures on the desktop for opening and closing the sidebars. Swiping right closes the right sidebar, or opens the left one when the right is closed and the left has content; swiping left closes the left sidebar or opens the right. Sidebars that are empty or unavailable are not opened.

plugin: {
    id:     "desktop-sidebar-gestures"
    name:   "Desktop Sidebar Gestures"
    author: "Jared"
    repo:   "nth-chile/obsidian-desktop-sidebar-gestures"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/desktop-sidebar-gestures"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/nth-chile/obsidian-desktop-sidebar-gestures"
    description: "Directional desktop swipe gestures for opening and closing Obsidian sidebars. - This plugin has not been manually reviewed by Obsidian staff."
    about:       "Add horizontal desktop swipe gestures to toggle Obsidian sidebars. Swipe right to close the right sidebar or, if closed and the left has content, open the left; swipe left to close the left or open the right, and empty/unavailable sidebars won't open."
