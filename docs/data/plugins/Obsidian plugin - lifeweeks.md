# Life in Weeks

Renders a life-in-weeks grid over the vault's weekly and daily notes, laying out each year as a row of 52 week cells. Clicking a cell opens or creates its note, and a long press switches to the day view. Multi-select, date-range editing and zoom help when working across several weeks.
