# Macros

Tracks food and nutrition in notes: meals, calories, macros and micronutrients are logged, and foods are searched across FatSecret, USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts, including barcode lookup and fuzzy matching. Nutrition Markdown files are generated and reusable meal templates and groups assembled. Interactive macro tables, pie charts and multi-day aggregation chart the diet over time.
