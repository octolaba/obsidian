# Scholar Sidekick - Cite from any identifier

Scholar Sidekick turns an identifier into a formatted citation: a DOI, PMID, ISBN, arXiv ID, ADS bibcode, PMCID or ISSN under the cursor is replaced in place, or a selection is wrapped. Built-in styles and more than 10,000 CSL styles are available, and a note's citations can be exported to BibTeX or RIS without a reference manager.

    id:     "scholar-sidekick"
