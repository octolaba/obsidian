# FleurPilot

An AI writing companion aimed at Chinese-language writing, with inline editing in which selecting text produces a rewrite, the diff is previewed and then applied. Whole-note polishing, proofreading, translation, summaries, and structure and tone analyses are also available. A context-aware chat runs alongside, with preconfigured domestic models including Qwen, GLM and DeepSeek.
