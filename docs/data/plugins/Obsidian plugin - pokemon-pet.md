# Pokemon Pet

Adds a collectible pixel Pokemon companion that wanders the bottom of the Obsidian window, hops onto text or images, and reacts when you type. Wild Pokemon appear by rarity and are caught by throwing a Pokeball, with controls for size, collection, wiki links, and hiding the pet. It works offline from a bundled fallback roster or fetches remote sprites and data, and can show Pomodoro completion notifications.
