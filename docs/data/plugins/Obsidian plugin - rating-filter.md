# Rating Filter

Adds 0-5 star ratings to notes and stores them as a numeric rating property in YAML frontmatter, decimals allowed. Stars are previewed at the top of a document and appear in Properties. A Rating Filter panel filters notes by rating greater than, less than, or equal to a value and opens the results; ratings are stored locally.

    id:     "rating-filter"
    name:   "Rating Filter"
    author: "Niccolò Niccoli"

    github_url:  "https://github.com/niccoloniccoli/obsidian-document-rating-system"

    about:       "Add star ratings to notes and save them as a numeric rating property in YAML frontmatter. Preview stars at the top of documents and set ratings from 0–5 (decimals allowed). Filter notes in the Rating Filter panel by Rating ≥, ≤, or = and open results; ratings are stored locally and show in Properties."
