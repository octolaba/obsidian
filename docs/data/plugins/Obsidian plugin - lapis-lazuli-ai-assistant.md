# Lapis Lazuli AI Assistant

Lapis Lazuli AI Assistant brings context-aware AI chat into the active Markdown note, querying or requesting edits from DeepSeek, OpenAI, Gemini and Claude. Proposed edits are shown as a git-style diff with coloured additions and deletions, and can be accepted or rejected as a whole or hunk by hunk before the inline suggestion is applied.
