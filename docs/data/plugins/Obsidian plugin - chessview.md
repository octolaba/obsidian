# ChessView

ChessView renders interactive chess boards from PGN or FEN notation inside notes. Puzzles can be made solvable with hints and solutions, moves annotated with NAGs, arrows and circles drawn, and moves navigated from the keyboard. A position can be exported to Lichess or Chess.com.
