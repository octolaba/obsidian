# Job Search Tracker

Job Search Tracker records job applications with company, role, location, salary range, source, status, contacts and interview notes. A Kanban pipeline in the sidebar moves applications through their stages, where status and contacts are updated. Interview-prep notes are created automatically with company research, questions to ask and answers to prepare.
