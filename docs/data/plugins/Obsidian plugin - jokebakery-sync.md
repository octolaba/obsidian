# JokeBakery Sync

JokeBakery Sync keeps a JokeBakery account and the vault in step in both directions, converting sprints, jokes and lists into Markdown files under their own folders. Edits made in the vault are pushed back to JokeBakery on the next sync, and structured fields and note sections are preserved. Conflicts are logged into a separate folder, and a JokeBakery account is required.

    id:     "jokebakery-sync"

    about:       "Sync JokeBakery and your Obsidian vault bidirectionally, converting sprints, jokes, and lists into Markdown files under Sprints/, Jokes/, and Lists/. Push edits back to JokeBakery on the next sync, preserve structured fields and note sections, and log conflicts in a Conflicts/ folder."
