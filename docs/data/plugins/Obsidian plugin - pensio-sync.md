# Pensio Journaling Sync

Pensio Journaling Sync sends journal entries from the vault to the Pensio service for emotion and mood tracking, relationship mapping and weekly or monthly reflections. Configured folders are synced selectively with frontmatter-aware entries, and deletions are mirrored. Authentication is per-device, and the recorded inputs state that a Pensio account is required.
