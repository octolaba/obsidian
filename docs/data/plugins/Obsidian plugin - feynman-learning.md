# Feynman Learning

Feynman Learning works a concept through a four-step Feynman Technique workflow in which AI drafts a simple explanation, exposes gaps and refines the understanding. Concepts are extracted and queued, AI quizzes are run, and answers are scored on three dimensions with targeted drills for weak points. Mastery is tracked with spaced repetition and a dashboard, and the plugin syncs to Notion.
