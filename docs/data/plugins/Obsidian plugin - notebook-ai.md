# Knowledge AI

Answers questions about the vault with citations anchored to the exact source chunks that support them, retrieving through a hybrid of BM25, vectors and summaries. Indexing covers Markdown, PDF, DOCX, XLSX and PPTX, with OCR and vision for images, and embeddings run either on device or through a provider. Study guides, timelines, mind maps, FAQs, briefings and slide decks can be generated from any folder.
