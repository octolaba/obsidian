# WakaTime

Usage activity in Obsidian is tracked automatically and turned into time and metrics data. The resulting usage and time-tracking reports are viewed on the WakaTime dashboard.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "obsidian-wakatime"
    name:   "WakaTime"
    author: "wakatime"
    repo:   "wakatime/obsidian-wakatime"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/obsidian-wakatime"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/wakatime/obsidian-wakatime"
    description: "Automatic time tracking and metrics generated from your usage activity."
    about:       "Track time and activity in Obsidian to generate automatic metrics and insights from your note-taking sessions. View detailed usage and time-tracking reports on your WakaTime dashboard."

        downloads:  13870
```
