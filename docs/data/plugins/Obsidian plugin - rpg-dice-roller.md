# RPG Dice Roller

RPG Dice Roller builds RPG dice formulas in a side-panel formula builder covering d20 grammar, advantage and disadvantage, reroll and explode, and keep or drop operations. It generates commands formatted for Discord or Roll20, and can copy them or insert clickable rolls into notes. Formulas in open files are detected automatically.
