# Uncertainty Calculator

Propagates measurement uncertainty through spreadsheet-style calc blocks in a note. Measured quantities are entered with a plus-minus value, correlations and significant figures are tracked, and expanded uncertainty and an uncertainty budget are computed. Monte Carlo propagation is available for full distribution results, and the plugin states that it follows the GUM and its Supplement 1.
