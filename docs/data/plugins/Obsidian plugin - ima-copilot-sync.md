# ima.copilot Sync

ima.copilot Sync copies Tencent IMA personal notes and knowledge-base entries into the vault as Markdown. Images and attachments are downloaded locally, sync is one-way from IMA to Obsidian and runs incrementally or on a schedule, and webpages, WeChat articles and documents are converted to Markdown or to AI-generated summaries.
