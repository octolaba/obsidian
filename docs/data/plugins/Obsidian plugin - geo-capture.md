# Geo Capture

Captures places inside notes for travel journals, check-ins, restaurant reviews and photo-based memories. Device coordinates can be inserted, nearby places searched through Google Places or OpenStreetMap, map links parsed, and image EXIF GPS read, producing Markdown-ready snippets with Google, Apple and OSM links. The recorded text states that it works on desktop and mobile and includes no telemetry.
