# Groove KB

Builds a connected music knowledge base by turning artists, albums, editions and collections into interlinked notes with frontmatter and a maintained registry. Artists can be searched and previewed, albums and editions imported and expanded with covers, tracklists and summaries, and items organized into bundles and collections while the links are kept up to date.
