# Sheet Music

Sheet Music renders ABC notation, strumming patterns and chord sheets inside notes as playable music notation. ABC scores play back and transpose with tempo control and custom render options, strumming and chord playback animate, chord and section markers highlight, and note autoscroll can be toggled.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "sheet-music"
    name:   "Sheet Music"
    author: "corecube"
    repo:   "corecube/obsidian-sheet-music"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/sheet-music"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/corecube/obsidian-sheet-music"
    description: "Render ABC notation, strumming patterns, and chord sheets inside notes. - This plugin has not been manually reviewed by Obsidian staff."
    about:       "Render playable music notation directly in notes using ABC, strumming patterns, and chord sheets. Play and transpose ABC with tempo control and custom render options, animate strumming and chord playback, highlight chord/section markers, and toggle note autoscroll."
```
