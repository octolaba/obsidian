# Notekeeper

Presents the vault as a wall of sticky notes in a masonry or compact card grid, in the style of Google Keep, where notes can be captured, pinned, colored and archived. Quick capture, inline editing with Markdown preview, multi-select bulk actions, drag-to-order pins, filters by label, live checkboxes and image thumbnails are provided.
