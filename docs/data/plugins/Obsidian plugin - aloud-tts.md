# Aloud

Plays highlighted text from notes with voices from OpenAI, Google Gemini, ElevenLabs, Azure, AWS Polly, Hume, MiniMax or a custom OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Audio streams sentence by sentence with the active sentence highlighted and the speed adjustable, and is cached locally to reduce cost. Clipboard playback, quick export or embedding, and operating-system media controls are supported.
