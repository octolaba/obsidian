# AI Idea Capture

AI Idea Capture takes ideas from Telegram or Discord as text, voice, images or links and converts them with AI into structured Markdown notes, drawing on language, speech-to-text, vision and web-search models. Sessions are archived automatically after inactivity and their context resumed later. Keys and data stay local and AES-256 encrypted, while content is sent to the AI providers you choose.
