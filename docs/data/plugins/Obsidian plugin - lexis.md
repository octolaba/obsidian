# Lexis

Lexis treats the note titles in a folder, together with their aliases, as a word bank, and creates word notes from selected text. Bank words are highlighted in reading and preview, definitions appear on hover, and example occurrences are collected automatically. FSRS spaced-repetition cards run in word-to-context and cloze form with progress saved to frontmatter, alongside bidirectional relations for synonyms, roots and look-alikes and a review heatmap.
