# Coach

Tracks fitness and workouts in the vault as plain Markdown, with routines built, sets and meals logged, and body measurements and progress recorded over time. Break timers, supersets, drop sets and sets to failure are handled, session duration is tracked automatically, personal records are badged as they are set, and analytics summarize the history.

    id:     "coach"
    name:   "Coach"
    author: "Matthew Newton"
    repo:   "ekrizdis367/obsidian-coach"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/coach"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/ekrizdis367/obsidian-coach"
    description: "An in-depth fitness and workout tracking system for your vault. - This plugin has not been manually reviewed by Obsidian staff."
