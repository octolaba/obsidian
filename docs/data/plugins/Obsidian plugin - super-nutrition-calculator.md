# Super Nutrition Calculator

Foods, ingredients and recipes are looked up across Boohee, USDA FoodData Central, Chinese food databases and local datasets, either as a single item or aggregated over several ingredients. Totals are compared against more than 30 dietary reference intake profiles, AI-assisted field mapping reconciles matches between sources, and results are inserted into notes as Markdown.
