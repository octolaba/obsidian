# Tasting Notes

Tasting Notes logs wine, whisky, beer and other tastings with producer, vintage, nose, palate, finish, rating and cellar status. Wine-specific fields such as colour and grape varieties and whisky details such as distillery, age, ABV and cask are recorded, and bottles can be marked as in the cellar with a quantity and a purchase date. A tasting sidebar browses the notes sorted by rating, with tabs for all, wine and whisky.

plugin: {
    id:     "tasting-notes"
    name:   "Tasting Notes"
    author: "wesswart77"
    repo:   "wesswart77/obsidian-tasting-notes"

    about:       "Log wine, whisky, beer and other tasting notes with producer, vintage, nose, palate, finish, ratings and cellar status. Record wine-specific fields (colour, grape varieties) and whisky details (distillery, age, ABV, cask), mark bottles as in cellar with quantity and purchase date, and browse notes in a rating-sorted tasting sidebar with All/Wine/Whisky tabs."
