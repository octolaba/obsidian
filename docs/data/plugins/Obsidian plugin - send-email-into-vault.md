# Send Email into Vault

Send Email into Vault converts mail sent to a unique ingestion address, together with its attachments, into a formatted Markdown note saved into the vault. Sync can be triggered manually, on a schedule or when the vault opens, new notes never overwrite existing files, and the folder for attachments is configurable. It works on desktop and mobile, and the recorded description states that emails are deleted after 72 hours and are never sold, shared or used for AI training, with no vault telemetry.
