# Weekly Meal Shopper

Standardises recipe notes and parses ingredient amounts into a consistent, machine-readable form. A weekly meal-prep canvas is created from bundled editable templates, recipe file cards are added to it, and a categorised shopping list is generated from the plan. Recipes can also be transcribed from URLs, videos or folders of images.
