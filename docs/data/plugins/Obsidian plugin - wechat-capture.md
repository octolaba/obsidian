# Wechat Capture

Extracts the full text of a WeChat Official Account article and saves it into the vault as a Markdown note, keeping title, author, body and images. Article content and attachments are stored locally so they can be read and annotated offline.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "wechat-capture"
    name:   "Wechat Capture"
    author: "lyxdream"
    repo:   "lyxdream/obsidian-wechat-capture"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/wechat-capture"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/lyxdream/obsidian-wechat-capture"
    description: "Extract the full text of WeChat Official Account articles and save them as Markdown notes. - This plugin has not been manually reviewed by Obsidian staff."
    about:       "Capture WeChat public account articles into your vault as Markdown, extracting title, author, body and images. Save article content and attachments locally for offline reading and note-taking."
```
