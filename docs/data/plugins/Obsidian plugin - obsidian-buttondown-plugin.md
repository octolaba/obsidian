# Buttondown

Buttondown sends a note to a buttondown.email account as an email draft with a single command. The note filename becomes the email subject and the note content becomes the body, and images referenced by the note are uploaded through the Buttondown API.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "obsidian-buttondown-plugin"
    name:   "Buttondown"
    author: "caro401"
    repo:   "caro401/obsidian-buttondown"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/obsidian-buttondown-plugin"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/caro401/obsidian-buttondown"
    description: "Send your notes to your buttondown.email account as email drafts."
    about:       "Create Buttondown newsletter drafts from Obsidian notes with a single command. Use the note filename as the email subject, the note content as the email body, and upload referenced images to Buttondown via its API."

        downloads:  4236

}
```
