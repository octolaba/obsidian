# Simple CanvaSearch

Simple CanvaSearch fuzzy-searches the notes and cards of the currently opened canvas and shifts focus to a match. A modal locates nodes by title, or by their note contents for deeper matches, and navigates to them within the canvas. Including contents is best avoided on very large canvases, where it slows the search down.

    id:     "simple-canvasearch"
    name:   "Simple CanvaSearch"
    author: "ddalexb"
    repo:   "ddalexb/obsidian-simple-canvasearch"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/simple-canvasearch"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/ddalexb/obsidian-simple-canvasearch"
