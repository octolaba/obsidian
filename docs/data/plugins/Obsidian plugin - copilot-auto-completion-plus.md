# Copilot Auto Completion Plus

Copilot Auto Completion Plus shows transparent inline fill-in-the-middle suggestions based on the text before and after the cursor. Completions are accepted with Tab or the right arrow and dismissed with Escape or by moving the cursor, and they are context- and language-aware for prose, code and math. Suggestions can be produced by OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Azure OpenAI or Ollama.
