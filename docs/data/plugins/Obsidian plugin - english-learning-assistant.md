# English Learning Assistant

Checks English writing for grammar, spelling and punctuation with AI-driven feedback and rewrite suggestions. Words are looked up offline from ECDICT or CSV data, shadowing practice aligns typing in real time with grammar-aware highlighting, and sentence structure is drawn as Mermaid trees. Writing progress is tracked, and vocabulary, grammar notes and mistake logs are saved automatically.
