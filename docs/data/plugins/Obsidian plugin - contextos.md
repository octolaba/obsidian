# ContextOS

ContextOS maintains a git-backed wiki by ingesting and indexing raw notes and documents in more than fifteen formats, extracting text from PDFs, Office files and images with OCR where needed. Text, chat and vision requests are routed to several providers, and the plugin generates structured change plans and lints the result. A side panel streams Markdown chat against the wiki.
