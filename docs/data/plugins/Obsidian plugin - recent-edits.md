# Recent Edits

Lists files modified in the last N days in a sidebar panel, grouped by calendar day and sorted newest first. Edits that came from filesystem writes outside the app are marked with an indicator dot. Noisy folders can be hidden by a background toggle or by exclusions, absolute paths copied, and entries dismissed until their next edit; pages preview on hover, and .md, .canvas, and .base files are included.
