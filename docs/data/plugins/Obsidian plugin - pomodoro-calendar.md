# Pomodoro Calendar

Runs a Pomodoro timer with an animated progress bar in themed styles such as coins, leaves, tomatoes, stars, and hearts. Completed sessions are synced to CalDAV calendars including iCloud, Google, and Fastmail as two-way calendar events, and timer state is synced across devices with play, pause, skip, and complete actions.
