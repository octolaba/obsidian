# MDToSM

Publishes notes to a WeChat official account as articles or image posts, from a compact right-side workspace that previews the note and converts Markdown into WeChat-friendly HTML. Drafts are created or posts submitted for publishing, local images are uploaded to the WeChat CDN when a draft is saved, and frontmatter supplies the metadata. Multiple accounts are supported, and single or sliced PNG images are exported into the vault or into WeChat drafts.
