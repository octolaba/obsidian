# Stonks

Live stock, ETF and crypto prices appear inline as a ticker is typed, refreshing in both editing and reading views and working on mobile. Expressions introduced by a dollar sign and colon embed a raw price or a field such as percentage change, and simple arithmetic combines tickers and note variables for portfolio figures or for use from Dataview and JavaScript. The recorded inputs state that quotes are fetched from Yahoo Finance.
