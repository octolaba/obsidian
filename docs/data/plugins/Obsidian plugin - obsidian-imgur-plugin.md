# Imgur

The plugin uploads images to Imgur instead of storing them in the vault. Images are pasted from the clipboard or dragged in, animated GIFs included, and uploads are either anonymous or bound to an Imgur account.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "obsidian-imgur-plugin"
    name:   "Imgur"
    author: "gavvvr"
    repo:   "gavvvr/obsidian-imgur-plugin"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/obsidian-imgur-plugin"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/gavvvr/obsidian-imgur-plugin"
    description: "Upload images from your clipboard to imgur.com and embeds uploaded image to your note."
    about:       "Upload images to Imgur instead of storing them in your vault. Paste from the clipboard or drag-and-drop files (including animated GIFs), and choose anonymous uploads or your Imgur account to shrink vault size and simplify sharing."

    stats: {
        downloads:  60985
        updated_at: 1730591262000
    }
}
```
