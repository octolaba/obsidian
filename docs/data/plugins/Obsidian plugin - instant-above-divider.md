# Instant Above Divider

Instant Above Divider inserts a divider line at the beginning of the current note and moves the cursor accordingly, optionally placing the divider after frontmatter or after a heading. It also offers a document outline listing headings and dividers, showing the first characters that follow each divider, where clicking an entry jumps to that section.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "instant-above-divider"
    name:   "Instant Above Divider"
    author: "sedationh"
    repo:   "sedationh/obsidian-instant-above-divider"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/instant-above-divider"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/sedationh/obsidian-instant-above-divider"
    description: "Quickly insert a divider line at the beginning of your note."
    about:       "Insert a divider line (---) at the start of the current note, automatically moving the cursor and placing the divider after frontmatter or after a heading when configured. View a document outline listing headings and dividers (showing the first N characters after dividers) and click items to jump to sections."
```
