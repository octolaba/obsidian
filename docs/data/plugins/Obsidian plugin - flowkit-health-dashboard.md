# FlowKit Health Dashboard

Scores every installed community plugin on quality, maintenance, performance, popularity and compatibility, and blends the five signals into one overall score. Vault-wide averages and at-risk counts sit next to maintained, aging or unmaintained badges and update or sideloaded flags. The list is searched, filtered and sorted, and each score explains itself on hover.

    id:     "flowkit-health-dashboard"

Show maintained/aging/unmaintained badges, update and sideloaded flags, and provide search, filter, sort and hoverable score explanations."
