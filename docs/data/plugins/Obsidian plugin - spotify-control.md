# Spotify Control

Spotify playback is driven from a now-playing sidebar with album art, hover transport controls, a seek bar, volume and a device picker. Time-synced lyrics come from LRCLIB with clickable timestamps, the upcoming queue is browsed and jumped, and tracks, albums and playlists are searched from inside Obsidian. Track metadata or a Spotify URI is inserted into a note, and the recorded inputs state that display works on a free account while playback control requires Premium.
