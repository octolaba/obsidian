# Japanese Novel Ruby

Japanese Novel Ruby handles ruby, or furigana, marks in the syntax common to Japanese novels on Aozora Bunko and Shosetsuka. Ruby can be inserted or removed and is shown with live preview, with a hidden-on-hover toggle, changeable delimiters and per-note activation. The interface is available in English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese.
