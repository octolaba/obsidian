# Slide and Reveal

Slide and Reveal turns labeled diagrams and images into scroll-to-reveal study material: covers are drawn over the labels, and scrolling inside the image uncovers them one group at a time. A quiz mode crops fragments from a folder of diagrams, shuffles them and asks for identification out of context. It works per folder, and its recorded About text states that no slide or image duplication is needed.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "slide-and-reveal"
    name:   "Slide and Reveal"
    author: "Grub Basket"
    repo:   "grub-basket/SnR"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/slide-and-reveal"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/grub-basket/SnR"
    description: "Scroll-to-reveal flashcard study for diagrams/images. Right-click any folder to open it as a Slide & Reveal view: drop covers over each label, then scroll in place to uncover them one pair at a time. - This plugin has not been manually reviewed by Obsidian staff."
    about:       "Study labeled images by drawing covers over diagram labels and scrolling inside the image to progressively reveal grouped labels. Quiz across diagrams by cropping fragments from folders, shuffling them, and prompting identification out of context. Per-folder, no slide/image duplication needed, unlike in PowerPoint."
```
