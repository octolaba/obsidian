# Nuance - Excalidraw Pen Sound

Plays paper-writing sounds while drawing on an Excalidraw canvas, giving audible feedback as the stroke is made. A Pencil, Fountain Pen or Marker profile can be chosen, volume and pitch adjusted, and the sounds previewed in the settings.
