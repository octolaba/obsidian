# Subscription Calculator

Recurring subscriptions are recorded with a name, price, currency, billing period and icon, and are toggled between active and paused instead of being deleted. A dedicated view manages the entries and reports yearly totals and approximate monthly cost per currency, with favicons cached. The recorded inputs state that the data stays local.
