# Count Words and Time

Tracks a writing session and reports typed units as characters, Chinese characters or English words, along with writing time, idle time, total time and a focus rate shown as a progress bar. Average speed is computed over a 60-second sliding window and reported per minute or per hour. A sidebar holds the display and offers pause, resume and start-new-session controls on desktop and mobile.
