# Radial timeline

Arranges the scenes of a longform writing project in a radial layout by act, subplot and narrative or chronological order, with concentric rings standing for subplots and hover revealing scene details and AI story pulses. Progress, Narrative, Chronological and Gossamer trace views compare structure, progress and revision state across the project, while Inquiry View investigates the manuscript and the wider saga corpus and generates briefings from the findings. The manuscript exports as Markdown or PDF, and spoiler-free timeline snapshots are shared on social media or for a Kickstarter campaign.
