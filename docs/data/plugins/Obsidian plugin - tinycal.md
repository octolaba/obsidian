# tinyCal

Tracks ongoing work as open-ended calendar events that keep extending to the current day until they are explicitly ended. Events are linked to notes through frontmatter, can be created from a day in the calendar or from an open note, and take their color from a tag. The calendar switches between month and week views, and all data stays in the vault.
