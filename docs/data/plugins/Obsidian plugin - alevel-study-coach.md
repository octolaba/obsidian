# A-Level Study Coach

Supports A-Level and IELTS study with per-subject AI coaching that records every help session, builds concept maps, tracks errors and weak points, and archives completed reviews automatically. Practice problems and writing and speaking exercises are corrected by the model, with automatic follow-ups and spaced repetition of expressions. Review reminders and weekly progress reports are produced, and all data is kept inside the vault.
