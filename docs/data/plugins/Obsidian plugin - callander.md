# Callander

Keeps a note page per friend and sorts jotted ideas into five categories: Gifts, Conversations, Activities, Places and Other. Ideas can be captured from anywhere and checked off once done, and meetups are logged on a timeline. Entries are resurfaced when they become relevant again.
