# Cockpit Board

Builds a Kanban board out of frontmatter properties, sorting notes into columns automatically and writing changes back to the files as cards are dragged, including multi-select bulk moves. The same data is also shown as week, month or year calendars, timers record their time into frontmatter, and cron expressions drive recurring tasks. Checklists are edited in a drag-and-drop modal, archived completions stay searchable, and privacy and focus modes and mobile gestures are available.
