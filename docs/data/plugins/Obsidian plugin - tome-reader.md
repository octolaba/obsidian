# Tome Reader

Reads EPUB books in a native, mobile-friendly Obsidian tab with theme and typography controls and a searchable table of contents carrying pinned bookmarks and chapter auto-scroll. Quotes and notes are captured into the vault, vocabulary entries are saved in a flashcard-ready form, passages can be pinned, and per-book progress resumes with spoiler-safe recaps. An AI key can optionally be supplied for translations and recaps.
