# Highlighter Plus

Highlighter Plus defines custom highlighters and underlines, each with its own colour, underline style, transparency and separate light and dark appearance. Highlights are applied with an extended ==id=Your text== syntax, or cycled with a hotkey that is context aware and targets only the relevant lines. The native highlighter and PDF export compatibility are stated to be kept.
