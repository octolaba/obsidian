# Markdown Relay

Publishes Obsidian Markdown notes one way to Feishu/Lark, Notion and ima, leaving the local files as the single source of truth. Notes and destination targets are selected in batches, destinations are validated before anything is sent, and progress is tracked with cancel and retry for individual tasks. Edits made on the remote side are prevented from merging back into the vault.
