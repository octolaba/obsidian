# Content OS

Content OS posts to LinkedIn directly from the vault through an in-Obsidian composer modal. The composer carries a live character counter that respects LinkedIn's 3000-character limit, and authentication goes through OAuth.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "content-os"
    name:   "Content OS"
    author: "eharris128"
    repo:   "eharris128/obsidian-content-os"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/content-os"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/eharris128/obsidian-content-os"
    description: "Post directly to LinkedIn from your vault."
    about:       "Post to LinkedIn directly from your vault using a simple in-Obsidian composer modal. Compose and publish posts with a live character counter that respects LinkedIn's 3000-character limit, and authenticate securely via OAuth."
```
