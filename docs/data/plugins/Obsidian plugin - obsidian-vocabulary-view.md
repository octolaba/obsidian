# Vocabulary View

Words and their explanations are written in vocaview blocks and previewed as lists, single-choice questions or flashcard-style cards. The preview can show the word, show the explanation, or randomize the order. Reusable vocabulary books collect words for adding, removing and reviewing across notes.
