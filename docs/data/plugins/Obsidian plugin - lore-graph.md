# Lore Graph

Hides whole folders from the graph view and reveals their notes on demand by clicking a related node. Hovering a node previews its hidden links, clicking pins a reveal or cascades into linked hidden notes, and the original node positions and the native rendering are preserved for revealed items.

    id:     "lore-graph"
    name:   "Lore Graph"
    author: "Pierre Bisiaux"

Hover a node to preview its hidden links, click to pin a reveal or cascade into linked hidden notes, and preserve original node positions and native rendering for revealed items."
