# AI Linzi

Connects the AI Linzi service to the vault through a streaming sidebar chat that uses the current note as context and shows clickable links to its sources. Markdown, text, PDF and DOCX files are searched and their content extracted locally, and long-document and transcript jobs can be resumed. Images are generated, results are collected across several notes, and output is saved back as notes.
