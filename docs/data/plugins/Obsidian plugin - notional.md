# Notional

Pushes formatted pages from Obsidian to Notion in one click, turning wiki-links into Notion page mentions and preserving nested structure, and pulls changes back the other way. Push and Pull are conflict-aware and, according to the recorded description, never overwrite silently; connecting is one click and keeps credentials on the device. A dockable sync panel tracks changes per side, resolves conflicts explicitly, uploads folders, and can sync automatically.
