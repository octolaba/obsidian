# Time Ruler

Time Ruler combines a task list and a calendar view into a time ruler on which nested tasks are time-blocked and dragged to schedule, reschedule or change their duration. Tasks are shown over read-only online calendars, created by dragging, and filtered by scheduled, unscheduled or due. It integrates with Dataview, Tasks, FullCalendar and Day Planner, and its built-in timer covers Pomodoro and time tracking.
