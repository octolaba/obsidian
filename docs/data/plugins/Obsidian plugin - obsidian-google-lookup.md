# Google Calendar and Contacts Lookup

Inserts Google Calendar events and Google Contacts, both personal and Workspace Directory entries, into notes to populate meeting metadata and contact profiles. Events and people are looked up through suggestion modals, several Google accounts are supported, and the inserted content is shaped by customizable templates.
