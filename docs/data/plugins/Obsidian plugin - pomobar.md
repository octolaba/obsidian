# PomoBar

Puts a minimal Pomodoro timer in the status bar that shows session state and swaps its icon while running. Left click starts and stops it, right click resets it when paused, and middle click cycles between 25, 15, and 5 minute durations. Optional audio alerts, automatic progression through work and break cycles, and assignable keyboard commands are also available.
