# Calendar of Notes

Presents a compact calendar that indexes notes by date, taken from filenames, from frontmatter, or from both. It appears in the sidebar on desktop and in a drawer on mobile, in month, week or year view, with activity dots marking days that have notes. Choosing a day opens the matching note directly, or lists all matches without leaving the calendar.
