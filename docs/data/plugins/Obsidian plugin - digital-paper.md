# digital paper

Disables backspace, delete and cut so that typed text cannot be erased, in the manner of writing with a pen on real paper. Paper mode is toggled on or off, and the status bar shows paper while it is active.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "digital-paper"
    name:   "digital paper"
    author: "Daniel Fernandes"
    repo:   "danferns/digital-paper-obsidian-plugin"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/digital-paper"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/danferns/digital-paper-obsidian-plugin"
    description: "turn off backspace and undo, just like writing with a pen on real paper."
    about:       "Disable backspace, delete and cut actions to prevent accidental erasure and keep everything you type. Toggle paper mode to turn the behavior on or off; the status bar displays \"paper\" when active."

    stats: {
        downloads:  4842

}
```
