# Banshan Habits Tracker

Banshan Habits Tracker records habits through interactive daily toggles and shows them in Today, Month and Year views, the latter two as GitHub-style heatmaps. Trackers are stored as readable Markdown notes, habits are scheduled by frequency or weekday, streaks are counted strictly or with forgiveness, per-habit history is available, and cards are reordered by dragging.
