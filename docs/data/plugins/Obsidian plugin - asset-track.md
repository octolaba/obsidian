# Asset Track

Turns monthly bill and statement exports into a personal financial record, with rules handling the repeated cleanup of imported rows. Transactions, accounts, loans, investments and assets are kept in one local SQLite source of truth, and reconciliation checks that the recorded cash flows explain how the assets changed. The workflow is monthly settlement rather than daily bookkeeping, and it runs locally on Obsidian desktop.
