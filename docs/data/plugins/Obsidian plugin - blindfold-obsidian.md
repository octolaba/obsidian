# BlindFold

BlindFold hides sections of a note behind a collapsible block so that long or distracting passages stay out of view. Clicking the block reveals its full content, including LaTeX and other Obsidian elements.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "blindfold-obsidian"
    name:   "BlindFold"
    author: "vzsky"
    repo:   "vzsky/blindfold-obsidian"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/blindfold-obsidian"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/vzsky/blindfold-obsidian"
    description: "Fold text by making it completely hidden."
    about:       "Hide long or distracting sections behind a collapsible blindfold block to keep documents concise and readable. Reveal content with a click to view full details, including LaTeX and other Obsidian elements."
```
