# Tegaki

Tegaki converts photographs of handwriting, whiteboards and slides into searchable notes by sending the images to AI vision APIs from Google or Anthropic. Printed text is captured with its layout and handwritten annotations with their position, including circles, arrows and margin notes. The full OCR result can be appended to the current note, or the handwriting extracted on its own. The description states plainly that images are sent to those external services.
