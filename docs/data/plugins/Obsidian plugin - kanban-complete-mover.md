# Kanban Complete Mover

Moves a Kanban card to a Complete lane as soon as its checkbox is ticked, creating that lane when none exists. Unchecking a card restores it to its origin, while a card dragged out of Complete is unchecked instead of being forced back. A completion date and time can optionally be stamped on the card.
