# Odaily

Odaily opens a schedule-focused home for capturing ideas and managing notes and tasks, with quick note, new docs, todo list, quick switcher and recent files. Navigation runs by day, week and month, memos are recorded with timestamps, and open and completed tasks are filtered by tag. Per-theme backgrounds and light or dark mode can be toggled.
