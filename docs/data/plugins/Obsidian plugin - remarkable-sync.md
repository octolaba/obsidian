# reMarkable Sync

Syncs reMarkable Paper Pro documents into the vault as PDFs, preserving nine pen types, fourteen colors, layered drawings, and text styles. Incremental or automatic syncing keeps the notes current, annotations are merged into the original PDF background, and extended pages are rendered correctly.
