# Ripple

Ripple keeps a micro-journal in a single vault folder, which holds timestamped Markdown posts with simple frontmatter and threaded replies. The feed reads newest-first and offers an in-feed composer, highlights, month navigation and optional AI reflections through the AI Providers plugin. The recorded text says it works on Obsidian mobile and names Pile as its inspiration.
