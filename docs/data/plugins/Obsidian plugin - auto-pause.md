# AutoPause

AutoPause allows one audio track to play at a time by pausing whatever is already playing when a new clip starts, with an option to reset it to the beginning instead. Global hotkeys play or pause and jump to the next or previous audio across tabs and documents, on desktop and mobile.

    id:     "auto-pause"
    name:   "AutoPause"
    author: "ckep1"
    repo:   "ckep1/obsidian-autopause"

    github_url:  "https://github.com/ckep1/obsidian-autopause"
