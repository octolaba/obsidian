# Tarot Practice

Tarot Practice draws single or multiple cards for daily practice, seeding the randomness with the words of an intention and the exact moment of the draw. Several decks are managed, including tarot, runes, Lenormand, I Ching, playing cards and custom ones, with card images displayed. Spreads can be built, customized and shared, draw history is tracked with statistics, and readings export as JSON or CSV, with mobile support.
