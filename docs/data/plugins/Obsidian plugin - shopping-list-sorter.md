# Unified Shopping List Sorter

Unified Shopping List Sorter inserts and maintains managed shopping-list blocks in notes, sorting them by checkbox status, configured store keywords and item name. Unchecked items stay above checked ones, items group by store keyword and sort A to Z with German-aware collation, and lists re-sort after edits with a configurable delay. Management markers are hidden in the editor, and ordinary checkboxes elsewhere are left alone.
