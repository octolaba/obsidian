# Layout Switcher (UA-EN)

Converts selected text between English QWERTY and Ukrainian keyboard layouts, for text typed with the wrong layout active. The direction can be detected automatically or forced to English or Ukrainian, and the conversion applies either to a selection or to the word under the cursor.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "layout-switcher-uk-en"
    name:   "Layout Switcher (UA-EN)"
    author: "kosperun"
    repo:   "kosperun/obsidian-layout-switcher-uk-en"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/layout-switcher-uk-en"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/kosperun/obsidian-layout-switcher-uk-en"
    description: "Convert selected text between English and Ukrainian keyboard layouts, for when you forgot to switch layout before typing - This plugin has not been manually reviewed by Obsidian staff."
    about:       "Convert selected text between English (QWERTY) and Ukrainian keyboard layouts when you typed with the wrong layout. Auto-detect direction or force English/Ukrainian conversion and operate on a selection or the word under the cursor."
```
