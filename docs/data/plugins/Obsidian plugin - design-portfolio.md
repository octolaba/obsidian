# Design Portfolio

Keeps a design portfolio and inspiration material in plain Markdown as case studies, mood boards and client work logs. Case studies carry Brief, Role, Process, Outcome, Learnings and Assets sections, mood boards are generated as image grids, and a dedicated sidebar browses projects and inspiration by year or by tag.

plugin: {
    id:     "design-portfolio"
    name:   "Design Portfolio"
    author: "wesswart77"
    repo:   "wesswart77/obsidian-design-portfolio"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/design-portfolio"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/wesswart77/obsidian-design-portfolio"

Create case studies with Brief, Role, Process, Outcome, Learnings and Assets, generate image-grid mood boards, and browse projects and inspiration by year or tag in a dedicated sidebar."
