# Occura

Every occurrence of a double-clicked word or a text selection is highlighted and counted, in the manner of Notepad++ or an IDE. Colored word classes, case-insensitive selection and automatic keyword highlighting are configurable, occurrences can be converted to tags, and highlights can be made permanent.
