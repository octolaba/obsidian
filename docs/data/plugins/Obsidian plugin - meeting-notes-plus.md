# Meeting Notes Plus

Creates structured meeting notes from a New Meeting modal capturing title, date, attendees, type and agenda. Each note carries frontmatter plus Agenda, Notes, Decisions and Action Items sections, where action items are checkboxes marked with an owner. Unchecked actions are extracted into a master Actions note, and a sidebar lists recent meetings with their dates, attendee counts and action counts.

    id:     "meeting-notes-plus"
    name:   "Meeting Notes Plus"
    author: "wesswart77"
