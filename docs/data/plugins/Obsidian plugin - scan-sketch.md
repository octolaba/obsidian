# Scan Sketch

Turns photos of handwritten notes into clean images, applying automatic perspective correction with interactive crop points. Backgrounds can be removed to produce transparent PNGs, and brightness, contrast, saturation and black-and-white filters are applied in real time. The result is exported into the vault as PNG or SVG.
