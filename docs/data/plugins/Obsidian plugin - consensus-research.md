# Parallax

Parallax supports the thinking that precedes a literature search: assumptions, theoretical lenses, challenges to a framing and new questions are built up and tagged qualitative, quantitative or mixed. Revising a section branches a new note instead of overwriting, so an earlier line of reasoning is never lost, and one question can be worked through several lenses at once. Evidence is searched through OpenAlex, with Semantic Scholar and Consensus optional, and lands as graded syntheses whose citations stay traceable to your own bibliography and linked to literature notes. The argument can then be mapped on a Canvas, turned into an interview guide, exported as a coding starter kit for Quadro, and kept as a deterministic account, with artefacts in 13 or more languages and AI suggestions offered as proposals you adopt item by item.
