# Cards4Links

Cards4Links turns pasted URLs into card-styled links carrying title, description, thumbnail, favicon and host, fetching Open Graph metadata for the preview. Layouts can be Card, Compact or Minimal, thumbnails repositioned or hidden, and a group of links shown as a carousel. Videos can be marked Watched and articles marked Read.

    id:     "cards-for-links"
    name:   "Cards4Links"
    author: "Matthew"
    repo:   "mats324/cards4links"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/cards-for-links"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/mats324/cards4links"

Auto-fetch Open Graph metadata for instant previews, choose Card/Compact/Minimal layouts, position or hide thumbnails, mark videos as Watched and articles as Read, and group cards."
