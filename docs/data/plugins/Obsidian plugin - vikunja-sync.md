# Vikunja Sync

Vikunja Sync keeps tasks in step between Obsidian and a Vikunja server, covering tasks, tags, due dates, priorities and completion status. Obsidian is treated as the source of truth and wins on conflicts, and the design targets single-user setups. Syncing runs manually or automatically.
