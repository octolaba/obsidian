# AI Research Assistant

A prompt-engineering utility for building and refining prompts and ChatGPT conversations inside Obsidian. Conversations are archived to searchable, taggable notes, and the memory context can be edited live, with messages marked Core, Remembered or Forgotten and models swapped mid-conversation to compare outputs. Conversation summaries and the raw inputs and outputs stay readable in the notes.
