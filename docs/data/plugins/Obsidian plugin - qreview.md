# Quick Review

Reviews and polishes a note with several configurable agents that check facts, improve wording, suggest expansions, link related concepts and restructure content. A review runs over a selection or over the whole note, and the results appear in a detachable panel with threaded follow-ups and one-click edits applied back into the editor.
