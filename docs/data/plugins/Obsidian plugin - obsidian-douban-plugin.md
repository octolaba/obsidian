# Douban

Douban imports movies, books, music, TV shows, diaries, notes and games from Douban into Obsidian, together with ratings, watch and read dates, comments, tags and cover images. Personal watch, read, listen and play histories can be synced, covers saved locally or to an image host, and timelines or Douban-style pages built from the imported data.
