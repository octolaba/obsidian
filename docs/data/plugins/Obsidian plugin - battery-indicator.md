# Battery Indicator

Battery Indicator shows the current battery level in Obsidian's status bar. The level appears as a textual percentage or as separate battery-level and charging icons, and it can be paired with a status bar organizer to control when or where the indicator is displayed.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "battery-indicator"
    name:   "Battery Indicator"
    author: "opisek"
    repo:   "opisek/obsidian-battery-indicator"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/battery-indicator"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/opisek/obsidian-battery-indicator"
    description: "Displays current battery level in the status bar."
    about:       "Display a battery indicator in Obsidian's status bar. Show a textual percentage or separate battery level and charging icons, and pair with a status bar organizer to control when or where the indicator appears."
```
