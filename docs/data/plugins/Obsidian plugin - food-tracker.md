# Food Tracker

Tracks food intake, calories and macronutrients with running totals and editor highlighting of calorie and nutrient values. A searchable nutrient database or inline nutrition entry can be used, OpenFoodFacts data imported by name or barcode, and workouts or negative calories logged to adjust the totals.
