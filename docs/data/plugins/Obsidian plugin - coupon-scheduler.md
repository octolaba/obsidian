# Coupon Scheduler

Coupon Scheduler schedules coupons, groceries, shopping reminders, red packets and recurring activities on a calendar that works offline. Cards are organized into outings, groceries or trash and track validity, hours, locations, price and notes. The data can be exported to and imported from JSON backups.
