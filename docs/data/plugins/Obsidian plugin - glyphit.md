# GlyphIt

Adds icons to files, folders, tabs, titles and inline text, taking them from zipped icon packs that stay compressed so large packs load fast. Icons are extracted to a cache the first time they are used, and short unique names are generated for nested or duplicate icons. Icons are recolored at render time, and packs are rescanned without a restart.
