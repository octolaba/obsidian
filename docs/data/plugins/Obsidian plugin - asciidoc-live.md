# AsciiDoc Live

Renders AsciiDoc files with the adoc, asciidoc and asc extensions, with a preview that re-renders as the text is typed. Pasted images are saved into the vault and inserted with image syntax, math in latexmath, asciimath and stem blocks renders through MathJax, and cross-reference links open and scroll to their targets. The preview also refreshes on external edits and in split-pane Source mode.

```cue

    id:     "asciidoc-live"
    name:   "AsciiDoc Live"
    author: "Nick Koshlensky"
    repo:   "koshlensky/asciidoc-live"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/asciidoc-live"

    about:       "Render .adoc, .asciidoc and .asc files with full AsciiDoc support and a live preview that automatically re-renders as you type. Save pasted images to the vault and insert image:: syntax, render math ([latexmath]/[asciimath]/[stem]) via MathJax, resolve xref/<<>> links to open and scroll to targets, and auto-refresh on external edits or in split-pane Source mode."
```
