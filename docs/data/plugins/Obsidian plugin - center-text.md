# Center Text

Center Text centers the text of notes and sets a maximum column width through a slider covering twenty to one hundred percent. Centering can be toggled on or off, applies in both reading view and live preview, and the settings persist across restarts.

    id:     "center-text"
    name:   "Center Text"
    author: "camblackwell"
    repo:   "camblackwell/Obsidian_Center_Text_Plugin"

    github_url:  "https://github.com/camblackwell/Obsidian_Center_Text_Plugin"
