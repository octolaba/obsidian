# Xiaohongshu Importer Plus

Imports Xiaohongshu notes or share URLs into the vault, with configurable default folders for notes and images and editable frontmatter fields. Images are downloaded locally while videos stay as remote links, and frontmatter is filled from placeholders for date, title, source and video URL.
