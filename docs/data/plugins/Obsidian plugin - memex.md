# Memex

Searches and queries a Memex account from inside Obsidian through a dedicated sidebar and inline search blocks. Matching Memex notes are opened directly, and supported results are dragged into the vault as embedded result-card blocks.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "memex"
    name:   "Memex"
    author: "worldbrain"
    repo:   "worldbrain/obsidian-memex"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/memex"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/worldbrain/obsidian-memex"
    description: "Search, cite & chat with your bookmarking second brain. - This plugin has not been manually reviewed by Obsidian staff."
    about:       "Search and ask questions against your Memex account from inside Obsidian using a dedicated sidebar and inline search blocks. Open matching Memex notes directly and drag supported results into your vault as embedded result-card blocks."
```
