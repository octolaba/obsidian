# Napkin Notes

Turns handwritten notes, sketches and whiteboard photos into inline image carousels styled like a PDF viewer. Images are uploaded from the filesystem, from a camera, or from a phone via QR code, and can carry annotations that make the notes searchable. The viewer is responsive, follows light and dark themes and offers an optional napkin texture.
