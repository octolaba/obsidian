# Bible Search and Reader

Searches Bible Markdown files held locally in the vault by theme, outline or verse, using a reference and keyword syntax, and returns paged results with keywords highlighted and individual matches selectable and orderable. A compact reader displays whole books and chapters and allows selections to be copied. Content can be projected in focus, parallel or mixed grouped modes, verse by verse or side by side.

Read books and chapters in a compact reader, copy selections, and project content in per-verse, parallel, or grouped mixed modes."
