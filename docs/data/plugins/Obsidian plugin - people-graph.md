# People Graph

People Graph renders the people and friends recorded in a vault as an interactive force-directed graph with photo nodes and relationship edges. People are positioned by closeness around a central You node and clustered by company. Hovering shows name, company, role and closeness, clicking opens the corresponding note, and the view supports panning, zooming and live updates.
