# Consistent Attachments

Consistent Attachments keeps attachments with their notes when files are moved or copied, relocating or duplicating the linked files automatically. A shared attachment is skipped, copied, or handled after a prompt; orphaned files are found by a scan and cleaned up; numeric suffixes prevent overwrites; and recent operations are listed for review alongside manual actions.
