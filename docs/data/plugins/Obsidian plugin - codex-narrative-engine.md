# Codex Narrative Engine

Codex Narrative Engine indexes campaign notes by their frontmatter into a live entity index that drives wiki-link autocomplete and hover summaries. Dead links and logical state conflicts are detected and reported as warnings, and entities can be created or renamed with their references updated automatically. With your own AI key it also runs Lore Chat, expands stubs, and generates NPCs, locations, items and other lore grounded in the vault.
