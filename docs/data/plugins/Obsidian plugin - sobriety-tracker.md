# Sobriety Tracker

Sobriety is tracked through an urge timer, one-tap daily check-ins and automatic logging of wins and relapses. Streaks, success rate, weekly and monthly breakdowns and an urge log show the recorded patterns. Check-ins missed while Obsidian was closed are caught up afterwards, and the interface switches between English and Chinese.
