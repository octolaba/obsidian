# Paperish Presentation

Presents the current note as minimal, typography-driven slides that mirror reading view, keeping embeds, LaTeX, callouts and output from community plugins intact. Decks are split at headings or at horizontal rules, and content can be revealed step by step. A contextual bar shows the ancestor headings of the current slide, and refreshing re-renders the snapshot with footnotes collected at the end.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "paperish-presentation"
    name:   "Paperish Presentation"
    author: "Brian J. Cardiff"
    repo:   "bcardiff/obsidian-paperish-presentation"

    github_url:  "https://github.com/bcardiff/obsidian-paperish-presentation"
    description: "Present the current note as minimal, typography-driven slides split at headings, preserving all rendered content. - This plugin has not been manually reviewed by Obsidian staff."
    about:       "Present any Obsidian note as minimal, typography-driven slides that mirror reading view, including embeds, LaTeX, callouts and community-plugin output. Split decks by headings or --- rules, reveal content step-by-step, and display ancestor headings in a contextual bar; refresh to re-render the snapshot with footnotes at the end."
```
