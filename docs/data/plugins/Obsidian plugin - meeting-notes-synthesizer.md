# Meeting Notes Synthesizer

Synthesizes existing meeting notes across a folder or a tag into a cross-meeting record. Decisions are extracted with their dates and source links, open actions are grouped by owner with due dates, and weekly rollups are produced. Decision history is traced, so the current version of a decision is distinguishable from earlier ones.

    id:     "meeting-notes-synthesizer"
