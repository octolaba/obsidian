# Note Definitions

Note Definitions keeps a personal dictionary inside the vault and underlines the words and phrases it defines wherever they appear in notes. Definitions are previewed on hover or through a command and their files can be opened directly, and entries are added or edited as ordinary vault content rather than as hidden metadata.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "note-definitions"
    name:   "Note Definitions"
    author: "dominiclet"
    repo:   "dominiclet/obsidian-note-definitions"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/note-definitions"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/dominiclet/obsidian-note-definitions"
    description: "Personal dictionary for your notes"
    about:       "Create a personal, in-vault dictionary and underline defined words and phrases in your notes. Preview definitions by hovering or via command, jump to their files, and add or edit entries directly in your vault without hidden metadata."

}
```
