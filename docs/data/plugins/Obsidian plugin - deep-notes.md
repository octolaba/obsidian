# Deep Notes

Generates probing Socratic questions and suggestions from notes and links concepts across the vault. Follow-up questions are generated recursively, answers are scored against ideal responses, and reviews are scheduled with spaced repetition. A local semantic index supplies cross-note context, and text is read out of images and Excalidraw drawings by OCR.
