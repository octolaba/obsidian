# Media Slider

Groups images, videos, audio, PDFs and Markdown files into a swipeable slider so they take less vertical space in a note. Before-and-after images are compared, slides are zoomed and panned, annotations are drawn and per-slide notes are saved. Navigation covers thumbnails, transitions, fullscreen, autoplay and audio or video visualizers.
