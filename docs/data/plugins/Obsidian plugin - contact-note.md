# Contact Note

Contact Note turns the frontmatter of notes designated as contacts into visual contact cards. The cards can be browsed in a built-in sidebar panel that supports searching and filtering, or surfaced through a Bases view that adds sorting, grouping and wider filtering.

```cue

    id:     "contact-note"
    name:   "Contact Note"
    author: "Jalad"
    repo:   "jalad25/contact-note"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/contact-note"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/jalad25/contact-note"
    description: "Turn frontmatter in notes designated as contacts into visual contact cards. Browse them with a built-in sidebar panel view or surface them through a Bases view. - This plugin has not been manually reviewed by Obsidian staff."
```
