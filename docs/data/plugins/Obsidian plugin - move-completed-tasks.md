# Move Completed Tasks

Moves checked tasks and their subtasks to the bottom of their checkbox group and adds a subtle highlight so the new position is visible. Commands either sink completed items within their group or collect every completed task under a Completed heading at the end of the note. A single undo reverses the move.

plugin: {
    id:     "move-completed-tasks"
    name:   "Move Completed Tasks"
    author: "Aaron Penne"
    repo:   "aaronpenne/move-completed-tasks"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/move-completed-tasks"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/aaronpenne/move-completed-tasks"
    description: "Automatically move completed tasks to the bottom of their checkbox group - This plugin has not been manually reviewed by Obsidian staff."
    about:       "Move checked tasks (and their subtasks) to the bottom of their task group and add a subtle highlight so you can spot the new position. Run commands to sink completed items within groups or collect all completed tasks under a ## Completed heading at the end of the note, and undo the move with a single Ctrl+Z."
