# Lumen

Opens a floating AI assistant panel in the right sidebar of Obsidian that reads the current note or selection and keeps multi-turn conversation history. It runs summarization, translation, in-depth analysis, to-do extraction, continuation and polishing through Claude, GPT, MiniMax or Kimi, and writes the result back by replacing the selection, appending to the end or overwriting the whole note. Both recorded inputs are written in Chinese; this body restates them in English.

    about:       "打开右侧悬浮 AI 助手面板，自动读取当前笔记或选区并保留多轮对话历史。执行总结、翻译、深度剖析、提取待办、续写与润色，并将结果一键替换选区、追加到末尾或覆写整篇笔记。"
