# FFmpeg Converter

FFmpeg Converter converts images, video and audio into another format or a reduced quality with FFmpeg in order to shrink the space a vault occupies. Common image, video and audio formats are supported, and the plugin states that vault size can be cut by up to 70 percent. FFmpeg and FFprobe are required, and temporary files are rolled back on error so the originals are restored.
