# NotePack CODEX

NotePack CODEX is a board-first thinking workbench where memos are tossed onto a dedicated codex board and AI personas annotate, categorize, debate and propose synthesis across them. Multi-seed idea card packs are drawn to force new directions, cards are pruned by keeping or discarding, and chosen cards are promoted into vault Markdown notes.
