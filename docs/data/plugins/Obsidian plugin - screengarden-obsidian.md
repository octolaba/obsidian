# screen.garden

screen.garden adds realtime multiplayer collaboration to Obsidian, with live cursors and presence indicators and without merge conflicts. Folders or whole vaults can be shared with fine-grained public and private permissions, files of any type including images and video are synced, and notes can be read or edited from the web.
