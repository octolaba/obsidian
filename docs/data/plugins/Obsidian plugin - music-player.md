# Music Player

Plays local MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A, FLAC and AAC files from the vault in a sidebar with library browsing, playlists, favorites, a queue and controls for seek, speed and volume. Embedded lyrics are displayed, including LRC and word-by-word karaoke, synced to playback beside album art. Playback modes include sequential, loop, repeat-one and shuffle, and the recorded text states that lyrics are read only from embedded metadata.
