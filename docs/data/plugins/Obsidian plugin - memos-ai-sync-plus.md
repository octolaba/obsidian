# Memos AI Sync Plus

Syncs notes from Memos v0.21 and earlier into the vault through the classic REST API, preserving content and metadata. Attachments are downloaded and embedded in the vault. Optional AI summaries, tags and weekly digests are produced, and syncs run manually or on a schedule.
