# Study Familiar

Tracks study progress on a dashboard showing level, an XP bar, a streak, today's quest and a prioritized list of what to study now, awarding XP for study done. Concepts are rated on a five-button confidence scale that records both the confidence and the review time. Timed sprints end with recall prompts, and suggested links and Zotero-friendly source links are offered.
