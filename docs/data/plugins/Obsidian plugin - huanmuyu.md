# Huanmuyu

Huanmuyu is a multilingual study system with a study calendar, daily new words presented with ten examples, IPA and native audio, spaced-repetition review, AI sentence correction and a local vocabulary store. Progress is kept locally in the vault, English, Japanese, Korean, French, Chinese, Spanish and Arabic are covered, and the only network contact stated is a license check against the vendor endpoint.
