# Workout Logger

Writes strength and cardio sessions into notes as Dataview-compatible inline metadata, recording per-set weight, reps and RPE with calculated volume and estimated 1RM, plus cardio speed, incline, duration and calories. A sidebar dashboard charts body metrics, calories and per-exercise performance, exercise notes are autocompleted and created on demand, and the interface covers English, Japanese, Chinese and Korean.
