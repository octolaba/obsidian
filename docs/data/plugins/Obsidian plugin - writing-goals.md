# Writing Goals

Sets overall and daily word goals for individual notes and for folders, updating progress in real time as text is written. Goals are stored in frontmatter and folder totals roll up recursively. Progress bars and a unified stats view collect the goals in one place for editing and for jumping to the notes behind them.
