# Bible sidecar

Bible sidecar opens a split-screen panel that shows Scripture in a preferred translation beside the notes in the workspace. Bible versions are selectable and chapters navigable, verses can be copied to the clipboard, and clicking a verse inserts it into the note.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "bible-sidecar"
    name:   "Bible sidecar"
    author: "janisringli"
    repo:   "janisringli/bible-sidecar-obsidian-plugin"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/bible-sidecar"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/janisringli/bible-sidecar-obsidian-plugin"
    description: "Open the bible in your preferred translation in a split-screen view within your workspace"
    about:       "Open the Bible sidecar panel to read scripture alongside your notes and copy verses to the clipboard. Select Bible versions, navigate chapters, and click verses to insert them directly into your notes."

    stats: {
        downloads:  1862

}
```
