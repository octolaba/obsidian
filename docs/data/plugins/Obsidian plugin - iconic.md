# Iconic

Iconic replaces most Obsidian icons with Lucide icons or device emojis across tabs, files, folders, ribbons, sidebars and the title bar, directly from the interface. Assignment is automated with rules matching name, extension, parent folder, tags, properties, dates or time of day, icons are searched by name, and colors are theme-aware or custom RGB.

    id:     "iconic"

    github_url:  "https://github.com/gfxholo/iconic"

    about:       "Replace almost any Obsidian icon with 1,700+ Lucide icons or 1,900+ device emojis across tabs, files, folders, ribbons, sidebars, and the title bar. Automate icon assignment with rule-based conditions (name, extension, parent folder, tags, properties, dates, time of day), search icons by name, and pick theme-aware or custom RGB colors."
