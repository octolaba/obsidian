# Coin Memo

Coin Memo parses accounting records out of journal files, extracting entries automatically and summarizing income, expenses, categories and date-range statistics. Screenshot bills are imported through OCR for one-click parsing, recent entries can be copied into today's note, entries reclassified in bulk by keyword, and reports exported to PDF or Markdown.
