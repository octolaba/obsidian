# Bible Study

Bible Study inserts and expands Chinese Union Version passages in Obsidian notes, turning shorthand references into clickable links that show a preview card on hover. A reading panel on the right selects book and chapter, searches by Chinese name, abbreviation or pinyin, and inserts or copies a passage into the current note. The recorded description and About text for this plugin are written in Chinese; this summary restates them in English.
