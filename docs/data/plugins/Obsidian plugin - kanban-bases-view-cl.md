# Kanban Bases View CL

A customized kanban view for Bases that organizes notes into columns based on any property and rearranges cards by drag and drop. Cards can show multi-image galleries and cover images, columns can be collapsed individually, and swimlanes are available. Column order, colors and widths are configurable and persisted, and quick-added cards arrive with their column value prefilled.
