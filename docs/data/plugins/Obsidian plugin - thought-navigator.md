# Thought Navigator

Thought Navigator builds visual, navigable thought trees from .moc files to map Zettelkasten notes, maps of content and longer-term knowledge. Nodes are edited on a canvas by dragging, connecting, grouping and coloring, images are pasted as nodes, and local graph context is explored beside them. A scratchpad moves nodes between maps of content, and graphs open through Obsidian URIs.
