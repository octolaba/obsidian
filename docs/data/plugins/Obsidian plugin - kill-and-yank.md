# Kill and Yank

Enables Emacs-style kill and yank editing in the editor. Text is cut from the cursor position or from a selection, and recently killed text can be pasted back from the kill ring.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "kill-and-yank"
    name:   "Kill and Yank"
    author: "inouetakuya"
    repo:   "inouetakuya/obsidian-kill-and-yank"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/kill-and-yank"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/inouetakuya/obsidian-kill-and-yank"
    description: "Enable kill and yank (like Emacs) in the editor."
    about:       "Enable Emacs-style kill and yank editing in the Obsidian editor. Cut from the cursor or selection and paste from the kill ring to recall recently killed text."
```
