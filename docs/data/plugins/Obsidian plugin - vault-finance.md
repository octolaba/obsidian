# Vault Finance

Tracks cash, bank and credit-card accounts and records expenses, income, refunds, transfers and card payments. Weekly and monthly summaries are currency-aware, credit limits and available credit are tracked, and multi-currency and cross-currency transfers are handled. Amounts are stored in the smallest currency unit to avoid rounding errors, and all data stays local with no network calls.
