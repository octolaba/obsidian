# URI Converter

Converts Obsidian URIs and Advanced URIs into internal links, covering note, heading and block targets in both Markdown-link and bare-URI form. Bidirectional links are preserved, which is what makes pasted or imported links from an external application such as Todoist navigable inside the vault.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "uri-converter"
    name:   "URI Converter"
    author: "wenlzhang"
    repo:   "wenlzhang/obsidian-uri-converter"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/uri-converter"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/wenlzhang/obsidian-uri-converter"
    description: "Convert URIs to internal links."
    about:       "Convert Obsidian URIs and Advanced URIs into internal Obsidian links, handling note, heading, and block links as well as Markdown-format and pure URI forms. Preserve bidirectional links and automate conversion of pasted or imported links from external apps like Todoist to keep notes navigable."
```
