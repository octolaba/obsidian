# Epochgram

Builds an interactive timeline of the vault by indexing dates from filenames, content and metadata, so no manual date frontmatter is required. The timeline zooms from single days to whole years, records are created, renamed, moved and deleted on it, and fuzzy search, filtering, note change history, calendar sync and recurring records are available. Summaries come from Markdown structure or from an AI provider — on device through Gemini Nano, or through OpenAI and Gemini in the cloud — and similar notes are matched by links, tags, titles, semantics and topics.
