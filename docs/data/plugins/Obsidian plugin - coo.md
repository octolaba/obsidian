# coo

coo places AI work inline in the paragraph being read or written rather than in a separate chat window. Discuss lets a passage be selected and asked about, saving the answer as a collapsed callout beneath the paragraph with the question as its title, and follow-up questions chain so each builds on the last, while Rewrite folds those answers back into the paragraph in one undoable step. Translate drops an inline translation after a selected word or phrase and keeps the original.
