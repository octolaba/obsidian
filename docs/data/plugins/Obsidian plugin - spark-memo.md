# Spark Memo

Quick captures of text, voice, photos and audio are made from a sidebar and appended to today's daily note. A pasted photo's EXIF time and location become a timestamped, geo-linked memo, timestamps in the journal section are highlighted, and tags and locations are rolled up. A yearly heatmap reviews the year, and a random past memo can be surfaced.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "spark-memo"
    name:   "Spark Memo"
    author: "Joe Hou"
    repo:   "houjoe0829/sparkmemo"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/spark-memo"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/houjoe0829/sparkmemo"
    description: "Highlight timestamps in your Journal section, capture quick notes, images, and audio from a sidebar, dictate with real-time speech-to-text, and review your year with a heatmap. - This plugin has not been manually reviewed by Obsidian staff."
    about:       "Capture thoughts, voice, photos, or audio in a quick-capture sidebar and append them to today's daily note. Convert pasted photos' EXIF time/GPS into memos timestamped and geo-linked; highlight HH:MM timestamps, roll up tags and locations, and view a yearly heatmap or a random past memo."

    stats: {
        downloads:  48
        updated_at: 1783785630000
    }
}
```
