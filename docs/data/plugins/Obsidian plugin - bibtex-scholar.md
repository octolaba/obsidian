# BibTeX Scholar

BibTeX Scholar manages BibTeX citations inside notes for literature reviews and paper writing. BibTeX blocks are inserted into notes and cited inline with autocomplete on the entry identifier, and a citation popover shows title, authors, abstract and quick actions. Relationships between works can be mapped with nested bullets, tables or flowcharts to capture comparisons and insights.
