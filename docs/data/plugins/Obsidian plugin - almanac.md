# Almanac

Opens a month or week calendar plus a compact sidebar for navigating daily notes, meetings and events in the vault. Events, meeting notes and daily pages are created and edited from templates, with quick commands, drag-and-drop scheduling and a time-grid week view.

    author: "myeongsoyi"
