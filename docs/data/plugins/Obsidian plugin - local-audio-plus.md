# Local Audio Plus

Scans local audio links in vault notes and inserts transcripts into the notes themselves. Transcription runs through OpenAI, Tencent Cloud ASR or iFlytek ASR with selectable models and optional speaker diarization, and the selected audio is sent to the configured provider.
