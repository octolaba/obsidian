# Time Recorder

Time Recorder records time in two clicks through a punch-in modal that closes the previous segment automatically and accepts custom activities grouped by category. Segments are back-filled and edited on a 24-hour timeline, live daily, weekly and monthly summaries are copied as Markdown, and all data stays in plain Markdown files on Desktop and Android.
