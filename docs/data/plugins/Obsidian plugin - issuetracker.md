# IssueTracker

IssueTracker syncs issues from selected GitCode repositories or an entire organization into the vault, persisting each one as a normalized note under a GitCode Issues folder. Issues are classified as bug, requirement or unknown, and their authors marked internal or external from collaborator data plus a whitelist. It also generates daily machine-friendly reports and briefs meant for an AI reader, and stores sync metadata and collaborator caches beside the issues.
