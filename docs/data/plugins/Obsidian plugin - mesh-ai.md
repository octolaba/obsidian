# Mesh AI

Stores AI prompts as reusable patterns and applies them to the active note, the clipboard or Tavily search results, in sequential or stitch mode. YouTube links are detected and their transcripts fetched as input. Patterns are collected into workflows and command-palette actions, pattern libraries are managed and downloaded, and the recorded text names OpenAI, Grocq, Ollama, Google, Microsoft and Anthropic as providers.
