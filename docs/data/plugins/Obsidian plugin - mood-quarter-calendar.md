# Mood Quarter Calendar

Mood Quarter Calendar lays out a year of moods quarter by quarter, drawing one consistent SVG face per day from the daily notes. Moods are read from frontmatter, inline fields or tags. Clicking a date opens a sticky note for recording or editing that day's mood, and all data stays local in Markdown.

```cue

    id:     "mood-quarter-calendar"
    name:   "Mood Quarter Calendar"
    author: "LinneaCai"
    repo:   "linglingcai0314/obsidian-mood-quarter-calendar"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/mood-quarter-calendar"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/linglingcai0314/obsidian-mood-quarter-calendar"
    description: "A Markdown-first mood calendar with custom SVG faces, tag/frontmatter input, and readable quarter-by-quarter year layout. - This plugin has not been manually reviewed by Obsidian staff."
    about:       "Display a year-long mood calendar arranged quarter-by-quarter with consistent SVG faces pulled from daily notes. Read moods from frontmatter, inline fields, or tags and show one face per day. Record or edit moods by clicking a date to open a sticky note, keeping all data local in Markdown."
```
