# Pantry

Turns recipe notes into a consolidated grocery list grouped by category, merging ingredient quantities and keeping checkboxes, collapsible sections and shopping progress. Recipes render as interactive cooking cards with scaled ingredients, portion multipliers, instructions, diet and allergen badges, one-off items and favorites.
