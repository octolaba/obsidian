# csv quiz practice

A plugin that turns a CSV question bank into a practice quiz inside Obsidian, giving immediate right-or-wrong feedback after each answer is chosen and showing the correct answer, with optional random question and option order. The bank can be filtered by tag, by a three-level category, and by favourite, mastered or wrong-answer marks. Edits to questions and marks are saved back to the CSV, and answering progress and accuracy are persisted.
