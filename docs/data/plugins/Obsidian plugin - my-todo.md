# My Todo

Organizes tasks into categories with custom tags and plans a week by assigning items to daily lists. Unfinished tasks roll over automatically and completed ones are cleared at a chosen time. Progress appears as a productivity heatmap, and the recorded text notes native light and dark theme support.

    id:     "my-todo"

    author: "amanxarora"
