# Jura Links

Turns legal citations, statutes, case numbers and journal references in notes into links to legal providers, including Dejure, Rewis.io, Buzer, LexMea and Landesrecht.online. Known statutes and abbreviations can be searched and filtered in a built-in window, and common legal citation formats are detected in the text.

    id:     "jura-links"
    name:   "Jura Links"
    author: "justanotherjurastudent"
    repo:   "justanotherjurastudent/Jura-Links"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/jura-links"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/justanotherjurastudent/Jura-Links"

Search and filter known statutes and abbreviations in a built-in window and let the plugin detect common legal citation formats in your text."
