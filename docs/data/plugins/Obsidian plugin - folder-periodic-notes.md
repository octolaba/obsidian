# Folder Periodic Notes

Organizes periodic notes into a year, month and day folder hierarchy, with yearly notes at the top, month folders inside years and daily or weekly notes inside months. Year and month notes are created automatically when a daily note is opened or created. Weekly, monthly and yearly notes are opened or created by entering the week number, the month or the year.
