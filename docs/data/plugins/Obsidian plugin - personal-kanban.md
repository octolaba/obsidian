# Personal Kanban

Personal Kanban manages personal projects on multiple local kanban boards inside Obsidian through a sidebar interface. Cards, columns and boards are reordered by drag and drop, and items can be moved across boards. Opening a card gives a modal for editing its title, tags and a full Markdown description.
