# Shukuchi

Shukuchi teleports to the nearest URL or internal link and jumps to its destination even when the cursor is not sitting on a link. Links on the same line take priority, otherwise the closest link is used, and commands open the target in the current pane, in a new tab or group, or in a new window. It supports desktop and mobile.

    id:     "shukuchi"
    name:   "Shukuchi"

    github_url:  "https://github.com/tadashi-aikawa/shukuchi"
    description: "Teleport to links (URL or internal link) and jump to their destinations."
    about:       "Teleport to the nearest URL or internal link and jump to its destination even when no link sits under the cursor. Prioritize links on the same line, otherwise move to the closest link, and provide commands to open links in-pane, new tabs/groups, or a new window; supports desktop and mobile."
