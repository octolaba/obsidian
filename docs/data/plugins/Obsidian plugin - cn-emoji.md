# Chinese Emoji Picker

Searches and inserts emoji by Chinese keyword: typing a full-width double colon followed by a word opens a floating picker under the cursor. More than 1,900 emoji can be browsed by category in a compact seven-column grid and previewed with system fonts. Choosing one replaces the trigger text with a standard Unicode character, so notes stay portable and free of dependencies.
