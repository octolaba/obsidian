# Focus Timer

Runs a local timer with countdown and stopwatch modes, adjustable durations and quick-start commands. Sessions are marked completed or abandoned and kept in a history shown as cards, with charts summarising daily, weekly, monthly and yearly focus. Live statistics and charts are embedded into notes.
