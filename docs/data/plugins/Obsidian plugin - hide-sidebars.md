# Hide Sidebars

Hide Sidebars auto-hides the left and right sidebars independently to reclaim writing space while the explorer, search, backlinks, outline and other panes stay reachable. A sidebar reappears when the pointer moves to the screen edge, an overlay mode floats it over the editor, and widths and the collapse delay are adjustable. Dedicated toggle icons control each side.
