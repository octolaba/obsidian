# Daily News Briefing

Generates AI news summaries in the vault from RSS feeds and several AI providers, named as OpenAI, Anthropic, Perplexity and Grok. Delivery can be scheduled, custom topics tracked in six languages, templates and a sidebar button used, and the resulting notes archived into monthly subfolders.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "daily-news-briefing"
    name:   "Daily News Briefing"
    author: "Adam Chen"
    repo:   "chenziqiadam/Daily-News-Briefing"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/daily-news-briefing"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/chenziqiadam/Daily-News-Briefing"
    description: "Get AI-powered daily news summaries in your vault. Features customizable topics, smart filtering, and automated scheduling."
```
