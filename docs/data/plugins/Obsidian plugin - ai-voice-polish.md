# AI Voice Polish

Records voice through a floating, movable recorder and transcribes mixed Chinese and English speech using several speech-to-text providers. Transcriptions are polished by AI in formal, concise, casual or correction-only styles, then inserted at the cursor or written into a dated Markdown note with the audio embedded and playable.
