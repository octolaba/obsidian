# Strava Sync

Activities are brought from Strava into Obsidian for referencing, tracking and visualization, either by importing a Strava CSV export or by syncing recent activities through the Strava API. Notes are produced from Handlebars templates with customizable front matter aimed at Dataview, and per-sport icons are attached for Iconize.
