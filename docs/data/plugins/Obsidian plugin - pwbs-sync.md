# PWBS Smart Briefings

Syncs the vault to the PWBS engine, which returns context-aware briefings in the sidebar: morning summaries, meeting preparation drawn from calendar entries and notes, and project briefings on demand. Briefings carry references back to their sources, and connections to Google Calendar, Notion and Zoom supply further context.
