# Invoice Forge

Invoice Forge turns billable hours logged in notes into client invoices, reading lines tagged as billable together with a client token. It generates numbered Markdown invoices with line items and totals and previews hours that have not been billed yet. A Pro tier is described as adding PDF and print export with branding, tax and multi-currency handling, recurring billing reminders and payment tracking.
