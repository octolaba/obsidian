# 30-Day Energy Log

Records energy levels and activities across six daily time slots over thirty days, rating each slot on the colour-coded levels Low, Steady, Active and Peak. Activities logged in a slot are marked as energizing, neutral or draining. The data is stored locally in the vault, an export writes a text file carrying a built-in AI prompt, and the interface is available in Chinese and English.
