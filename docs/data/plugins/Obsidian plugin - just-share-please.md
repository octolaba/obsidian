# Just Share Please

Shares individual notes online through anonymized links, so the rest of the vault stays private while a single note is readable at an anonymous URL. Access granted this way is read-only, and the service can also be self-hosted.

```cue
plugin: {
    id:     "just-share-please"
    name:   "Just Share Please"
    author: "Ell"
    repo:   "ellpeck/ObsidianJustSharePlease"

    html_url:    "https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/just-share-please"
    github_url:  "https://github.com/ellpeck/ObsidianJustSharePlease"
    description: "Quickly and easily share individual notes online using an anonymized link. Also easy to self-host!"
    about:       "Share individual notes online via anonymized links. Keep your vault private while granting read-only access through anonymous URLs."
```
