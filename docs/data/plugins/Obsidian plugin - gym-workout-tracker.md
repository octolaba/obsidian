# Gym Workout Tracker

Tracks gym workouts from a monthly calendar, where clicking a date starts or edits a session and indicator dots mark workout days. Reusable templates and a library of more than 1,300 exercises with GIF demonstrations and thumbnails are available, and numbers from the last session are filled in automatically. All data is kept in a single local file, with kilogram and pound conversion, so the vault is not cluttered.
