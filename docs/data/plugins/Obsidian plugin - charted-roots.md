# Charted Roots

Charted Roots imports GEDCOM, CSV and Gramps XML or .gpkg data and builds linked Markdown family trees inside the vault, with bidirectional relationship editing and source, citation and evidence tracking classified along Mills lines. Trees are visualized as interactive charts, canvas trees, maps and timelines, and exported to Canvas, Excalidraw, PDF, ODT or Markdown. Text reports cover timelines, statistics, lists, tables and Dataview-compatible queries, and books can be compiled with a table of contents, bibliography and name index. Fictional calendars and custom universes are supported for worldbuilding and TTRPG campaigns.
