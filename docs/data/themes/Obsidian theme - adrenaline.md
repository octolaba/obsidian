# Adrenaline

Adrenaline is a minimal Obsidian theme that draws its customization from the accent colour, aiming at a distraction-free and space-efficient workspace in light and dark mode. It hides the ribbon and the nav bar buttons until hover, adds hover and active line indicators, tightens spacing and replaces the folder icons. Blockquotes, callouts and highlighting are restyled as well.

![Adrenaline screenshot](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/spekulucius/obsidian-adrenaline/HEAD/adrenaline_thumbnail.png)
