# AwareAI

AwareAI is a dark-first theme built on deep navy surfaces with a red accent of its own, and a palette it calls Signal covering callouts, tags and syntax. A light variant places the same system over a warm off-white. Its recorded description credits the design to the AwareAI team and their product design system, and states that contrast and typography are tuned for long notes.
