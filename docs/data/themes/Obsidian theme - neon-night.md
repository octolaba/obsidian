# Neon Night

A dark theme built on deep night tones pierced by electric-blue and luminous-yellow highlights. The shadowed surfaces are meant to rest the eyes, while the high-contrast hues keep text sharp.

![Neon Night screenshot](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sto3iv/Neon-Night/HEAD/screenshot.png)

Folk say: \"A small spark lights the darkest woods.\" My eyes feel so rested! Now, I will close my notes and enjoy a warm cup of linden tea with our first batch of sweet raspberry jam."
}
