# Forest Phosphor

Forest Phosphor imitates a CRT phosphor monitor, layering saturated cyan, amber and green glows over a deep forest glass substrate and designed for dark mode. The recorded description claims legibility, strong accent contrast and glossy callout bloom, and states that one coordinated palette is shared across Obsidian, VSCode and iTerm2.
