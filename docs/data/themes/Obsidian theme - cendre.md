# cendre

A dark theme whose hues are computed from wood-fire emissions, applying Planck's law at 1300 K mapped through CIE 1931 into OKLCH to keep the palette internally coherent. Three depth variants, hard, medium and soft, are rendered from a single palette generator.

![cendre screenshot](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aejkatappaja/cendre-obsidian/HEAD/screenshot.jpg)

```cue
theme: {
    name:   "cendre"
    author: "aejkatappaja"
    repo:   "aejkatappaja/cendre-obsidian"
    slug:   "cendre"

    html_url:       "https://community.obsidian.md/themes/cendre"
    github_url:     "https://github.com/aejkatappaja/cendre-obsidian"
    screenshot_url: "https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aejkatappaja/cendre-obsidian/HEAD/screenshot.jpg"

    modes: ["dark"]
    about: "Apply a dark Obsidian theme with hues computed from wood-fire emissions (Planck's law at 1300 K mapped through CIE 1931 into OKLCH) for a coherent, natural palette. Provide three depth variants—hard (deepest), medium, and soft—rendered from a single palette generator."
}
```
