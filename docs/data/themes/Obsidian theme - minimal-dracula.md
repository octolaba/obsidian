# Minimal Dracula

Minimal Dracula gives Obsidian a clean editing look based on the Dracula palette. Its muted gray and blue tones are described as optimized for raw editing, and the theme covers desktop, tablet and mobile.

![Minimal Dracula screenshot](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/druxorey/minimal-dracula-for-obsidian/HEAD/resources/cover.png)

}
