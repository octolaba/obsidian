# ITS Theme

ITS Theme is a story-focused theme with dark and light modes, a colored graph, squared edges and underlined headings. Images are centered and zoomed, callouts and blockquotes are styled, and the recorded description adds rectangular tag pills, minimal metadata, revised checkboxes and cleaner embeds.

![ITS Theme screenshot](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/slrvb/Obsidian--ITS-Theme/HEAD/ITS.png)
