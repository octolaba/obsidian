# Compline

Compline is a muted, contemplative palette for focused, distraction-free writing, with warm, low-contrast colors designed for long sessions. It ships two variants that share one color system: Compline in dark and Lauds in light.

![Compline screenshot](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/baileylu121/obsidian-compline/HEAD/screenshot.png)
