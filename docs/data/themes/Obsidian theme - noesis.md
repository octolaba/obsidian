# noesis

A theme aimed at technical notes, research, task lists, Codeboxes, callouts and long-form writing, with coordinated light and dark presets including a Hack The Box preset. Codebox palettes are selectable, and technical callouts and task-priority markers are provided. Tabs, sidebars, the explorer, graph, canvas, dialogs and print or PDF output share one styling, and everything is offline-ready.
