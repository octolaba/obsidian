# Noctis Family

Noctis Family carries the full Noctis color family into Obsidian as 11 selectable variants, ranging from deep teals and saturated azures to warm roses and light pastels. Variants are switched through the Style Settings plugin; without Style Settings installed the theme defaults to Noctis in dark mode and Noctis Lux in light mode.

![Noctis Family screenshot](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/baylesj/obsidian-noctis-family/HEAD/screenshot.png)

```cue
theme: {
    name:   "Noctis Family"
    author: "baylesj"
    repo:   "baylesj/obsidian-noctis-family"
    slug:   "noctis-family"

    html_url:       "https://community.obsidian.md/themes/noctis-family"
    github_url:     "https://github.com/baylesj/obsidian-noctis-family"
    screenshot_url: "https://raw.githubusercontent.com/baylesj/obsidian-noctis-family/HEAD/screenshot.png"

    modes: ["dark", "light"]
    about: "Apply the full Noctis color family to Obsidian with 11 selectable variants, from deep teals and saturated azures to warm roses and light pastels. Switch variants via the Style Settings plugin; defaults to Noctis (dark) or Noctis Lux (light) if Style Settings not installed."
}
```
