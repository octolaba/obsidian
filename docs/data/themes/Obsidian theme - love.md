# Love

Love styles Obsidian in scarlet and crimson with vibrant highlights that make key text stand out. Lines carrying the flashcard tag receive a soft glow to serve as active-recall prompts, Dataview cards can be enabled through the collage cssclasses, and callouts are presented as distinct groups.
