# Kanagawa Hokusai

Kanagawa Hokusai is a port of the kanagawa.nvim color scheme carrying three full variants: Wave, a warm high-contrast dark; Dragon, a muted low-contrast dark; and Lotus, a warm light. Style Settings switches between the dark variants and configures fonts, and the recorded description states that the original semantic color mappings are preserved.

![Kanagawa Hokusai screenshot](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/k3-2o/kanagawa-hokusai/HEAD/assets/wave.png)
