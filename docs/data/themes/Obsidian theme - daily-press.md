# Daily Press

A monochrome look modelled on thermal receipts and newsprint pages, with high-contrast monospace receipt typography and classic serif headlines. Dashed perforation lines divide the page, images render in 1-bit high contrast and return to color on hover, and corners are square throughout. The styling is pure CSS.

![Daily Press screenshot](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/preval85/obsidian-daily-press/HEAD/screenshots/light.png)

```cue
theme: {
    name:   "Daily Press"
    author: "PrEvAl85"
    repo:   "preval85/obsidian-daily-press"
    slug:   "daily-press"

    html_url:       "https://community.obsidian.md/themes/daily-press"
    github_url:     "https://github.com/preval85/obsidian-daily-press"
    screenshot_url: "https://raw.githubusercontent.com/preval85/obsidian-daily-press/HEAD/screenshots/light.png"

    modes: ["dark", "light"]
    about: "Transform your vault into a stack of monochrome thermal receipts and newsprint pages with high-contrast, monospace receipt typography and classic serif headlines. Apply dashed perforation lines, 1-bit high-contrast images with hover-to-restore color, zero rounded corners, and pure-CSS styling."
}
```
