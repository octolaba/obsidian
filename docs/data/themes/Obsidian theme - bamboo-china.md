# Bamboo China

Gives the editor a restrained paper-and-ink look, with a pure xuan zhi writing canvas and ink-toned frames around sidebars and panels. Sixteen named East-Asian color moods can be selected on either a light or a dark base, and typography is CJK-first with platform-aware details. Contrast is checked against WCAG and reduced-motion preferences are respected.

![Bamboo China screenshot](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/miaoziguan/obsidian-bamboo-china/HEAD/image-1.png)
