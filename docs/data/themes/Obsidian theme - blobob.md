# blobob

blobob is an animated theme offering more than seventy colour options across dark and light palettes, configured through the Style Settings plugin. Its animation covers tabs, buttons and menus, and the tab bar keeps its left and right controls fixed in place.

![blobob screenshot](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kazi-aidah/blobob/HEAD/assets/screenshot.png)
