# NotSwift

NotSwift gives Obsidian a Mac-native Big Sur look, borrowing UI cues from Mail, Finder and Notes: a fat header, mac-like colors, chevrons and Notes-style bullets and toggles. It places sidebar toggle buttons and square left-side tab close buttons, refines scrollbars and stacked tabs, hides the ribbon and improves collapsed-item visibility. The recorded description states that it is designed for the Apple San Francisco Pro and Mono fonts.
