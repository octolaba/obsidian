# cybrcore

Cybrcore offers eleven cybrpapers wallpapers or a solid palette, and assigns three role colors, primary, secondary and tertiary, that drive text, headings, tags, navigation and links. Eight top-level folders can be colorized, image grids and card column counts are adjustable, and built-in focus and hider modes collapse the interface to maximize media.
