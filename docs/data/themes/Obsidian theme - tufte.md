# Tufte

Applies Tufte-style book typography with the embedded ET Book typeface, a marginalia gutter, hairline rules, lining tabular figures, and true sidenotes and margin notes alongside full-bleed banner plates. A full Chinese typographic system covering Song, Kai and Hei faces is included, together with prose tables, a Bases bookshelf gallery and quiet paper-and-ink chrome. Light and dark modes are provided, and companion plugins handle the figures and sidenotes.
