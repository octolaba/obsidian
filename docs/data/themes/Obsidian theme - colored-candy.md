# Colored Candy

Colored Candy colors the interface from a chosen accent and generates two pseudo-accents by offsetting hues and adapting saturation and lightness to produce cohesive backgrounds. The palette is deliberately low-contrast and soft, and Style Settings exposes options including background-to-text contrast.

![Colored Candy screenshot](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/erallie/colored-candy/HEAD/colored-candy-thumbnail.png)
