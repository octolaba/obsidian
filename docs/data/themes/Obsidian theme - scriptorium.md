# Scriptorium

Scriptorium is a warm, minimal reading and writing workspace with soft paper tones, low-contrast surfaces and restrained accents. Navigation is kept quiet, tags and callouts are flattened, and its typography is described as tuned for mixed Chinese and Latin text.
