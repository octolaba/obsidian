# steam

steam is a flat, zero-radius dark theme modelled on the classic olive-green Steam interface, with deep backgrounds, wire greens and sharp yellow highlights. Retro CRT effects are included, among them active text glow, horizontal scanlines and a screen vignette. Advanced Kanban boards are covered by a preconfigured retro patch for columns, tags and cards.
