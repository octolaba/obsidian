# Allium

Allium applies a floral purple and red palette, with contrast between background and text raised to reduce eye strain. Heading, bold and italic colours are restyled for clearer emphasis while the overall look stays calm. A light mode is included but recorded as early, and a matching app icon is offered alongside the theme.

![Allium screenshot](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/xainapse/Allium/HEAD/AlliumScreenshot.png)
