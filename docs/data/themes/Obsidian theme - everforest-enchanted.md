# Everforest Enchanted

Everforest Enchanted supplies Everforest-inspired light and dark palettes with separate colors for internal and external links, header underlines and optional style settings. Images and embeds are styled with alt-text captions and grids, and the recorded description adds custom callouts, code-block language labels, readable and wide line widths, full-width tables, custom backgrounds, stacked panes and parity with reading mode.

![Everforest Enchanted screenshot](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fireisgood/obsidian-everforest-enchanted/HEAD/promo_screenshot.png)
