# Chrono Notes

Chrono Notes provides a calendar workspace with year, month and week views for creating and opening daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly and yearly notes. Optional calendar extensions add Chinese lunar dates, solar terms, traditional festivals and Ganzhi dates, along with regional holidays for Mainland China and Singapore. Tasks, statistics, heatmaps, time-range notes, templates and local read-only ICS calendars held inside the vault are also covered.
