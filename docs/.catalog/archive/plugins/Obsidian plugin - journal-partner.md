# Journal Partner

Journal Partner highlights HH:MM timestamps in a Journal section as customizable capsule badges, so the time of each entry stands out. A Quick Record sidebar captures notes, pasted images and audio recordings with transcription into today's daily note, including real-time dictation. A year heatmap and KPIs report days, entries, recordings and streaks.
